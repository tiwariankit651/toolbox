## LONG DESCRIPTION (~800 chars)

FreeToolHubs is a privacy-first collection of 115+ free online tools built for students, job seekers, creators and developers.

Unlike most online tools, FreeToolHubs processes your files entirely inside your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server. That means your documents, photos and personal data never leave your device.

Highlights:
- PDF tools: merge, split, compress, edit, add text, PDF to image, image to PDF
- AI background remover and background changer (runs locally via WebAssembly)
- Image compressor with target-size mode, converter (JPEG/PNG/WebP/AVIF), resizer, upscaler
- OCR supporting English plus 9 Indian languages, including PDF input
- Career tools: resume builder with live ATS score, cover letter writer, GST invoice generator
- Student tools: typing test with Hindi mode and certificate, CGPA calculator, flashcards, formula sheets
- Developer tools: JSON formatter, regex tester with pattern library, Base64, SQL formatter, crontab builder

No signup. No watermarks. No usage limits. Free forever.
