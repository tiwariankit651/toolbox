❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is ToolBox really free?

Yes, 100% free. No hidden charges, no signup required.

Are my files safe?

Absolutely. All processing happens in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server. We cannot see or access your files.

Do I need to install anything?

No. ToolBox works directly in your web browser on any device — desktop, tablet, or mobile.

Is there a file size limit?

Since everything runs in your browser, the limit depends on your device's memory. Most files up to 100MB work fine.

Can I use ToolBox offline?

Most tools work offline after the first visit. Bookmark this page for quick access.
