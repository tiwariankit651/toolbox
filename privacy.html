Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to FreeToolHubs.com ("we," "us," or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how our website operates. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to your data.

FreeToolHubs.com provides a collection of 98 free online tools including image converters, text utilities, PDF tools, calculators, and more. Our fundamental design principle is privacy-first: the vast majority of our tools operate entirely within your web browser, meaning your files and data never leave your device.

By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please discontinue use of our website.

2. Information We Collect

We want to be completely transparent: we do not collect, store, or have access to any files you process using our tools. We do not require user registration, accounts, or personal information to use any of our tools.

The limited information we may collect includes:

  • Analytics Data: Through Google Analytics, we collect anonymized usage data such as pages visited, time spent on pages, referring URLs, browser type, device type, screen resolution, and approximate geographic location (country/city level). This data is aggregated and cannot be used to personally identify you.
  • Advertising Data: Google AdSense may collect information through cookies to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements. This is governed by Google's own privacy policies.
  • Technical Data: Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may log standard web server information including IP addresses, request timestamps, and HTTP headers for security and performance purposes.

We do not collect: names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment information, uploaded files, processed content, or any other personally identifiable information through normal use of our tools.

3. How Our Tools Work

FreeToolHubs.com hosts 98 browser-based tools. Understanding how they work is essential to understanding our privacy practices:

Client-Side Processing (96 of 98 tools): The overwhelming majority of our tools — including image converters, PDF tools, text formatters, color pickers, calculators, code formatters, hash generators, and file converters — run entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. When you select a file or enter data:

  • Your file is read by your browser's local File API.
  • All processing (conversion, compression, formatting, calculation) happens in your browser's memory using JavaScript, HTML5 Canvas, Web Workers, or WebAssembly.
  • The output is generated locally and made available for download directly from your browser.
  • At no point does your file or data travel over the internet to any server.
  • Once you close or refresh the page, all data is cleared from browser memory.

Tools That Use External APIs (2 of 98 tools): Two tools require external API calls to function:

  • Translator Tool: Uses the MyMemory Translation API (mymemory.translated.net) to translate text between languages. The text you submit for translation is sent to MyMemory's servers for processing. Please review MyMemory's usage policy for their data handling practices.
  • URL Shortener Tool: Uses the is.gd API to create shortened URLs. The URL you submit is sent to is.gd's servers to generate a short link. Please review is.gd's privacy policy for their data handling practices.

We clearly indicate on each tool's page whether it processes data locally or uses an external service.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses the following cookies and local storage mechanisms:

Essential Cookies / Local Storage

  • Theme Preference: We store your dark/light mode preference in your browser's localStorage so it persists between visits. This is not a cookie and is never transmitted to any server.

Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics)

  • _ga: Used to distinguish unique users. Expires after 2 years.
  • _ga_*: Used to persist session state. Expires after 2 years.
  • These cookies help us understand which tools are most popular, how users navigate our site, and where we can improve the user experience.

Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense)

  • Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.
  • You may also opt out of third-party vendor cookies by visiting aboutads.info.

5. Third-Party Services

We integrate with the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics (analytics.google.com): Web analytics service that tracks and reports website traffic. Data is processed by Google in accordance with their Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdSense (adsense.google.com): Advertising service that displays relevant ads on our pages. Governed by Google's Advertising Policies.
  • Cloudflare (cloudflare.com): Provides CDN, DNS, and DDoS protection services for our website. Cloudflare may process visitor IP addresses and request metadata for security purposes. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • is.gd API: Used exclusively by our URL Shortener tool. Only the URL you choose to shorten is transmitted. See is.gd Privacy Policy.
  • MyMemory Translation API: Used exclusively by our Translator tool. Only the text you submit for translation is transmitted. See MyMemory's policies.

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to any other third parties beyond those listed above.

6. Data Retention

Our data retention practices reflect our privacy-first approach:

  • Server-Side Storage: We do not store any user files, processed data, or personal information on our servers. We operate no databases containing user data.
  • Browser Local Storage: Your theme preference (dark/light mode) is stored in your browser's localStorage on your own device. You can clear this at any time through your browser settings.
  • Google Analytics Data: Analytics data is retained by Google according to their standard retention settings (26 months by default). This data is aggregated and anonymized.
  • Cloudflare Logs: Cloudflare retains security and performance logs according to their own retention policies, typically for a limited period.
  • Files You Process: Any files you load into our tools exist only in your browser's temporary memory (RAM) and are automatically cleared when you close the tab, navigate away, or refresh the page. We have no mechanism to access or recover this data.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

FreeToolHubs.com is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. Since our tools do not require registration or the submission of personal information, children can safely use our browser-based tools without providing any personal data.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at ankitkumartiwari690@gmail.com and we will take steps to remove such information. 8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to request information about what data we hold about you. Since we do not collect personal data through our tools, there is typically no personal data to disclose.
  • Right to Deletion: You have the right to request deletion of your personal data. You can clear locally stored preferences (theme settings) by clearing your browser's localStorage. For analytics data, you can opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Right to Opt Out: You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time via Google Ad Settings. You can disable cookies entirely in your browser settings.
  • Right to Rectification: You have the right to correct inaccurate personal data. Since we do not collect personal data, this right is generally not applicable.
  • Right to Data Portability: You have the right to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format. As we do not store personal data, this right is generally not applicable.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You may restrict how we process your data by disabling cookies, using ad blockers, or opting out of analytics tracking.
  • Do Not Track: We respect Do Not Track (DNT) browser signals. However, please note that third-party services like Google Analytics and AdSense have their own DNT policies.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), these rights are provided under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). For users in California, similar rights are provided under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

9. Security Measures

We take the security of our website and your browsing experience seriously:

  • HTTPS Encryption: Our entire website is served over HTTPS (TLS/SSL), ensuring that all communication between your browser and our servers is encrypted.
  • Cloudflare Protection: We use Cloudflare's security services including DDoS protection, Web Application Firewall (WAF), and bot management to protect our website from malicious attacks.
  • No Server-Side Data Storage: By processing all data client-side, we eliminate the risk of server-side data breaches. There is no database to hack, no file storage to compromise, and no user accounts to breach.
  • Regular Updates: We keep our website's code and dependencies updated to address known security vulnerabilities.
  • Minimal Data Collection: Our strongest security measure is collecting as little data as possible. You cannot breach data that doesn't exist.

While no website can guarantee absolute security, our architecture minimizes risk by keeping your data on your own device rather than transmitting it to remote servers.
