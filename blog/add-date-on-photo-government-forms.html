📅 Add Date on Photo for Government Forms

April 2026 · 3 min read

Many government exam forms require the date of photograph to be printed on the image. Our Date on Photo tool makes this easy.

Which Exams Require It?

  • SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS
  • UPSC Civil Services
  • Banking (IBPS, SBI)
  • Railway (RRB)
  • State PSC exams

How to Use

  1. Upload your passport photo
  2. Select the date
  3. Choose position (bottom strip recommended)
  4. Download the dated photo

Add date to your photo!
