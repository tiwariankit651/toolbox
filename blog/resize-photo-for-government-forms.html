📐 How to Resize Photo for Government Forms

April 2026 · 4 min read

Almost every government form requires a photo and signature in specific dimensions. Getting rejected because of wrong image size is frustrating. Here is a complete guide with exact sizes for all major Indian government exams and forms.

Photo Size Requirements

Form / ExamPhoto SizeFile Size
Indian Passport3.5 x 4.5 cm10 KB - 300 KB
SSC (CGL/CHSL)100x120 to 150x200 px20-50 KB (JPG)
UPSC110x140 px20-300 KB
Bank PO / Clerk200x230 px20-50 KB
Railway (RRB)3.5x4.5 cm20-50 KB
US Visa2x2 inches (600x600 px)Max 240 KB

Signature Size Requirements

Form / ExamSignature SizeFile Size
SSC140x60 px10-30 KB
UPSC150x70 px10-40 KB
Bank PO140x60 px10-20 KB
General3.5x1.5 cm10-50 KB

How to Resize — Step by Step

  1. Open FreeToolHubs Image Resizer
  2. Upload your photo
  3. Select the preset for your form (Passport, SSC, UPSC, Bank PO, etc.) — or enter custom dimensions
  4. Choose unit: pixels, cm, mm, or inches
  5. Select output format: JPG (smaller size) or PNG (better quality)
  6. Click "Resize and Download"

Tips for Government Form Photos

  • White background is required for most forms — use our Background Changer
  • JPG format is usually required — select JPG in output format
  • If file size is too large after resizing, use our Image Compressor
  • For digital signature, use our Signature Maker to draw and download
  • Face should cover 70-80% of the photo area
  • No sunglasses, caps, or heavy filters
