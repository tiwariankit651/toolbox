📝 How to Fill SSC Form Online — Step by Step Guide

May 2026 · 5 min read

Filling the SSC CGL or CHSL application form can feel overwhelming, especially if it is your first time. Every year, thousands of candidates face form rejections due to incorrect photo uploads, wrong details, or missed deadlines. This complete guide walks you through the entire SSC form filling process step by step so you can submit your application without any errors.

Before You Start — Documents You Need

  • Aadhaar card or PAN card — for identity verification
  • 10th and 12th marksheets — for educational details
  • Passport-size photo — JPG format, 20–50 KB, 3.5cm × 4.5cm
  • Signature scan — JPG format, 10–20 KB, on white paper
  • Valid email and mobile number — OTP verification required
  • Category certificate — if applicable (SC/ST/OBC/EWS)

Step 1: One-Time Registration

Visit the official SSC website (ssc.gov.in) and click "Register Now." Fill in your basic details — name (exactly as on your 10th certificate), father's name, date of birth, email, and mobile number. You will receive an OTP on both email and phone. After verification, you get a Registration ID and password. Save these carefully — you will need them for all future SSC exams.

Step 2: Login and Select Exam

Log in with your Registration ID and password. From the dashboard, select the exam you want to apply for (CGL, CHSL, MTS, etc.). Click "Apply" to begin the application form.

Step 3: Fill Personal Details

Enter your personal information carefully. Double-check your name spelling, date of birth, and category. Common mistakes here include misspelling names, selecting the wrong gender, or choosing an incorrect category. These errors can lead to form rejection and there is often no correction window.

Step 4: Educational Qualification

Select your highest qualification and enter marks/percentage. For CGL, you need a graduation degree. For CHSL, 12th pass is sufficient. Enter your board/university name and passing year accurately.

Step 5: Photo and Signature Upload

This is where most candidates face problems. SSC has strict requirements:

  • Photo: JPG/JPEG, 20–50 KB, recent passport-size with white background
  • Signature: JPG/JPEG, 10–20 KB, black ink on white paper

If your photo is too large, use our Image Compressor to reduce file size without losing quality. Need to resize to exact dimensions? Use the Image Resizer to set precise pixel dimensions. Both tools work in your browser — no upload to any server required.

Step 6: Exam Centre and Payment

Select your preferred exam centres (usually 3 choices). Pick centres close to your location. Then pay the application fee online via UPI, net banking, or debit/credit card. General/OBC male candidates pay ₹100. Women, SC, ST, and Ex-servicemen are exempted. Save the payment receipt.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Name mismatch — use the exact name from your 10th certificate
  • Blurry photo — use a clear, recent photo with plain background
  • Oversized files — compress images to meet KB limits
  • Wrong category — ensure your certificate matches the selected category
  • Not saving confirmation — always download and print the final application
  • Last-minute submission — servers crash on deadline day, apply early

After Submission

Download the application PDF and keep it safe. You will need the registration number for admit card download. Check the SSC website regularly for updates on exam dates and admit card availability.
