📋 Track Your Exam Syllabus Progress

April 2026 · 3 min read

One of the biggest challenges in exam preparation is knowing how much syllabus you have covered and how much is left. A visual progress tracker solves this problem.

Why Track Syllabus Progress?

  • Stay organized — know exactly what topics are pending
  • Reduce anxiety — seeing progress motivates you
  • Plan better — allocate time to uncovered topics
  • No surprises — avoid missing important topics before the exam

Pre-Built Syllabi Available

Our Syllabus Tracker comes with ready-made checklists for:

  • UPSC Prelims — 25 topics covering all GS subjects
  • SSC CGL — 19 topics across all sections
  • Banking (IBPS PO) — 20 topics for all papers
  • Custom — create your own syllabus checklist

How to Use

  1. Select your exam or create custom syllabus
  2. Check off topics as you complete them
  3. Watch the progress bar fill up
  4. Data auto-saves — come back daily to update
