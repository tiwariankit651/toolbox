How to Arrange Pages Properly

This is where most people mess up. You've taken 8 photos of your assignment, but they're not in order. Or you accidentally added the last page first.

Tips for proper page arrangement:

  • Name your files sequentially — Before uploading, rename files as 01.jpg, 02.jpg, 03.jpg. Most tools sort by filename.
  • Use drag-and-drop reordering — Our tool lets you drag pages to rearrange them visually
  • Preview before converting — Always check the order before hitting convert
  • Remove duplicates — Accidentally added the same photo twice? Remove it before converting

Pro tip: If you're scanning a handwritten assignment, take photos in order from page 1 to last page. Baad mein reorder karna mushkil hota hai when you have 15+ pages that look similar.

Quality Tips — Get Clear, Readable PDFs

A blurry PDF is worse than no PDF. Here's how to ensure your converted PDFs look professional:

While Taking Photos

  • Use good lighting — natural daylight is best, avoid shadows
  • Keep the camera parallel to the document (not at an angle)
  • Fill the frame — don't include too much background
  • Use your phone's document scanning mode if available (CamScanner mode in most phones)
  • Clean your camera lens — sounds obvious but makes a huge difference

While Converting

  • Use the highest quality setting for important documents
  • Choose "Fit to Page" for documents, "Original Size" for photos
  • If the file is too large, use our image compressor first to reduce individual image sizes

File Size Optimization

Government portals are notorious for file size limits. SSC wants below 2MB, IBPS wants below 1MB, some college portals have 500KB limits. Pagal kar dete hain ye limits.

Here's how to keep your PDF small:

  • Compress images before converting — Use our image compressor to reduce each image to 100-200KB without visible quality loss
  • Use JPEG format — JPG files are smaller than PNG for photos. Only use PNG for screenshots or text-heavy images
  • Reduce resolution — For documents, 150 DPI is enough. You don't need 4K resolution for a marksheet scan
  • Crop unnecessary areas — Remove the table/desk background from your document photos
  • Convert to grayscale — If the document is black and white anyway, grayscale reduces file size by 60-70%

Quick math: 5 images × 3MB each = 15MB PDF. After compression: 5 images × 200KB each = 1MB PDF. Same readability, 15x smaller file. That's the difference between "upload successful" and "file too large" error.

Troubleshooting Common Issues

  • "PDF is blurry" — Your source images are low quality. Retake photos with better lighting and steady hands.
  • "File too large" — Compress images first, or reduce the quality setting during conversion.
  • "Pages are rotated" — Rotate images before adding them to the converter. Most tools have a rotate button.
  • "Black borders around pages" — Crop your images to remove the background before converting.
  • "Portal rejects my PDF" — Check if they need a specific page size (usually A4) or color mode (RGB vs grayscale).

Agar koi aur problem aaye toh yaad rakho — 90% of PDF issues come from poor source images. Fix the photo quality first, conversion is the easy part.
