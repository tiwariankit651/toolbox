🎯 How to Prepare for Government Exams — Free Study Tools

April 2026 · 5 min read

Preparing for SSC CGL, UPSC, Banking, or Railway exams? You do not need expensive coaching or paid apps. Here are free tools and strategies that can help you prepare effectively from home.

1. Build Your Knowledge Base with Flashcards

Active recall is the most effective study technique. Instead of just reading notes, test yourself with flashcards.

Our Flashcard Study Tool has ready-made sets for:

  • Ancient, Medieval, and Modern Indian History
  • Indian Polity and Constitution
  • Indian Geography
  • Indian Economy
  • Science and Technology
  • English Vocabulary
  • Computer Knowledge

You can also create your own flashcard sets and share them with friends.

2. Improve Your Typing Speed

Many government exams require a typing test:

  • SSC CHSL: 35 WPM English / 30 WPM Hindi
  • SSC CGL: 35 WPM
  • Court Stenographer: 80-100 WPM

Practice daily with our Typing Speed Test — supports English, Hindi, and 8 other Indian languages with beginner to expert difficulty levels.

3. Use the Pomodoro Technique

Study for 25 minutes, take a 5-minute break. This prevents burnout and improves focus. Use our Pomodoro Timer to stay on track.

4. Calculate Your CGPA and Percentage

Many forms ask for CGPA or percentage. Use our CGPA Calculator to convert between grading systems accurately.

5. Prepare Your Documents

Government forms need specific photo and signature sizes. Use these tools:

  • Image Resizer — resize photos to exact pixels or cm (presets for SSC, UPSC, Banking)
  • Signature Maker — create digital signature
  • PDF Compressor — reduce document size for upload
  • PDF Merger — combine all documents into one file
  • Background Changer — change photo background to white

Government exam preparation is a marathon, not a sprint. Study daily even if it is just 1-2 hours. Use our tools to make your preparation efficient and organized. All tools are free, work on mobile, and need no signup.
