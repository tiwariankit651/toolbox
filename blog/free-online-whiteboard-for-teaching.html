🎨 Free Online Whiteboard for Teaching and Drawing

April 2026 · 3 min read

Need a quick whiteboard for explaining a concept, drawing a diagram, or brainstorming ideas? Our free online whiteboard works instantly — no app download, no signup.

Who Can Use It?

  • Teachers — explain concepts during online classes, draw diagrams
  • Students — make quick diagrams for assignments, practice drawing
  • Professionals — brainstorm ideas, sketch wireframes
  • Anyone — doodle, draw, or just have fun

Features

  • Pen tool with custom colors and sizes
  • Eraser tool for corrections
  • Multiple background colors — white, dark, gray, yellow
  • Undo button for mistakes
  • Export as PNG image
  • Touch support for mobile and tablets

The whiteboard runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is saved to any server. Use our Whiteboard tool for your next class or meeting.
