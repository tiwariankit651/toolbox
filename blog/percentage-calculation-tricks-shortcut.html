Basic Percentage Formula — Pehle Yeh Samjho

Percentage ka simple formula hai:

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

Example: Tumne 500 mein se 350 marks score kiye. Percentage = (350 ÷ 500) × 100 = 70%

Yeh toh basic hai. Ab aate hain real shortcut tricks pe jo mental math mein kaam aaye.

5 Percentage Shortcut Tricks

Trick 1: 10% Nikalna = Divide by 10

Kisi bhi number ka 10% nikalna hai? Bas ek zero hata do (ya decimal point ek jagah left move karo).

  • ₹4,500 ka 10% = ₹450
  • ₹1,200 ka 10% = ₹120
  • ₹780 ka 10% = ₹78

Yeh trick baaki sab tricks ki foundation hai — isko yaad rakhna!

Trick 2: 5% Nikalna = 10% ka Half

5% calculate karna ho toh pehle 10% nikalo, phir uska aadha kar do.

  • ₹4,500 ka 5% = ₹450 ÷ 2 = ₹225
  • ₹1,200 ka 5% = ₹120 ÷ 2 = ₹60

Real use: Restaurant bill pe 5% GST calculate karna ho toh yeh trick use karo. Bill ₹860 hai? 10% = ₹86, 5% = ₹43. Done!

Trick 3: 15% Nikalna = 10% + 5%

15% is simply 10% plus 5%. Dono tricks combine kar lo.

  • ₹2,000 ka 15% = ₹200 + ₹100 = ₹300
  • ₹3,600 ka 15% = ₹360 + ₹180 = ₹540

Real use: Restaurant mein 15% tip dena hai? Bill ₹1,400 → 10% = ₹140, 5% = ₹70, total tip = ₹210.

Trick 4: 1% Nikalna = Divide by 100

Kisi bhi number ka 1% nikalna hai? Decimal point 2 jagah left move karo.

  • ₹50,000 ka 1% = ₹500
  • ₹8,500 ka 1% = ₹85

Ab iska power yeh hai ki koi bhi odd percentage nikal sakte ho. 3% chahiye? 1% × 3. 7% chahiye? 1% × 7.

Example: ₹50,000 ka 3% = ₹500 × 3 = ₹1,500. Simple!

Trick 5: Reverse Percentage — Original Price Nikalo

Yeh trick bahut kaam ki hai jab sale price diya ho aur original price nikalna ho.

Formula: Original Price = Sale Price ÷ (1 - Discount%/100)

  • Koi item ₹850 mein mila after 15% discount. Original = ₹850 ÷ 0.85 = ₹1,000
  • ₹720 after 20% off. Original = ₹720 ÷ 0.80 = ₹900

Next time Myntra pe "70% OFF" dikhe toh original price verify kar lena — bahut baar inflated hota hai!

Real-Life Examples — Kahan Kahan Use Hota Hai

🛒 Shopping Discount Calculation

Amazon Great Indian Festival pe ₹2,999 ki shoes pe 30% off hai. Kitne mein milega?

10% = ₹300, so 30% = ₹300 × 3 = ₹900 discount. Final price = ₹2,999 - ₹900 = ₹2,099

📝 Exam Marks Percentage

Tumhare Class 12 boards mein total 500 marks hain, tumne 425 score kiye:

Percentage = (425 ÷ 500) × 100 = 85%

Quick trick: 500 base hai toh simply marks ko 5 se divide karo aur result mein decimal hata do. 425 ÷ 5 = 85. Done!

💰 Salary Hike Calculation

Current salary ₹40,000 hai aur 12% hike mili:

10% = ₹4,000. 2% = ₹800 (1% = ₹400, × 2). Total hike = ₹4,800. New salary = ₹44,800

Markup vs Margin — Yeh Confuse Mat Hona

Business karte ho ya commerce student ho toh yeh difference samajhna important hai:

  • Markup = (Profit ÷ Cost Price) × 100 — cost ke upar kitna add kiya
  • Margin = (Profit ÷ Selling Price) × 100 — selling price ka kitna hissa profit hai

Example: Item cost ₹100, sold at ₹150:

  • Markup = (50 ÷ 100) × 100 = 50%
  • Margin = (50 ÷ 150) × 100 = 33.3%

Same transaction, different percentages! Isliye dono mein confuse mat hona. Hamare calculator tool mein dono calculate kar sakte ho.
