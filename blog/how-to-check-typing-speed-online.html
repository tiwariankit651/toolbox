⌨️ How to Check Typing Speed Online Free — WPM Kaise Check Kare

July 2026 · 6 min read

Typing speed — ye ek aisi skill hai jo almost har field mein kaam aati hai. Chahe tum SSC CHSL ka typing test de rahe ho, data entry job ke liye apply kar rahe ho, ya simply apna college assignment jaldi finish karna chahte ho — typing speed matter karti hai.

Lekin pehle ye jaanna zaroori hai ki tumhari current speed kitni hai. Tabhi toh improve karoge na? Aaj main tumhe bataunga — WPM kya hai, kaise check karte hain, aur kaise improve karte hain.

WPM Kya Hota Hai?

WPM = Words Per Minute. Ye measure karta hai ki tum ek minute mein kitne words type kar sakte ho. Lekin yahan "word" ka matlab exact word nahi hai — standard calculation mein 1 word = 5 characters (spaces included) maana jaata hai.

Formula:

WPM = (Total Characters Typed ÷ 5) ÷ Time in Minutes

Example: Agar tumne 1 minute mein 200 characters type kiye, toh tumhara WPM = 200 ÷ 5 = 40 WPM.

Net WPM vs Gross WPM

  • Gross WPM — Total words typed (including mistakes)
  • Net WPM — Gross WPM minus errors. Ye tumhari actual usable speed hai

Jab koi "typing speed" bolta hai, toh usually Net WPM ki baat hoti hai.

Finger Placement Basics — Touch Typing

Agar tum abhi bhi 2-3 fingers se type karte ho (hunt & peck), toh speed kabhi 40+ nahi jayegi. Touch typing seekhna padega:

Home Row Position

Dono haath keyboard pe rakho:

  • Left hand: A S D F (pinky se index finger tak)
  • Right hand: J K L ; (index finger se pinky tak)
  • Thumbs: Space bar pe

F aur J keys pe ek chhota bump hota hai — wo tumhe bina dekhe position find karne mein help karta hai.

Kaunsi Finger Kaunsi Key?

  • Index fingers: F/G (left), H/J (right) + numbers 4,5,6,7
  • Middle fingers: D (left), K (right) + numbers 3,8
  • Ring fingers: S (left), L (right) + numbers 2,9
  • Pinky: A, Shift, Caps (left), ;, Enter, Backspace (right)

Common Mistakes Jo Speed Slow Karti Hain

  • Keyboard dekhna — Screen pe dekho, keyboard pe nahi. Initially slow lagega but 1 week mein habit ban jayegi
  • Galat finger use karna — Har key ke liye designated finger use karo
  • Speed pe focus, accuracy ignore — Pehle accuracy 95%+ lao, speed automatically badhegi
  • Irregular practice — Daily 15 min better hai than weekly 2 hours
  • Tense rehna — Relax raho, wrists straight rakho, shoulders down

4-Week Improvement Plan

Week 1: Foundation (Target: Learn home row)

  • Daily 15-20 minutes practice
  • Sirf home row keys practice karo (ASDF JKL;)
  • Screen dekho, keyboard nahi — ye rule strictly follow karo
  • Speed matter nahi karti abhi — accuracy 95%+ rakho
  • Expected speed: 10-15 WPM (ye normal hai, frustrate mat ho)

Week 2: Expand (Target: Top & bottom rows)

  • Top row (QWERTY) aur bottom row (ZXCVB) add karo
  • Simple words type karo — "the", "and", "for", "with"
  • Daily 20 minutes, 2 sessions mein divide karo
  • Expected speed: 15-25 WPM

Week 3: Build Speed (Target: Sentences)

  • Full sentences type karo
  • Punctuation practice — comma, period, question mark
  • Numbers aur special characters add karo
  • Daily typing test do — track progress
  • Expected speed: 25-35 WPM

Week 4: Polish (Target: Consistency)

  • Paragraphs type karo — articles, stories
  • Speed tests do regularly — hamare typing test se
  • Weak keys identify karo aur extra practice do
  • Expected speed: 35-45 WPM with 95%+ accuracy

Daily Practice Tips

  • Consistency > Duration — 15 min daily beats 2 hours on weekends
  • Morning mein practice karo — Fresh mind = better muscle memory
  • Real content type karo — News articles, book paragraphs copy karo
  • Track progress — Weekly apna WPM note karo, graph banao
  • Ergonomics — Chair height sahi rakho, wrists flat, screen eye level pe

Typing Test Ke Liye Pro Tips

Jab actual exam ya job test dena ho:

  • Test se pehle 5 min warm-up karo
  • Backspace zyada use mat karo — time waste hota hai
  • Rhythm maintain karo — burst typing avoid karo
  • Agar galti ho gayi aur aage badh gaye, toh chhodo — wapas jaake fix karne mein zyada time lagta hai
