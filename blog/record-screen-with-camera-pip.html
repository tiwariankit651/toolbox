🎥 Record Screen with Camera Overlay (PIP)

April 2026 · 3 min read

Want to record your screen with your face visible in the corner? This is called Picture-in-Picture (PIP) mode — perfect for tutorials, presentations, and online teaching.

How to Record with Camera

  1. Open Screen Recorder
  2. Check "Camera Overlay (PIP)"
  3. Choose camera position (bottom-right, top-left, etc.)
  4. Select camera size (small, medium, large)
  5. Optionally check "Include Audio"
  6. Click Start Recording and share your screen
  7. Your camera feed appears as a rounded overlay
  8. Click Stop and download the video

Best For

  • YouTube tutorials — show your face while demonstrating
  • Online teaching — students see you while you explain slides
  • Presentations — add a personal touch
  • Bug reports — show your reaction while demonstrating issues

Works best in Chrome and Edge. No software installation needed — everything runs in your browser.
