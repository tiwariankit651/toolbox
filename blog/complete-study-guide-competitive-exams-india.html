🎯 Complete Study Guide for Competitive Exams in India

April 2026 · 10 min read

Preparing for competitive exams in India — whether SSC CGL, UPSC Civil Services, Banking (IBPS/SBI), Railway, or State PSC — requires a systematic approach, consistent effort, and the right tools. This guide provides a complete roadmap using free online tools that can significantly improve your preparation efficiency.

Phase 1: Understanding the Exam

Before starting preparation, understand your target exam thoroughly. Use our Exam Calendar to check upcoming exam dates and plan your timeline. Key exams in 2026 include UPSC Prelims (May), SSC CGL (June), SBI PO (June), IBPS PO (October), and various state PSC exams throughout the year.

Phase 2: Syllabus Tracking

The biggest mistake aspirants make is not tracking their syllabus completion. Our Syllabus Progress Tracker comes with pre-built checklists for UPSC Prelims (25 topics), SSC CGL (19 topics), and Banking (20 topics). You can also create custom syllabi. Check off topics as you complete them and watch your progress bar fill up. Data saves automatically in your browser.

Phase 3: Daily Study Routine

Consistency is more important than intensity. Here is a recommended daily routine using free tools:

Morning Session (2 hours)

  • Flashcard Revision (30 min): Use our Flashcards with 250+ cards across 9 subjects — Ancient History, Medieval History, Modern History, Indian Polity, Geography, Economy, Science, English Vocabulary, and Computer Knowledge
  • Focused Study (90 min): Use our Pomodoro Timer — 25 minutes study, 5 minutes break, repeat 3 times

Afternoon Session (2 hours)

  • MCQ Practice (45 min): Take quizzes on our Quiz Maker — 201 questions across 19 subjects including SSC PYQ, Current Affairs, Reasoning, and Math Aptitude
  • Current Affairs (30 min): Read news and use our Speed Reader to practice reading faster
  • Vocabulary Building (15 min): Learn new words with our Vocabulary Builder — English words with Hindi meanings and quiz mode
  • Typing Practice (30 min): Essential for SSC CHSL/CGL — practice on our Typing Test with Hindi and English support

Evening Session (1 hour)

  • Essay/Answer Writing (30 min): Use our Essay Outline Generator to practice structuring answers for UPSC mains
  • Formula Revision (15 min): Quick revision using our Formula Sheet — 80+ formulas for Math, Physics, Chemistry
  • Habit Tracking (5 min): Log your daily study habits on our Habit Tracker to maintain streaks

Phase 4: Document Preparation

When exam forms open, you need documents ready in specific formats. Here is your checklist:

Photo Requirements

Use our Image Resizer with built-in presets for SSC (150×200 px), UPSC (110×140 px), Banking (200×230 px), and Passport (3.5×4.5 cm). Select the output format (JPG recommended) and ensure file size is within limits.

Signature

Create a digital signature using our Signature Maker — draw with mouse or finger, choose black ink, download as transparent PNG. Then resize to required dimensions (typically 140×60 px for SSC).

Date on Photo

Many exams require the date of photograph on the image. Use our Date on Photo tool to add a clean date strip.

Document Merging and Compression

Combine all certificates into one PDF using PDF Merger, then compress using PDF Compressor to meet upload size limits.

Phase 5: Resume and Cover Letter

After clearing the exam, you need professional documents for the interview stage. Our Resume Builder offers 3 templates (Classic, Modern, Minimal) with custom accent colors, and our Cover Letter Generator creates formatted letters from a simple form.

Key Tips for Success

  1. Start early: Give yourself at least 6-12 months for UPSC, 3-6 months for SSC/Banking
  2. Be consistent: 4-5 hours daily is better than 12 hours on weekends
  3. Practice MCQs daily: Even 20 questions a day adds up to 7000+ questions in a year
  4. Track progress: Use the syllabus tracker to know exactly where you stand
  5. Stay healthy: Use the habit tracker to maintain exercise, sleep, and study balance
  6. Revise regularly: Flashcards with spaced repetition are the most effective revision method
