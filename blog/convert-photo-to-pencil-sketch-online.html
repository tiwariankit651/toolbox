April 2026 · 3 min read

Transform your photos into beautiful pencil sketch artwork — no drawing skills needed. Our tool uses edge detection and color dodge blending to create realistic sketch effects.

Three Sketch Styles

  • Pencil — classic pencil drawing look with soft edges
  • Charcoal — darker, more dramatic with deeper shadows
  • Ink — high contrast black and white, bold lines

How to Create a Sketch

  1. Open Image to Pencil Sketch
  2. Upload any photo

  5. See original vs sketch side by side
  6. Download your sketch

Best Results With

  • Portrait photos with good lighting
  • High contrast images
