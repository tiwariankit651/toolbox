💹 SIP vs Lumpsum Investment — Mutual Fund Mein Invest Kaise Kare

July 2026 · 8 min read

Agar aap mutual fund mein invest karna chahte ho lekin confuse ho ki SIP better hai ya lumpsum — toh yeh guide aapke liye hai. Hum simple Hindi-English mein samjhayenge ki dono mein kya farak hai, kab kaunsa choose karna chahiye, aur compounding ka magic kaise kaam karta hai.

SIP Kya Hai? (Systematic Investment Plan)

SIP matlab har mahine ek fixed amount mutual fund mein invest karna. Jaise aap ₹5,000 har mahine invest karte ho — chahe market upar ho ya neeche. Yeh bilkul recurring deposit jaisa hai, bas returns zyada milte hain.

SIP ka sabse bada fayda yeh hai ki aapko market timing ki tension nahi leni padti. Aap consistently invest karte rehte ho aur long term mein wealth create hoti hai.

Lumpsum Investment Kya Hai?

Lumpsum matlab ek baar mein poori rakam invest kar dena. Jaise aapko bonus mila ₹2,00,000 — aur aapne ek hi baar mein mutual fund mein daal diya. Yeh tab kaam aata hai jab aapke paas ek badi amount available ho.

SIP Kab Better Hai?

  • Salaried people — har mahine salary aati hai, ₹5,000–₹10,000 SIP set karo
  • Market volatile ho — SIP average out karta hai ups and downs
  • First time investor — ₹500 se start karo, risk kam lagta hai
  • Long term goals — retirement, child education, house down payment

Lumpsum Kab Better Hai?

  • Bonus ya inheritance mila — ₹5 lakh ek baar mein invest karo
  • Market crash ke baad — neeche market mein zyada units milte hain
  • Short term bullish market — poora paisa ek saath kaam karta hai
  • Property sale ka paisa — idle mat rakhna, invest karo

Rupee Cost Averaging — Simply Samjho

Yeh SIP ka superpower hai. Jab market neeche hota hai, aapke ₹5,000 se zyada units milte hain. Jab upar hota hai, kam units milte hain. Overall average cost kam ho jata hai.

Example: Jan mein NAV ₹100 (50 units), Feb mein NAV ₹80 (62.5 units), March mein NAV ₹120 (41.67 units). Average cost = ₹97.3/unit — jo ₹100 se kam hai!

Real Mutual Fund Returns — India

  • Nifty 50 Index Fund — ~12% average (last 20 years)
  • Large Cap Funds — 11-14% average
  • Mid Cap Funds — 14-18% (higher risk)
  • ELSS Tax Saving — 12-15% + Section 80C benefit

Note: Past returns guarantee nahi hain, lekin long term mein equity ne FD (6-7%) se consistently better perform kiya hai.
