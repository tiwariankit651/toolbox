🎨 Best Free Alternatives to Canva & Photoshop

May 2026 · 5 min read

Canva charges ₹500/month for Pro features. Photoshop costs ₹1,675/month. For most everyday image tasks — resizing a photo, removing a background, compressing for web, or adding a watermark — you do not need expensive subscriptions. FreeToolHubs offers browser-based tools that handle these tasks instantly, with no signup, no uploads to external servers, and zero cost.

The Problem with Canva and Photoshop

  • Canva Free — limited exports, watermarked premium elements, requires account creation
  • Canva Pro — ₹500/month for background remover and resize features
  • Photoshop — ₹1,675/month, heavy software, steep learning curve
  • Both — upload your images to their servers, raising privacy concerns

If you just need to get a specific task done quickly, here are free alternatives that work right in your browser.

Task-by-Task Alternatives

1. Image Compression (Photoshop "Save for Web")

In Photoshop, you would use File → Export → Save for Web to reduce file size. Our Image Compressor does the same thing — reduce JPG/PNG file size while maintaining visual quality. Perfect for website optimization, email attachments, or meeting government form upload limits.

2. Image Resizing (Canva Resize / Photoshop Image Size)

Canva locks the "Magic Resize" feature behind Pro. Photoshop requires opening the entire application just to change dimensions. Our Image Resizer lets you set exact pixel dimensions or scale by percentage — instantly, no account needed.

3. Background Removal (Canva BG Remover / Photoshop Select Subject)

Canva's background remover is Pro-only. Photoshop requires manual selection or AI tools that need a subscription. Our Background Remover uses AI to remove backgrounds in one click — completely free, processed locally in your browser.

4. Adding Watermarks (Photoshop Layers)

In Photoshop, watermarking requires creating text layers, adjusting opacity, and positioning manually. Our Watermark Tool lets you add text or logo watermarks with adjustable opacity and position — no layer knowledge required.

5. Photo Collages (Canva Collage Templates)

Canva offers collage templates but requires signup and uploads your images. Our Collage Maker creates beautiful photo collages with multiple layout options — all processed locally on your device.

6. Image to PDF (Photoshop PDF Export)

Converting multiple images to a single PDF in Photoshop is tedious — open each file, arrange, export. Our Image to PDF tool lets you drag multiple photos and combine them into one PDF in seconds.

7. PDF Merging (Adobe Acrobat Pro)

Adobe charges for PDF merging in Acrobat Pro. Our PDF Merger combines multiple PDF files into one — free, no file size limits, no account required.

8. Pencil Sketch Effect (Photoshop Filters)

Creating a pencil sketch effect in Photoshop requires multiple filter steps and layer blending. Our Pencil Sketch tool converts any photo to a realistic pencil drawing with one click.

Why Browser-Based Tools Win

  • Privacy — your images never leave your device
  • Speed — no software to download or update
  • Cost — completely free, no hidden charges
  • Accessibility — works on any device with a browser (phone, tablet, laptop)
  • No account — start using immediately, no email verification

When You Still Need Canva or Photoshop

These tools are not replacements for everything. If you need advanced photo retouching, vector illustration, multi-page design layouts, or brand kit management, Canva Pro or Photoshop still make sense. But for the 80% of tasks that are simple image operations, free browser tools save you time and money.
