Yeh situations mein aapko digital signature chahiye hogi:

Government Forms Ke Liye Signature — Size Requirements

Government exam forms mein specific signature size chahiye hoti hai:

  • SSC: 140x60 pixels, 1-12 KB, JPG format
  • UPSC: 150x70 pixels, max 40 KB
  • IBPS/Banking: 140x60 pixels, 10-20 KB
  • JEE/NEET: 150x70 pixels, 4-30 KB

Tips for govt form signatures:

  • White paper pe black pen se sign karo
  • Signature ke around zyada white space mat chodo
  • Crop karke exact signature area rakhho
  • JPG format mein save karo (PNG nahi — size zyada hoti hai)

India Mein Digital Signature Ki Legal Validity

Bahut log confuse hote hain — kya online signature legally valid hai? Short answer: Haan, conditions ke saath.

  • IT Act 2000 (Section 3A) — Electronic signatures legally valid hain India mein
  • Simple e-signature (jo hum bana rahe hain) — Valid for most private agreements, job documents, internal forms
  • DSC (Digital Signature Certificate) — Government-issued, required for ITR filing, MCA filings, e-tendering. Yeh alag cheez hai — Certifying Authority se milta hai.
  • Aadhaar-based e-sign — OTP-based signing, legally valid under IT Act

Kahan valid hai simple e-signature: Job offer letters, rent agreements, freelance contracts, internal company documents, college forms.

Kahan DSC chahiye: Government tenders, company registration (MCA), income tax filing, patent applications.

Professional Signature Banane Ke Tips

  • Consistent rakho — Har jagah same signature use karo. Alag-alag signatures se problems aati hain.
  • Readable banana zaruri nahi — Signature unique honi chahiye, readable nahi. Lekin initials clear hone chahiye.
  • Blue ya black ink — Professional documents mein sirf yeh 2 colors acceptable hain. Blue preferred hai kyunki original vs photocopy differentiate hota hai.
  • Size moderate rakho — Na bahut choti, na bahut badi. Signature line ke 70-80% space cover kare.
  • Practice karo — Paper pe 20-30 baar sign karo before finalizing. Consistency important hai.
  • Simple rakho — Bahut complex signature reproduce karna mushkil hota hai. Simple yet unique best hai.

Common Mistakes — Avoid Karein

  • Low resolution image upload karna — blurry signature reject hoti hai
  • Background remove na karna — white box dikhta hai document pe
  • Pencil se sign karna — faint aati hai scan mein
  • Signature mein date likhna — date separately add karo
  • Har baar alag signature banana — consistency maintain karo
