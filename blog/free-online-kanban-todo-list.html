📋 Free Online Kanban Board and To-Do List

April 2026 · 3 min read

Stay organized with a simple Kanban-style task board. Move tasks between columns as you work on them — a proven method used by top companies worldwide.

What is Kanban?

Kanban is a visual task management method with three columns: To Do (planned), Doing (in progress), and Done (completed). It helps you see your workload at a glance.

How to Use

  1. Open To-Do Kanban Board
  2. Add tasks to the To Do column
  3. Move tasks to Doing when you start working
  4. Move to Done when completed
  5. Clear done tasks to keep the board clean

Great For

  • Daily task management
  • Study planning — track assignments and chapters
  • Project management
  • Exam preparation — organize topics to study

All data saves automatically in your browser. Come back anytime to update your tasks.
