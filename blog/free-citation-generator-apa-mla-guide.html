Citations Kyon Zaruri Hain?

1. Plagiarism Se Bachao

Kisi aur ka idea ya words use karo bina credit diye = plagiarism. Indian universities ab Turnitin aur Urkund jaise tools use karti hain. Plagiarism pakdi gayi toh:

  • Assignment reject ho sakti hai
  • Marks deduct ho sakte hain
  • Serious cases mein rustication bhi ho sakta hai

Proper citation = safe zone. Tum openly source use kar sakte ho — bas credit do.

2. Credibility Badhti Hai

Jab tumhare paper mein 10-15 proper references hain toh professor ko lagta hai — "yeh banda/bandi ne actually research ki hai." Tumhara argument stronger lagta hai because it's backed by existing research.

3. Readers Ko Source Milta Hai

Agar koi tumhara paper padh ke original source dekhna chahe toh citation se directly ja sakta hai. Yeh academic integrity ka core principle hai.

Confused? Default mein APA use karo unless professor ne specifically kuch aur bola ho.

APA Format Examples

📖 Book Citation (APA)

Last Name, Initials. (Year). Title of book in italics. Publisher.

Example:

Sharma, R. K. (2023). Indian economy: Problems and prospects. McGraw Hill India.

📄 Journal Article (APA)

Author, A. B. (Year). Title of article. Journal Name, Volume(Issue), Pages. https://doi.org/xxxxx

Example:

Patel, S., & Gupta, M. (2024). Digital literacy among rural Indian youth. Journal of Education Technology, 15(3), 45–62. https://doi.org/10.1234/jet.2024.003

🌐 Website Citation (APA)

Author/Organization. (Year, Month Day). Title of page. Site Name. URL

Example:

Ministry of Education. (2025, March 15). New Education Policy 2020 highlights. Government of India. https://www.education.gov.in/nep

MLA Format Examples

📖 Book Citation (MLA)

Last Name, First Name. Title of Book. Publisher, Year.

Example:

Bhatt, Chetan. Half Girlfriend. Rupa Publications, 2014.

🌐 Website Citation (MLA)

Author. "Title of Page." Website Name, Day Month Year, URL.

Common Citation Mistakes — Yeh Mat Karna

  1. URL se kaam chala lena — sirf link dalna citation nahi hai. Format follow karo.
  2. Date miss karna — APA mein date bahut important hai. "n.d." likho agar date nahi mili.
  3. Italics miss karna — book titles aur journal names italics mein hone chahiye.
  4. et al. ka galat use — APA mein 3+ authors ke liye pehli baar sab likho, phir "et al." use karo (in-text mein 3+ pe directly et al.).
  5. Bibliography vs Reference List confuse karna — neeche explain kiya hai.

Bibliography vs Reference List — Kya Difference Hai?

  • Reference List (APA) — sirf woh sources jo tumne paper mein cite kiye hain. Agar use nahi kiya, list mein mat daalo.
  • Bibliography (Chicago/MLA) — sab sources jo tumne padhe, chahe directly cite kiye ho ya nahi. Broader scope.

APA paper likh rahe ho? "References" heading use karo. MLA likh rahe ho? "Works Cited" heading use karo.

In-Text Citation Rules

APA In-Text

  • 1 author: (Sharma, 2023)
  • 2 authors: (Patel & Gupta, 2024)
  • 3+ authors: (Kumar et al., 2025)
  • Direct quote: (Sharma, 2023, p. 45)

MLA In-Text

  • 1 author: (Bhatt 67)
  • 2 authors: (Patel and Gupta 112)
  • No page number (website): (Kumar)

Important: In-text citation aur reference list dono hone chahiye. Sirf ek se kaam nahi chalega!

Pro Tips for Indian Students

  • Professor ka instruction > internet rules. Agar professor ne specific format bola hai, wahi follow karo.

  • IGNOU, DU, JNU — sab APA prefer karte hain for social sciences.
  • PhD thesis mein minimum 50-100 references expected hain.
  • Wikipedia cite mat karo directly — uski references use karo (original source pe jao).
