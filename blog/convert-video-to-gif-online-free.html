🎬 How to Convert Video to GIF Online

April 2026 · 3 min read

GIFs are perfect for social media, memes, tutorials, and messaging. Converting a video clip to GIF is easy with our free tool.

How to Convert

  1. Open Video to GIF Converter
  2. Upload your video (MP4, WebM, MOV)
  3. Set start and end time to trim
  4. Choose FPS (10 for small file, 20 for smooth)
  5. Set output width
  6. Click Convert and download your GIF

Tips for Smaller GIFs

  • Keep duration under 5 seconds
  • Use 10 FPS instead of 20
  • Set width to 320px or less
  • Trim to only the important part

Everything runs in your browser using gif.js library. Your video never leaves your device.
