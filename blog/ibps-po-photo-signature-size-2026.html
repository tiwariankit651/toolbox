🏦 IBPS PO Photo & Signature Size Requirements 2026

June 2026 · 4 min read

IBPS (Institute of Banking Personnel Selection) conducts exams for PO, Clerk, SO, and RRB posts. The online application requires uploading a photograph, signature, left thumb impression, and a handwritten declaration. Getting the dimensions and file size wrong is one of the most common reasons for application rejection. Here is everything you need to know.

IBPS PO Photo Requirements

  • Dimensions: 200 x 230 pixels
  • File size: 20 KB to 50 KB
  • Format: JPEG / JPG
  • Background: White or off-white
  • Face coverage: 80% of the frame
  • Date requirement: Photo taken after 01-01-2026 (printed on photo)

IBPS PO Signature Requirements

  • Dimensions: 140 x 60 pixels
  • File size: 10 KB to 20 KB
  • Format: JPEG / JPG
  • Ink: Black ink on white paper
  • Note: Signature in CAPITAL letters will be rejected

IBPS Left Thumb Impression

  • Dimensions: 240 x 240 pixels (preferred) or 200 x 230
  • File size: 20 KB to 50 KB
  • Method: Use blue/black stamp pad ink on white paper

How to Resize Signature for IBPS

  1. Sign on white paper with black ink pen
  2. Photograph or scan the signature
  3. Open the Image Resizer
  4. Set width to 140 and height to 60 pixels
  5. Compress to under 20 KB using the Image Compressor

Important Tips for IBPS Candidates

  • Name & date on photo: IBPS requires the candidate's name and date printed below the photo
  • No caps signature: Writing your name in all capitals is not accepted as a valid signature
  • Same signature everywhere: Use the same signature style as on your ID proof
  • Keep originals safe: You will need the same photo for admit card verification
