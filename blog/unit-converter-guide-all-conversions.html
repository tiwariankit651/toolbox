Sabse Zyaada Search Hone Wali Conversions

📏 Distance — KM ↔ Miles

Formula: 1 km = 0.621 miles | 1 mile = 1.609 km

🌡️ Temperature — °C ↔ °F

Formula: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32 | °C = (°F - 32) × 5/9

Quick trick: °C ko double karo aur 30 add karo for rough °F. 25°C ≈ 50+30 = 80°F (actual 77°F — close enough!).

📏 Length — CM ↔ Inches

Formula: 1 inch = 2.54 cm | 1 cm = 0.394 inches

Common uses: Height measurement (5'7" = 170 cm), screen sizes (15.6 inch laptop = 39.6 cm), photo prints.

🥤 Volume — Liters ↔ Gallons

Formula: 1 US gallon = 3.785 liters | 1 liter = 0.264 gallons

Useful for: Petrol prices comparison (US vs India), water intake tracking, cooking large batches.

  • Recipes — Foreign recipes mein cups, oz, Fahrenheit — Indian kitchen mein ml, grams, Celsius
  • Fitness — Gym apps mein pounds, Indian scale pe kg. BMI calculate karna ho
  • Science & Engineering — Physics problems mein unit conversion fundamental hai
  • Shopping — Amazon US se kuch order karo toh dimensions inches mein hoti hain
  • Competitive Exams — SSC, Banking mein unit conversion questions aate hain

🤓 Fun Facts — Metric vs Imperial History

Kya aap jaante ho?

  • Sirf 3 countries officially Imperial system use karti hain — USA, Myanmar, aur Liberia. Baaki poori duniya metric pe hai!
  • India ne 1957 mein officially metric system adopt kiya. Pehle hum tola, ser, maund use karte the
  • "Foot" literally ek insaan ke pair ki length se aaya hai — King Henry I ke pair se!
  • NASA ne ek $125 million Mars orbiter kho diya kyunki ek team ne metric use kiya aur doosri ne imperial. Unit conversion matters!
  • Fahrenheit scale Daniel Fahrenheit ne 1724 mein banaya — 0°F sabse thanda temperature tha jo unhone measure kiya (brine solution se)

Quick Mental Math Tricks

Calculator na ho toh ye shortcuts kaam aayenge:

  • KM → Miles: Multiply by 0.6 (ya 60% le lo)
  • Miles → KM: Multiply by 1.6
  • KG → Pounds: Double it + 10%
  • Inches → CM: Multiply by 2.5
  • °C → °F: Double + 30 (rough estimate)
  • Liters → Gallons: Divide by 4 (roughly)
