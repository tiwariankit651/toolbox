Common Indian English Mistakes

Hindi speakers ko English mein kuch specific mistakes hoti hain because dono languages ki structure alag hai:

1. Tense Confusion

  • ❌ "I am having a car" → ✅ "I have a car"
  • ❌ "He is knowing the answer" → ✅ "He knows the answer"
  • ❌ "I didn't went" → ✅ "I didn't go"

Hindi mein "mere paas car hai" ko directly translate karte hain "I am having" — but English mein stative verbs ke saath continuous tense nahi use hota.

2. Articles (a, an, the) — Sabse Tricky

  • ❌ "I am engineer" → ✅ "I am an engineer"
  • ❌ "Please give me the water" (general) → ✅ "Please give me water"
  • ❌ "I went to the market yesterday" (specific market) — yeh actually correct hai!

Hindi mein articles nahi hote, isliye yeh Indians ke liye sabse mushkil part hai.

3. Prepositions

  • ❌ "I am waiting since 2 hours" → ✅ "I have been waiting for 2 hours"
  • ❌ "Discuss about the topic" → ✅ "Discuss the topic"
  • ❌ "He told to me" → ✅ "He told me"

4. Subject-Verb Agreement

  • ❌ "The team are playing well" → ✅ "The team is playing well"
  • ❌ "Each students have books" → ✅ "Each student has a book"

Grammar Naturally Improve Kaise Kare

Tool use karna great hai, but long-term improvement ke liye yeh habits develop karo:

1. Daily English Reading (30 min)

Newspapers (The Hindu, Indian Express), blogs, ya novels — jo bhi interest ho. Reading se grammar patterns subconsciously absorb hote hain. Start karo easy content se — gradually level badhao.

2. Write Daily (Even 5 Lines)

Daily diary, social media posts, ya email drafts — kuch bhi likho. Phir humara grammar checker se check karo. Mistakes note karo aur repeat mat karo.

3. Listen to English Content

Podcasts, YouTube channels, English movies with subtitles — listening se sentence structure naturally samajh aati hai.

4. Learn in Patterns, Not Rules

Instead of memorizing "present perfect is used for..." — sentences yaad karo. "I have been waiting for 2 hours" — yeh pattern yaad karo, rule nahi.

Professional Email Writing Tips

India mein job emails mein sabse zyada grammar mistakes hoti hain. Quick tips:

  • Subject line clear rakho — "Application for Software Developer Position" not "Regarding job"
  • Short paragraphs — 2-3 lines max per paragraph
  • "Please find attached" not "Please find the attached herewith enclosed"
  • Avoid "Dear Sir/Madam" — use the person's name if possible
  • Proofread with our tool before hitting send — 2 minutes mein sab mistakes fix
