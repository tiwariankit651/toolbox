📄 How to Compress PDF for Government Portal Uploads

April 2026 · 3 min read

Many government portals in India have strict file size limits for document uploads. Whether you're applying for a passport, filing income tax returns, or submitting forms on DigiLocker, UMANG, or state government websites, you'll often encounter upload limits between 200 KB and 2 MB.

Scanned documents and certificates often exceed these limits. Here's how to compress your PDFs quickly and for free.

Common File Size Limits

  • Income Tax Portal: 1 MB per document
  • Passport Seva: 300 KB - 1 MB
  • EPFO / PF Portal: 1 MB
  • University Applications: 200 KB - 500 KB
  • Job Applications: 1 - 2 MB

How to Compress PDF — Step by Step

  1. Open FreeToolHubs PDF Compressor
  2. Click "Choose File" or drag and drop your PDF
  3. The tool automatically compresses embedded images
  4. Download the compressed file

Your file never leaves your device — everything is processed in your browser. No upload to any server, so your documents stay private and secure.

Tips for Best Results

  • Scan at 150-200 DPI instead of 300 DPI — this significantly reduces file size while keeping text readable
  • Use grayscale for text documents — color scans are 3-4x larger
  • Merge then compress — if you have multiple documents, use our PDF Merger first, then compress the combined file
  • Convert images to PDF with our Image to PDF tool which lets you set custom dimensions

Why Browser-Based Compression is Better

Unlike online tools that upload your files to their servers, FreeToolHubs processes everything locally in your browser. This means:

  • Your Aadhaar, PAN, and other sensitive documents stay on your device
  • No waiting for upload/download — instant processing
  • Works even with slow internet connections
  • No file count or daily limits
