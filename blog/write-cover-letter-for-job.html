📄 How to Write a Cover Letter

April 2026 · 3 min read

A good cover letter can make the difference between getting an interview and being ignored. Our Cover Letter Generator helps you create one in minutes.

What to Include

  • Your contact information
  • Company and position details
  • Why you want this specific job
  • Your relevant skills and experience
  • Professional closing

Tips

  • Keep it under one page
  • Customize for each job
  • Mention specific skills from the job description
  • Be confident but not arrogant
