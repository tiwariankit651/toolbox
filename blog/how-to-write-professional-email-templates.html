📧 Professional Email Kaise Likhe — Free Templates & Complete Guide

July 2026 · 9 min read

Ek achhi email aapko interview dila sakti hai, ek buri email aapka impression kharab kar sakti hai. Problem yeh hai ki schools aur colleges mein email writing properly sikhaya nahi jaata. Result? Log "Respected Sir/Madam" likhte hain 2026 mein bhi, subject line blank chhod dete hain, ya 500 words ka paragraph likh dete hain bina formatting ke.

Is guide mein hum email ka structure, 5 real scenarios ke saath templates, aur common mistakes cover karenge. Plus hamare tool ki features jo aapko 30 seconds mein professional email generate karne deti hain.

Email Structure — 5 Parts Jo Har Email Mein Hone Chahiye

1. Subject Line (Sabse Important)

Subject line decide karti hai ki email open hogi ya nahi. Rules:

  • Specific > vague. "Leave Application — 22 July" ✅ vs "Request" ❌
  • 5-8 words ideal length
  • Action hint do — "Meeting Request: Project Discussion — Thursday 3 PM"
  • CAPS lock ya exclamation marks avoid karo — spam lagta hai

2. Greeting

2026 mein "Dear Sir/Madam" outdated hai — iska matlab hai aapne research nahi ki. Better options:

  • Formal: "Dear Mr. Sharma," / "Dear Ms. Gupta,"
  • Semi-formal: "Hi Rahul," / "Hello Team,"
  • Name nahi pata: "Hello," ya "Hi there," (still better than Sir/Madam)

3. Body (2-3 Short Paragraphs)

First line mein purpose batao. Phir context. Phir action needed. Bas. No lengthy introductions like "I hope this email finds you well" (unless genuinely checking on someone).

4. Closing Line

Clear next step mention karo: "Please confirm by Thursday" ya "Let me know if you need anything else."

5. Signature

Name, designation, phone number, LinkedIn (optional). Fancy quotes aur multiple fonts avoid karo.

5 Ready-Made Templates — Copy & Customize

Template 1: Job Application Follow-Up

Subject: Following Up — [Position Name] Application — [Your Name]

Hi [Hiring Manager Name],

I applied for the [Position] role on [Date] and wanted to follow up on my application status. I'm very excited about the opportunity to contribute to [Company Name]'s [specific project/team].

I'd be happy to provide any additional information needed. Would it be possible to schedule a brief call this week?

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Phone] | [LinkedIn URL]

Template 2: Leave Application

Subject: Leave Request — [Dates] — [Your Name]

Hi [Manager Name],

I'd like to request [casual/sick/earned] leave from [Start Date] to [End Date] ([X] days) due to [brief reason].

I've completed [pending task status] and [Colleague Name] has agreed to handle any urgent matters during my absence.

Please let me know if you need anything else from my side.

Thanks,
[Your Name]

Template 3: Meeting Request

Subject: Meeting Request: [Topic] — [Proposed Day/Time]

Hi [Name],

I'd like to schedule a 30-minute meeting to discuss [specific topic]. This is regarding [brief context — 1 line].

Would [Day, Time] work for you? I'm also available [alternative slot]. Happy to adjust to your schedule.

Agenda:
1. [Point 1]
2. [Point 2]
3. Next steps

Let me know what works best.

Regards,
[Your Name]

Template 4: Complaint / Issue Escalation

Subject: [Issue] — Order #[Number] — Urgent Resolution Needed

Hi [Support/Manager Name],

I'm writing regarding [specific issue] with [product/service/order]. Details:
• Order/Account: [ID]
• Date of purchase/incident: [Date]
• Issue: [Clear 1-2 line description]

I've already tried [steps taken] but the issue persists. I'd appreciate a resolution by [reasonable deadline].

Please let me know the next steps.

Regards,
[Your Name]
[Contact Number]

Template 5: Thank You After Interview

Subject: Thank You — [Position] Interview

Hi [Interviewer Name],

Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today about the [Position] role. I enjoyed learning about [specific thing discussed — team structure, project, company culture].

Our conversation about [specific topic] reinforced my excitement about this opportunity. I'm confident I can contribute to [specific value you'd add].

Looking forward to hearing about next steps.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

Do's and Don'ts — Email Etiquette 2026

✅ Do's

  • Proofread — ek typo se unprofessional lagta hai
  • Reply within 24 hours — even if just acknowledging receipt
  • Use bullet points — long paragraphs nobody reads
  • Mention deadline clearly — "by Friday 5 PM" not "soon"
  • CC only relevant people — unnecessary CC irritates everyone

❌ Don'ts

  • Don't use "Dear Sir/Madam" — it screams 2005. Find the name
  • Don't write essay-length emails — if it's more than 5 paragraphs, schedule a call
  • Don't use ALL CAPS — it reads as shouting
  • Don't reply-all unless everyone needs to see it
  • Don't send empty subject lines — instant spam folder risk
  • Don't use comic sans or colored fonts — just don't
  • Don't start with "This is to inform you that..." — just inform directly

Tone Adjustment — Formal vs Friendly

Same message, different tone:

  • Formal (Senior management, new client): "I would appreciate your feedback on the attached proposal at your earliest convenience."
  • Friendly (Colleague, familiar client): "Hey, can you take a quick look at the proposal and let me know your thoughts?"

Rule: Jis tone mein saamne wala reply kare, usi tone mein likhte raho. First email mein slightly formal side pe raho, phir calibrate karo.

Subject Line Tips — Examples

  • ❌ "Important" → ✅ "Q3 Budget Approval Required by Friday"
  • ❌ "Question" → ✅ "Quick Question: Client Onboarding Timeline"
  • ❌ "Hi" → ✅ "Invoice #1234 — Payment Confirmation"
  • ❌ "Follow up" → ✅ "Following Up: Marketing Proposal Sent 15 July"
  • ❌ "Request" → ✅ "Leave Request: 25-27 July — Rahul"

Final Tips

Email writing ek skill hai jo practice se improve hoti hai. Start with these rules: short subject line, named greeting, 3 paragraphs max, clear ask, professional signature. Bas itna follow karo aur aap 90% logon se better emails likhenge.

Remember — email ka goal clarity hai, not impressiveness. Simple language mein clear message > fancy words mein confusing message.
