💰 Income Tax Calculator India — Old vs New Regime Complete Guide (FY 2025-26 / 2026-27)

April 2026 · 12 min read

⚠️ Disclaimer: Yeh article general informational purposes ke liye hai aur yeh tax advice nahi hai. Apni specific situation ke liye ek qualified Chartered Accountant (CA) se consult karein. Tax laws change hote rehte hain aur individual circumstances vary karti hain.

Har saal tax filing season aata hai aur sabse common confusion yeh hoti hai — Old regime ya New regime, kaun sa better hai mere liye? 2020 mein government ne new tax regime introduce ki thi, aur tab se almost har budget mein isme changes aaye hain. FY 2025-26 aur FY 2026-27 ke liye new regime default option ban gayi hai, matlab agar aap actively old regime choose nahi karte toh automatically new regime apply hogi.

Is detailed guide mein hum cover karenge — dono regimes ke tax slabs, kaunsi deductions kahan allowed hain, different salary levels pe break-even analysis, aur practically kaun sa regime aapke liye better hoga. Plus, humara Tax Calculator use karke aap apne exact numbers ke saath compare kar sakte ho.

What Changed — New Regime Kaise Evolve Hui

New tax regime 2020 mein aai thi but initially log zyada interest nahi le rahe the kyunki deductions nahi milti thi aur slabs itni attractive nahi thi. Lekin Budget 2023 aur 2024 ke baad significant changes aaye:

  • Standard deduction added: Initially new regime mein standard deduction nahi thi. Ab Rs. 75,000 standard deduction available hai (FY 2025-26 onwards).
  • Rebate limit increased: Tax rebate ab Rs. 7,00,000 taxable income tak hai, matlab effectively 7 lakh tak income pe zero tax.
  • New regime is default: Ab new regime by default apply hoti hai. Old regime opt karna padta hai actively.
  • Lower surcharge: Highest surcharge 37% se reduce hokar 25% ho gaya new regime mein.
  • More slabs, lower rates: New regime mein progressive slabs hain jo gradually increase hoti hain, instead of old regime ki steep jumps.

New Regime Tax Slabs — FY 2025-26 / FY 2026-27

  • Up to Rs. 3,00,000: NIL (zero tax)
  • Rs. 3,00,001 to Rs. 7,00,000: 5%
  • Rs. 7,00,001 to Rs. 10,00,000: 10%
  • Rs. 10,00,001 to Rs. 12,00,000: 15%
  • Rs. 12,00,001 to Rs. 15,00,000: 20%
  • Above Rs. 15,00,000: 30%

Standard Deduction: Rs. 75,000
Tax Rebate (Section 87A): Full rebate if taxable income up to Rs. 7,00,000 — effective tax = NIL

Old Regime Tax Slabs — FY 2025-26 / FY 2026-27

  • Up to Rs. 2,50,000: NIL
  • Rs. 2,50,001 to Rs. 5,00,000: 5%
  • Rs. 5,00,001 to Rs. 10,00,000: 20%
  • Above Rs. 10,00,000: 30%

Standard Deduction: Rs. 50,000
Tax Rebate (Section 87A): Full rebate if taxable income up to Rs. 5,00,000

Deductions — Kahan Kya Allowed Hai

Yeh sabse important difference hai dono regimes mein. Old regime mein aapko bahut saari deductions milti hain jo taxable income reduce karti hain. New regime mein majority deductions available nahi hain.

Old Regime mein Allowed Deductions:

  • Section 80C (Rs. 1.5 lakh): PPF, ELSS, EPF, life insurance premium, home loan principal, tuition fees, NSC, tax-saving FD
  • Section 80D (Rs. 25,000-1,00,000): Health insurance premium — self, spouse, children ke liye Rs. 25,000 aur parents ke liye additional Rs. 25,000-50,000
  • HRA Exemption: Rent pay karte ho toh HRA exempt ho sakta hai based on actual rent, salary, and city
  • LTA (Leave Travel Allowance): Domestic travel pe exemption, 4 years mein 2 trips
  • Home Loan Interest (Section 24b): Up to Rs. 2,00,000 interest deduction self-occupied property ke liye
  • Section 80CCD(1B): Additional Rs. 50,000 NPS contribution
  • Section 80E: Education loan interest — no limit
  • Section 80G: Donations to approved charities
  • Professional Tax: Up to Rs. 2,500

New Regime mein Allowed Deductions:

  • Standard Deduction: Rs. 75,000
  • Employer's NPS contribution (Section 80CCD(2)): Up to 14% of basic salary (central govt) / 10% (others)
  • Family pension deduction: Rs. 15,000 or 1/3rd, whichever is less
  • Agniveer Corpus Fund (Section 80CCH)

Baaki sab — 80C, 80D, HRA, LTA, home loan interest — new regime mein available nahi hain.

Break-Even Salary Analysis — Examples at Different Income Levels

Ab practical examples dekhte hain. Hum assume kar rahe hain ki person salaried hai aur typical deductions claim kar raha hai. Exact numbers ke liye hamara Tax Calculator use karo.

Gross Salary: Rs. 7,00,000

New Regime: After standard deduction (75K), taxable income = Rs. 6,25,000. Under Rs. 7L rebate limit, so tax = Rs. 0.

Old Regime: After standard deduction (50K) + 80C (1.5L), taxable income = Rs. 5,00,000. Under 5L rebate, tax = Rs. 0.

Verdict: Dono mein zero tax hai, but new regime simpler hai — 80C investment force nahi karna padega.

Gross Salary: Rs. 10,00,000

New Regime: Taxable income = 10L - 75K = Rs. 9,25,000. Tax = (4L × 5%) + (2.25L × 10%) = Rs. 20,000 + Rs. 22,500 = Rs. 42,500. With cess = Rs. 44,200.

Old Regime (with deductions ~ Rs. 3L): Assume 80C (1.5L) + 80D (25K) + HRA (75K) + Std deduction (50K) = Rs. 3L deductions. Taxable = Rs. 7,00,000. Tax = (2.5L × 5%) + (2L × 20%) = Rs. 12,500 + Rs. 40,000 = Rs. 52,500. With cess = Rs. 54,600.

Verdict: New regime saves approximately Rs. 10,400. Old regime 10L pe tab hi better hai jab total deductions Rs. 3.75 lakh+ ho.

Gross Salary: Rs. 15,00,000

New Regime: Taxable = 15L - 75K = Rs. 14,25,000. Tax = (4L × 5%) + (3L × 10%) + (2L × 15%) + (2.25L × 20%) = 20K + 30K + 30K + 45K = Rs. 1,25,000. With cess = Rs. 1,30,000.

Old Regime (with deductions ~ Rs. 4.5L): Assume 80C (1.5L) + 80D (50K) + HRA (1.5L) + Home loan (50K) + Std deduction (50K) = Rs. 4.5L. Taxable = Rs. 10,50,000. Tax = (2.5L × 5%) + (5L × 20%) + (50K × 30%) = 12,500 + 1,00,000 + 15,000 = Rs. 1,27,500. With cess = Rs. 1,32,600.

Verdict: Almost equal! 15L pe old regime sirf tab significantly better hai jab deductions Rs. 5L+ cross kare (strong HRA + home loan + maxed 80C + 80D).

Gross Salary: Rs. 20,00,000

New Regime: Taxable = 20L - 75K = Rs. 19,25,000. Tax = 20K + 30K + 30K + 60K + (4.25L × 30%) = Rs. 2,67,500. With cess = Rs. 2,78,200.

Old Regime (with deductions ~ Rs. 5L): Taxable = Rs. 14,50,000. Tax = 12,500 + 1,00,000 + (4.5L × 30%) = Rs. 2,47,500. With cess = Rs. 2,57,400.

Verdict: Old regime Rs. 20,800 save karta hai at 20L — but sirf tab jab Rs. 5L+ deductions genuinely claim kar rahe ho.

Gross Salary: Rs. 30,00,000

New Regime: Taxable = 30L - 75K = Rs. 29,25,000. Tax = 20K + 30K + 30K + 60K + (14.25L × 30%) = Rs. 5,67,500. With cess = Rs. 5,90,200.

Old Regime (with deductions ~ Rs. 6L): Taxable = Rs. 23,50,000. Tax = 12,500 + 1,00,000 + (13.5L × 30%) = Rs. 5,17,500. With cess = Rs. 5,38,200.

Verdict: Old regime clearly better at 30L — saves Rs. 52,000. Higher income pe old regime ka advantage badhta hai, assuming substantial deductions claim kar rahe ho.

Who Should Pick Which Regime?

New Regime Best Hai Agar:

  • Aap recently job start ki hai aur abhi investments zyada nahi hain
  • Rent nahi dete ya HRA claim nahi kar sakte
  • Home loan nahi hai
  • Total deductions Rs. 3.5-4 lakh se kam hain
  • Simplicity chahiye — koi tax planning tension nahi
  • Income Rs. 7-12 lakh range mein hai

Old Regime Best Hai Agar:

  • HRA claim karte ho (metro city mein rent pay karte ho)
  • Home loan EMI pay kar rahe ho (Section 24b interest deduction)
  • 80C fully utilize karte ho (PPF, ELSS, EPF combine mein 1.5L easily)
  • Parents ke liye health insurance premium pay karte ho (80D)
  • NPS mein additional 50K invest karte ho (80CCD(1B))
  • Total deductions Rs. 4-5 lakh se zyada hain
  • Income Rs. 15L+ hai with significant deductions

Quick rule of thumb: Agar aapki total deductions (80C + 80D + HRA + Home Loan Interest + others) Rs. 3.75 lakh se zyada hain toh old regime generally better hai. Isse kam hain toh new regime.

How to Switch Between Regimes

  • Salaried employees: Har financial year ke start mein employer ko intimation de sakte ho. ITR file karte waqt bhi switch kar sakte ho — final choice ITR mein hoti hai.
  • Business income wale: Agar business/professional income hai toh switch karne pe restrictions hain. Ek baar old regime choose ki toh ek hi baar lifetime mein switch back kar sakte ho.
  • Default regime: Agar kuch nahi choose karte toh new regime automatically apply hoti hai.
  • Form 10-IE: Old regime choose karne ke liye Form 10-IE file karna padta hai ITR se pehle ya saath mein.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Deductions overestimate karna: Log sochte hain ki unke paas bahut deductions hain, but actually calculate karne pe 2-3 lakh hi nikalta hai. Honestly calculate karo — humara Salary Calculator help kar sakta hai.
  • HRA without rent receipts: HRA claim karna hai toh rent receipts aur landlord PAN (if rent above 1L/year) zaroori hai. Bina documentation ke claim risky hai.
  • Section 80C last-minute investments: March end mein jaldi mein investment karna sirf tax save karne ke liye — better hai ki saal bhar planned investments karo through SIP. Humara SIP Calculator use karo planning ke liye.
  • Forgetting cess: 4% Health and Education Cess tax amount pe lagta hai. Logo calculated tax batate hain but cess bhool jaate hain.
  • Not comparing every year: Aapki situation har saal change hoti hai — naya home loan, parents ka insurance, rent change. Har saal fresh comparison karo.
  • Ignoring employer NPS: Employer NPS contribution (80CCD(2)) dono regimes mein available hai. Agar employer offer karta hai toh zaroor utilize karo — yeh extra benefit hai above 80C limit.
  • EMI vs interest confusion: Home loan mein sirf interest portion deductible hai (Section 24b), principal 80C mein aata hai. Total EMI nahi, sirf interest component.

Loan ke baare mein zyada detail chahiye toh hamara EMI Calculator use karo — interest aur principal breakup clearly dikhata hai.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: Kya har saal regime switch kar sakte hain?

Haan, salaried employees har financial year mein regime change kar sakte hain. Business income wale ke liye restrictions hain — ek baar old se new gaye toh sirf ek baar wapas aa sakte ho. Salaried log freely switch kar sakte hain ITR file karte waqt.

Q2: New regime mein EPF contribution ka kya hota hai?

EPF contribution continue rehta hai (employer deduct karta hai), lekin new regime mein aap iske liye 80C deduction claim nahi kar sakte. EPF pe interest aur maturity amount tax rules follow karte hain irrespective of regime chosen for that year.

Q3: Rs. 7 lakh rebate ka matlab kya hai exactly?

New regime mein agar aapki taxable income (after standard deduction) Rs. 7,00,000 se kam ya equal hai, toh calculated tax pe Section 87A rebate milti hai aur effective tax zero ho jaata hai. Matlab Rs. 7.75 lakh tak gross salary pe zero tax possible hai new regime mein (75K standard deduction apply hone ke baad).

Q4: Old regime mein maximum kitna save ho sakta hai?

Theoretically agar aap sab deductions maximize karo — 80C (1.5L) + 80CCD(1B) (50K) + 80D (75K-1L) + HRA (varies, 2-4L in metros) + Home Loan Interest (2L) + Standard Deduction (50K) — toh Rs. 7-8 lakh+ deductions possible hain. High income pe isse Rs. 2-3 lakh+ tax saving ho sakti hai compared to new regime.

Q5: NPS ke tax benefits dono regimes mein milte hain?

Employer ka NPS contribution (Section 80CCD(2)) dono regimes mein deductible hai — yeh unique advantage hai. Lekin employee ka additional 50K contribution (80CCD(1B)) sirf old regime mein deductible hai. So agar employer NPS offer karta hai, both regimes mein benefit milega.

Q6: Student ya fresher ke liye kaun sa regime best hai?

Freshers ke liye almost always new regime better hai. Jab salary low hai (7-10L) aur abhi investments start nahi kiye — koi home loan nahi, HRA minimal hai — toh new regime mein lower tax lagta hai without any planning needed. Jaise income badhe aur deductions build ho, tab old regime pe switch karne ka evaluate karo.
