💰 Income Tax Calculator — Old vs New Regime (FY 2025-26)

April 2026 · 4 min read

Confused between old and new tax regime? Here is a simple comparison to help you decide which one saves more tax.

New Regime Tax Slabs (FY 2025-26)

  • Up to Rs. 3,00,000 — Nil
  • Rs. 3,00,001 to 7,00,000 — 5%
  • Rs. 7,00,001 to 10,00,000 — 10%
  • Rs. 10,00,001 to 12,00,000 — 15%
  • Rs. 12,00,001 to 15,00,000 — 20%
  • Above Rs. 15,00,000 — 30%

Standard deduction: Rs. 75,000. Rebate up to Rs. 7,00,000 taxable income.

Old Regime Tax Slabs

  • Up to Rs. 2,50,000 — Nil
  • Rs. 2,50,001 to 5,00,000 — 5%
  • Rs. 5,00,001 to 10,00,000 — 20%
  • Above Rs. 10,00,000 — 30%

Standard deduction: Rs. 50,000. Allows deductions under 80C (Rs. 1.5 lakh), 80D, HRA, etc.

Which Regime to Choose?

  • New regime is better if you have few deductions (less than Rs. 3-4 lakh)
  • Old regime is better if you have significant deductions (80C, 80D, HRA, home loan)
