📊 Free Syllabus Tracker for Government Exams — UPSC, SSC, Banking

June 2026 · 4 min read

Government exam syllabi are massive. UPSC has 9 papers covering everything from history to ethics. SSC CGL covers quantitative aptitude, reasoning, English, and GK. Without tracking what you have covered and what remains, you will either waste time re-reading completed topics or miss important ones entirely. A syllabus tracker solves this.

Why You Need a Syllabus Tracker

  • Visual progress: See exactly what percentage of syllabus is done
  • Identify gaps: Spot untouched topics before it is too late
  • Stay motivated: Watching progress bars fill up keeps you going
  • Plan revision: Know which topics need second or third revision
  • Time management: Allocate remaining days to pending topics proportionally

How to Use the Syllabus Tracker

  1. Open the FreeToolHubs Syllabus Tracker
  2. Create a new tracker (e.g., "UPSC Prelims 2026")
  3. Add subjects and topics from the official syllabus
  4. Mark topics as "Not Started", "In Progress", or "Completed"
  5. Update daily as you finish topics
  6. Review the progress dashboard to plan next steps

Sample Syllabus Breakdown

UPSC Prelims GS Paper 1

  • History — Ancient, Medieval, Modern, Art & Culture
  • Geography — Physical, Indian, World
  • Polity — Constitution, Governance, Panchayati Raj
  • Economy — Basics, Budget, Banking, Agriculture
  • Science — Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Space
  • Environment — Ecology, Biodiversity, Climate Change
  • Current Affairs — Last 12 months

SSC CGL Tier-1

  • Quantitative Aptitude — 25 topics
  • Reasoning — 20 topics
  • English — Grammar, Vocabulary, Comprehension
  • General Awareness — Static GK, Current Affairs

Tips for Effective Syllabus Tracking

  • Break into small topics: "Indian History" is too broad — split into "Maurya Empire", "Mughal Period", etc.
  • Update daily: Do not let tracking become a weekly chore
  • Mark revision separately: First reading ≠ revision-ready
  • Set weekly targets: "Complete 5 topics this week" is actionable
  • Review on Sundays: Weekly review helps adjust your plan

Combine with Other Study Tools

Use the Pomodoro Timer to stay focused while studying each topic. Create Flashcards for completed topics to aid revision. Test yourself with the Quiz Maker before marking a topic as fully done.

Works Offline — No Data Lost

Your progress is saved in your browser's local storage. No account needed, no data sent to servers. Even if you lose internet, your tracker stays intact. Works perfectly on mobile phones — update your progress right after finishing a study session.
