✍️ How to Make AI Text Sound Human

April 2026 · 3 min read

AI tools like ChatGPT write well, but the text often sounds robotic and formal. Teachers and plagiarism checkers can spot AI-written text. Here is how to make it sound more natural.

Signs of AI Text

  • No contractions (writes "do not" instead of "don't")
  • Every sentence is similar length
  • Overly formal phrases ("utilize" instead of "use")
  • Repetitive structure
  • No personal touch or casual expressions

How to Humanize

  1. Open AI Text Humanizer
  2. Paste your AI-generated text
  3. Choose tone: Casual, Professional, or Academic
  4. Select humanization level: Light, Medium, or Heavy
  5. Click Humanize and copy the result

The tool adds contractions, replaces formal phrases, varies sentence starters, and adjusts tone. It shows what percentage of text was changed.
