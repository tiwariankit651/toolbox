📸 Photo Background White Kaise Kare Free — Complete Guide

July 2026 · 8 min read

Passport banwana ho, visa application fill karni ho, ya kisi government form ke liye photo chahiye — almost har jagah white background photo maangi jaati hai. Studio jaao toh ₹100-200 lagta hai ek simple photo ke liye, aur agar photo reject ho gayi toh dobara jaana padta hai. Lekin kya aap ghar baithe apni photo ka background white kar sakte ho? Bilkul haan! Is guide mein main aapko step-by-step bataunga ki photo background white kaise kare — free mein, bina kisi software ke.

White Background Photo Kahan-Kahan Chahiye?

India mein almost har official document ke liye white background wali photo mandatory hai:

  • Indian Passport — white ya off-white background mandatory (3.5 × 4.5 cm)
  • US/UK/Schengen Visa — strictly white background (2 × 2 inch for US)
  • Aadhaar Card — white/light background preferred
  • PAN Card Application — white background required
  • Job Applications — especially IT companies, banks, government jobs
  • College Admission Forms — DU, JEE, NEET sab white background maangti hain
  • Driving License — RTO mein white background photo chahiye

Basically agar aap India mein koi bhi official kaam kar rahe ho, toh white background photo aapke paas honi hi chahiye.

DIY Method — Ghar Pe White Background Photo Kaise Lein

Agar aapke paas thoda time hai aur achhi lighting available hai, toh ghar pe hi white background photo le sakte ho:

Option 1: White Wall Use Karo

Sabse simple method. Apne ghar ki white wall ke saamne khade ho jao. Lekin dhyan rakho:

  • Wall par koi stain, nail marks, ya shadow na ho
  • Wall se 2-3 feet door khade raho (taaki shadow na pade)
  • Natural light (window ke saamne) best hai — tube light se uneven lighting hoti hai

Option 2: White Bedsheet/Chart Paper

Agar wall white nahi hai, toh ek white bedsheet ya white chart paper backdrop ki tarah use karo. Iron karna mat bhoolna — creases photo mein clearly dikhte hain aur rejection ka reason ban sakte hain.

Lighting Tips

  • Subah 9-11 baje ya dopahar 2-4 baje window ke paas best natural light milti hai
  • Direct sunlight avoid karo — harsh shadows aati hain
  • Face pe even lighting honi chahiye — ek side dark nahi hona chahiye
  • Phone ka flash OFF rakho — red eye aur unnatural shadows create hota hai

Photo Size Requirements — Document Wise

White background ke saath correct size bhi important hai. Wrong size = rejection. Yeh hain common requirements:

  • Indian Passport: 3.5 × 4.5 cm (350 × 450 px at 300 DPI)
  • US Visa: 2 × 2 inch (600 × 600 px)
  • UK Visa: 3.5 × 4.5 cm
  • Schengen Visa: 3.5 × 4.5 cm
  • PAN Card: 3.5 × 2.5 cm
  • Aadhaar Update: 3.5 × 4.5 cm (file size 2KB-1MB)
  • SSC/UPSC Forms: varies — usually 3.5 × 4.5 cm, file size under 300KB

Photo ka size adjust karne ke liye humara Image Resizer Tool use karo — exact pixel dimensions set kar sakte ho.

Common Rejection Reasons — In Galtiyon Se Bachein

Bahut logon ki photos reject hoti hain in reasons se:

  • Shadow behind head — sabse common reason. Wall se door khade ho aur proper lighting use karo
  • Uneven white background — ek corner cream aur ek corner bright white — reject!
  • Face too small/too large — passport photo mein face 70-80% frame cover karna chahiye
  • Blurry photo — camera steady rakho, ya timer use karo
  • Glasses reflection — specs hatake photo lo (most embassies prefer no glasses)
  • Off-white background — cream, beige, light grey accept nahi hoti visa applications mein
  • File size too large/small — government portals ki specific limits hoti hain

Complex Background Hai? Yeh Tips Follow Karo

Agar aapki photo ka background bahut complex hai (trees, crowd, patterns), toh automatic tool ko thodi difficulty ho sakti hai. Aise mein:

  • High contrast wali photo use karo — jahan aap clearly background se alag dikho
  • Plain clothes pehno — white shirt + white background = tool confused ho sakta hai
  • Hair ke edges — bahut curly ya flyaway hair mein edges rough aa sakte hain
  • Best results ke liye solid color background wali photo se start karo (green, blue, grey)

Agar tool perfect result nahi de raha, toh try karo ki original photo better lighting mein ek plain wall ke saamne lo.

File Size Compress Karna Na Bhoolein

Government portals ki strict file size limits hoti hain. Background change karne ke baad agar file size zyada hai, toh humara Image Compressor use karo. Quality maintain karte hue file size 50-80% tak reduce ho jaata hai.

Quick Checklist — Perfect White Background Photo

  • ✅ Pure white background (no cream, no grey)
  • ✅ No shadows behind head or body
  • ✅ Face properly centered, 70-80% frame coverage
  • ✅ Even lighting on face — no dark side
  • ✅ Correct dimensions as per document requirement
  • ✅ File size within limits (usually under 300KB-1MB)
  • ✅ Eyes open, neutral expression, mouth closed
  • ✅ No glasses reflection, no hat/cap
