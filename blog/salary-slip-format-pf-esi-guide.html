🧾 Salary Slip Ka Format — Aur Har Deduction Ka Asli Hisaab

October 2026 · 10 min read

Salary slip dekhte waqt do sawaal sabse zyada aate hain. Pehla: "PF 12% hai toh mera sirf ₹1,800 kyun katta hai, jabki mera basic ₹30,000 hai?" Doosra: "pichhle saal ESI katta tha, ab nahi katta — kyun?"

Dono ka jawab ceilings mein hai — wo limits jo statutory rules mein likhi hain par slip par nahi dikhti. Is guide mein har deduction ka asli hisaab hai, numbers ke saath.

Slip Mein Kya-Kya Hona Chahiye

Ek proper salary slip ke paanch hisse hote hain:

  1. Company header — naam, address, aur kis mahine ki slip hai
  2. Employee details — naam, ID, designation, aur kitne din ka payment
  3. Earnings — basic, HRA, conveyance, special allowance, aur total gross
  4. Deductions — PF, ESI, professional tax, TDS, advance, aur total
  5. Net pay — figures mein aur words mein bhi

"Amount in words" wala hissa log skip kar dete hain, par wahi slip ko ek document banata hai printout ke bajaye. Bank aur visa office isko dekhte hain.

PF — ₹1,800 Ka Rahasya

Rule ye hai: employee contribution = basic salary ka 12%.

Par usme ek shart hai jo aksar nahi batai jaati: PF ke liye basic salary ₹15,000 par cap hoti hai.

Basic ₹12,000 → 12% of 12,000 = ₹1,440
Basic ₹15,000 → 12% of 15,000 = ₹1,800
Basic ₹30,000 → 12% of 15,000 = ₹1,800
Basic ₹80,000 → 12% of 15,000 = ₹1,800

Toh ₹1,800 statutory maximum hai. Basic ₹15,000 cross karne ke baad PF nahi badhta.

💡 Kuch companies poore basic par PF contribute karti hain, ceiling ignore karke. Tab ₹30,000 basic par ₹3,600 katega. Ye company ki policy hai, rule nahi — isliye slip par ₹1,800 se zyada dikhe toh wo galti nahi hai.

Employer Ka Hissa Slip Par Kyun Nahi Hota

Employer bhi 12% contribute karta hai. Par wo aapki salary se nahi katta — wo employer ka apna cost hai. Isliye wo deduction mein nahi dikhta.

Haan, wo CTC mein dikhta hai. Yahi ek badi wajah hai ki CTC aur in-hand mein itna fark hota hai.

ESI — ₹21,000 Par Band

ESI (Employees' State Insurance) medical benefits deta hai. Deduction rate 0.75% of gross hai, par sirf tab tak jab tak gross monthly wages ₹21,000 ya kam hon.

Gross ₹18,000 → 0.75% = ₹135
Gross ₹21,000 → 0.75% = ₹158
Gross ₹22,000 → ₹0 — limit cross, ESI se bahar

Toh agar aapko increment mila aur gross ₹21,000 cross kar gaya, ESI deduction gayab ho jaata hai. In-hand thoda badh jaata hai, par ESI ke medical benefits bhi chale jaate hain.

Ek detail: ESI eligibility contribution period ke hisaab se chalti hai (April-September, October-March). Beech mein limit cross karne par aksar us period ke end tak coverage continue rehti hai.

Employer ka hissa 3.25% hai — wo bhi aapki salary se nahi katta.

Professional Tax — State Ka Khel

Ye wo deduction hai jiske baare mein koi generalisation kaam nahi karta.

  • Kai states mein lagta hi nahi — Delhi, UP, Haryana, Rajasthan jaise
  • Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu jaise states mein lagta hai
  • Amount salary slab par depend karta hai, aur state ke hisaab se different slabs hain
  • ₹200 mahina mid-level salaries par aam hai, aur annual cap ₹2,500 hota hai

Toh apne state ka slab check karna chahiye, ya HR se puchhna chahiye. ₹200 maan lena galat ho sakta hai.

CTC Vs Gross Vs In-Hand — Teen Alag Numbers

Ye confusion job offer ke time sabse mehengi padti hai.

Kya Isme kya hota hai Slip par?
CTC Gross + employer PF + gratuity provision + insurance premium + bonus provision Nahi
Gross Basic + HRA + conveyance + allowances — earnings ka total Haan
Net / in-hand Gross − PF − ESI − PT − TDS − advance Haan

Ek example — ₹6 lakh CTC:

CTC (saal)          = ₹6,00,000 → ₹50,000/month
− employer PF       = −₹1,800
− gratuity provision = −₹960 (lagbhag)
Gross              = ₹47,240
− employee PF       = −₹1,800
− professional tax   = −₹200
− TDS               = −₹1,500 (lagbhag)
In-hand            = ₹43,740

₹50,000 ka offer, ₹43,740 haath mein. ₹6,260 ka fark — aur isme kuch bhi galat nahi hua, bas CTC aur in-hand do alag cheezein hain.

HRA Ko Basic Se Jodo

Ek practical baat: aapka basic kitna hai wo HRA exemption ko directly affect karta hai, kyunki exemption ka hisaab basic par hota hai.

Kuch companies basic ko kam rakhti hain aur allowances badha deti hain, taaki PF cost kam rahe. Isse aapka PF bhi kam hota hai (retirement saving kam) aur HRA exemption bhi limited ho jaata hai. Offer compare karte waqt basic ka percentage dekhna chahiye, sirf CTC nahi.

HRA exemption ka poora formula yahan hai, aur calculator bhi.

Chhoti Company Ke Liye — Slip Kaise Banayein

Agar aap employer hain aur 5-50 log hain, toh payroll software ka subscription aksar zaroorat se zyada hota hai. Minimum ye karo:

  1. Ek spreadsheet rakho — naam, ID, basic, HRA, allowances, deductions
  2. PF aur ESI rules follow karo — ceilings ke saath, warna compliance issue banta hai
  3. Har mahine slip do, chahe ek hi page ki ho. Employee ko loan ya visa ke liye ye chahiye hoti hai
  4. Copy rakho — audit aur disputes mein kaam aati hai

Humara salary slip generator us spreadsheet ko seedha le leta hai — CSV paste karo ya file drop karo, aur poori team ki slips ek PDF mein ya alag-alag files ki ZIP mein mil jaati hain. PF aur ESI apne aap calculate hote hain ceilings ke saath.

Employee Ke Liye — Slip Mein Kya Check Karo

  • ☐ Basic sahi hai? — ye PF aur HRA dono ko affect karta hai
  • ☐ PF ₹1,800 se zyada nahi (unless company poore basic par de rahi hai)
  • ☐ ESI — gross ₹21,000 se upar hai toh ye nahi hona chahiye
  • ☐ Professional tax — aapke state mein lagta hai?
  • ☐ Days paid — leave without pay hui toh wo reflect ho raha hai?
  • ☐ Net pay bank credit se match karta hai?
  • ☐ Amount in words hai — bank isko dekhta hai

Koi cheez mismatch kare toh HR se puchho. Slip ki galti Form 16 tak chali jaati hai, aur phir ITR mein.

FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions

PF 12 percent hai toh mera ₹1,800 hi kyun katta hai?

Kyunki PF ke liye basic salary ₹15,000 par cap hoti hai. 12% of 15,000 = ₹1,800, aur yahi maximum statutory contribution hai. Basic ₹20,000 ho ya ₹80,000, contribution ₹1,800 hi rahega jab tak employer poore basic par voluntarily contribute na kare.

ESI kab tak katta hai?

ESI tab tak lagta hai jab tak gross monthly wages ₹21,000 ya usse kam hon. Employee ka hissa 0.75% hota hai. Gross ₹21,000 se upar jaane par employee ESI se bahar ho jaata hai aur deduction band ho jaata hai.

Professional tax kitna hota hai?

Ye state ke hisaab se alag hota hai aur kai states mein lagta hi nahi. Maharashtra aur Karnataka mein mid-level salaries par ₹200 mahina aam hai. Apne state ka slab check karna chahiye, maan lena nahi chahiye.

CTC aur gross salary mein kya fark hai?

CTC mein wo cheezein bhi hoti hain jo aapke slip par nahi aati — employer ka PF contribution, gratuity provision, insurance premium. Gross wo hai jo slip par earnings total dikhata hai. Isliye ₹6 lakh CTC ka matlab ₹50,000 mahina in-hand nahi hota.

Salary slip legally valid hone ke liye kya chahiye?

Slip ki validity employer ke issue karne aur figures sahi hone se aati hai, kisi software se nahi. Company name, employee details, month, earnings aur deductions ka breakup, net pay aur amount in words hona chahiye. Kai jagah signature ya stamp bhi expect kiya jaata hai.

Loan ke liye kitne salary slips chahiye hote hain?

Zyadatar banks teen mahine ki latest slips maangte hain, saath mein 6 mahine ka bank statement aur Form 16. Kuch lenders 6 mahine ki slips maangte hain, khaas kar jab income variable ho.

Summary

Do ceilings yaad rakho aur aadhi confusion khatam. PF ka basic ₹15,000 par cap hota hai, isliye employee contribution ₹1,800 par ruk jaata hai. ESI gross ₹21,000 tak hi lagta hai, uske baad band. Teesri baat: CTC, gross aur in-hand teen alag numbers hain, aur offer letter wala number in-hand nahi hota.
