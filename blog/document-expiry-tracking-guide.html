📅 Document Expiry Kaise Track Kare — Kaunsa Kitne Din Pehle

October 2026 · 9 min read

Expiry dates isliye miss hoti hain ki wo wahan likhi hoti hain jahan koi dekhta nahi — ek purani file mein, ya phone ke notes mein. Spreadsheet bhi tab hi kaam karti hai jab aap usko kholo.

Asli solution ye nahi hai ki aap list banayein. Asli solution ye hai ki reminder aapke paas khud aaye. Is guide mein ye hai ki kaunsa document kitne din pehle renew karna chahiye, kyun, aur reminder set karne ka sahi tareeka.

Sabse Badi Galti: Expiry Date Par Reminder Lagana

Log aksar expiry date par hi reminder lagate hain. Tab tak bahut der ho chuki hoti hai, kyunki renewal mein time lagta hai.

Sahi tareeka ye hai: reminder = expiry date − renewal mein lagne wala time − buffer.

Aur ye time har document ke liye bilkul alag hai. Passport mein hafte lag sakte hain, PUC same day ho jaata hai.

Passport Ka 6-Month Rule — Sabse Mehengi Galti

Ye wo rule hai jo log airport par pata karte hain.

Bahut se desh entry ke waqt passport mein kam se kam 6 mahine ki validity maangte hain. Matlab aapka passport agle 5 mahine mein expire ho raha hai toh wo aaj hi travel ke liye bekaar hai — chahe technically valid ho.

Toh effective expiry date wo nahi hai jo passport par likhi hai. Wo 6 mahine pehle hai. Aur uske upar renewal ka time jodo.

💡 Isliye passport ka reminder 180 din par hona chahiye — kam se kam. Agar aap travel plan karte hain toh 240 din behtar hai, kyunki tab aapke paas renewal ke liye bhi time rahega.

Insurance Lapse — Sirf Fine Nahi, Poori Liability

Vehicle insurance lapse hone par log sirf fine ki baat karte hain. Asli risk bahut bada hai.

  • Third-party insurance ke bina gaadi chalana illegal hai — Motor Vehicles Act ke tahat
  • Accident hone par poori liability aapki — kisi ko chot lagi ya doosri gaadi ka damage hua, toh sab aapki jeb se
  • No-claim bonus chala jaata hai — saalon ka accumulated discount, ek lapse mein khatam
  • Renewal par fresh inspection aksar chahiye hota hai, aur premium zyada lag sakta hai

Health insurance mein lapse ka apna nuksaan hai: pre-existing conditions ka waiting period dobara shuru ho sakta hai. Jo coverage aapne 3 saal wait karke kamaya tha, wo reset ho jaata hai.

Warranty — Ye Paisa Hai Jo Log Chhod Dete Hain

Fridge, AC, washing machine, laptop — sab ki warranty hoti hai, aur zyadatar log usko use kiye bina expire hone dete hain.

Do cheezein karo:

  1. Warranty expiry se 30 din pehle reminder lagao — aur us waqt device ko dhyan se check karo. Koi chhoti problem hai toh abhi claim karo, baad mein paisa lagega
  2. Extended warranty ka decision bhi usi waqt lena hota hai — expire hone ke baad option nahi milta

AMC (annual maintenance contract) mein bhi same baat hai — expire hone se pehle ek service le lo, kyunki wo paid ho chuki hai.

Reminder Set Karne Ka Sahi Tareeka

Teen options hain, aur teeno equal nahi hain.

1. Phone Notes — Kaam Nahi Karta

Note likhne se koi notification nahi aati. Aapko khud note kholna padta hai, aur wahi problem hai jo solve karni thi.

2. Reminder App — Kaam Karta Hai Par Data Jaata Hai

Reminder apps notification dete hain. Par zyadatar apps aapka data apne server par rakhte hain. Agar aap policy number, licence number ya passport number daal rahe hain, toh wo kisi aur ke system par chala gaya.

3. Calendar — Sabse Behtar

Calendar pehle se aapke phone par hai, notification deta hai, aur aapke Google/Apple account se tied hai — kisi naye third party ke paas nahi.

Sabse safe tareeka ye hai: ek .ics file banao aur calendar mein import kar do. .ics ek standard calendar format hai jisme event aur alarm dono hote hain. Import karne ke baad reminder aapke calendar ka hissa ban jaata hai, aur original list kahin bhejne ki zaroorat nahi.

Humara document expiry tracker yahi karta hai — list browser mein rakhta hai, aur .ics file export kar deta hai jisme har document ka event aur aapke chune hue lead time ka alarm hota hai. Google Calendar, Apple Calendar aur Outlook teeno isko padhte hain.

Ek Zaroori Baat: Backup Lo

Jab koi tool data aapke browser mein rakhta hai, toh wo sirf us browser mein hota hai. Faayda: kuch upload nahi hota. Nuksaan: agar aap site data clear kar do, doosra browser use karo, ya phone badlo — list chali gayi.

Isliye backup optional nahi hai. JSON backup download karke use Drive ya email mein rakho. Restore karne par list exactly wapas aa jaati hai.

⚠️ Ye trade-off samajhna zaroori hai: data aapke paas rehta hai, par uski zimmedari bhi aapki hai. Jo tools cloud sync dete hain wo convenience dete hain — badle mein aapka data unke server par hota hai. Policy numbers ke liye local rakhna behtar hai, bas backup lena yaad rahe.

Ek Practical Setup — 10 Minute Ka Kaam

  1. Saare documents nikaalo — passport, licence, RC, insurance policies, PUC, warranty cards, rent agreement
  2. Expiry dates list karo — ek baar, poora
  3. Lead time set karo — upar ki table ke hisaab se
  4. .ics export karke calendar mein import karo — ye step reminder ko real banata hai
  5. JSON backup download karke safe jagah rakho
  6. Saal mein ek baar review karo — renew ki hui dates update karo

Ek baar set karne ke baad ye apne aap chalta hai. Saal bhar notifications aate rehte hain.

Family Ke Liye

Agar aap ghar ke documents sambhalte hain toh ek list mein sabke documents rakho, naam ke saath — "Passport — Asha", "Passport — Papa". Tracker mein naam field isi liye free text hai.

Bachhon ke passport ka ek extra point: minor passport sirf 5 saal ka hota hai (ya 18 saal ki umar tak, jo pehle aaye), adults ke 10 saal ke mukable. Toh wo zyada jaldi expire hote hain aur log bhool jaate hain.

FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions

Passport ka 6 month rule kya hai?

Bahut se desh entry ke waqt passport mein kam se kam 6 mahine ki validity maangte hain. Iska matlab hai ki passport expire hone se 6 mahine pehle hi travel ke liye bekaar ho jaata hai. Isliye reminder 6 mahine se zyada pehle hona chahiye, kyunki renewal mein bhi time lagta hai.

Vehicle insurance lapse ho gaya toh kya hota hai?

Third-party insurance ke bina gaadi chalana Motor Vehicles Act ke tahat illegal hai aur fine lagta hai. Isse bada risk ye hai ki accident hone par poori liability aapki ho jaati hai. Lapse ke baad renewal par aksar fresh inspection bhi chahiye hota hai aur no-claim bonus chala jaata hai.

Driving licence expire hone ke baad kitna time milta hai?

Licence expiry ke baad grace period hota hai jisme normal renewal ho jaata hai. Us period ke baad late fee lagti hai aur bahut der ho jaane par dobara test dena pad sakta hai. Expired licence par gaadi chalana invalid licence maana jaata hai.

PUC certificate kitne din ka hota hai?

Naye vehicles ka PUC aksar ek saal ka hota hai, uske baad zyadatar petrol aur diesel vehicles ke liye 6 mahine ka. Ye same-day kaam hai, isliye iska reminder sirf 15 din pehle kaafi hai.

Reminder app use karun ya calendar?

Calendar behtar hai do wajah se. Pehla, wo pehle se aapke phone par hai aur notification dikhata hai. Doosra, document numbers kisi third-party app ke server par nahi jaate. Ek .ics file export karke calendar mein import kar dena sabse safe tareeka hai.

Document numbers online tool mein daalna safe hai?

Depend karta hai ki tool server par upload karta hai ya browser mein rakhta hai. Browser-based tool mein data aapke device par rehta hai aur wo offline bhi chalta hai. Policy aur licence numbers ke liye ye farak bahut matter karta hai.

Summary

Teen baatein. Reminder expiry date par nahi, renewal time se pehle lagao — passport 180 din, insurance 30, PUC 15. Aur calendar mein import karo, list bana kar chhod na do — jo list aapko kholni pade wo reminder nahi hai.
