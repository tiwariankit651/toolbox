🏦 EMI Calculator — Home Loan & Car Loan EMI Kaise Calculate Kare

July 2026 · 7 min read

Loan lena hai — ghar ke liye, car ke liye, ya personal need ke liye — lekin pehle yeh samajhna zaroori hai ki EMI kitni aayegi. Bahut log loan le lete hain bina calculate kiye, aur phir monthly budget tight ho jaata hai. Is guide mein hum EMI formula simply samjhayenge, real examples denge, aur batayenge ki kaise aap lakhs rupees interest mein bacha sakte ho.

EMI Kya Hai?

EMI = Equated Monthly Installment. Yeh woh fixed amount hai jo aap har month bank ko pay karte ho jab tak loan poora nahi ho jaata. Har EMI mein do parts hote hain:

  • Principal — Actual loan amount ka ek hissa jo repay ho raha hai
  • Interest — Bank ka profit jo aap pay kar rahe ho

Shuru mein EMI ka zyada hissa interest hota hai. Jaise jaise time jaata hai, principal ka portion badhta jaata hai.

EMI Formula — Simply Explained

EMI calculate karne ka formula hai:

EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / [(1+r)^n - 1]

Jahan:

  • P = Principal (loan amount)
  • r = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • n = Total months (years × 12)

Real Example: ₹20 Lakh Home Loan

Let's calculate:

  • Loan Amount (P): ₹20,00,000
  • Interest Rate: 8.5% per annum → Monthly rate (r) = 8.5/12/100 = 0.00708
  • Tenure: 20 years → Months (n) = 240

EMI = ₹17,356 per month

20 saal mein total payment: ₹17,356 × 240 = ₹41,65,440

Interest paid: ₹41,65,440 - ₹20,00,000 = ₹21,65,440 😱

Haan, aapne sahi padha — ₹20 lakh ke loan pe aap ₹21+ lakh sirf interest de rahe ho! Isliye EMI calculate karna aur prepayment plan banana bahut important hai.

EMI Ko Kya Affect Karta Hai?

1. Loan Amount (Principal)

Zyada loan = zyada EMI. Simple. Isliye down payment jitna zyada ho utna better.

2. Interest Rate

Sirf 0.5% ka fark bhi lakhs ka difference bana deta hai:

  • ₹20L at 8.0% for 20 years → EMI: ₹16,729 | Total Interest: ₹20,15,000
  • ₹20L at 8.5% for 20 years → EMI: ₹17,356 | Total Interest: ₹21,65,440
  • ₹20L at 9.0% for 20 years → EMI: ₹17,995 | Total Interest: ₹23,18,800

0.5% ka fark = ₹1.5 lakh+ extra interest! Isliye lowest rate ke liye negotiate karo.

3. Loan Tenure

Longer tenure = lower EMI but MUCH more interest:

  • ₹20L at 8.5% for 15 years → EMI: ₹19,698 | Total Interest: ₹15,45,640
  • ₹20L at 8.5% for 20 years → EMI: ₹17,356 | Total Interest: ₹21,65,440
  • ₹20L at 8.5% for 25 years → EMI: ₹16,054 | Total Interest: ₹28,16,200

15 years vs 25 years mein ₹12.7 lakh ka fark hai interest mein! EMI sirf ₹3,644 zyada hai per month.

Prepayment Benefits — Lakhs Bachao

Prepayment matlab loan ki EMI ke upar extra amount pay karna. Yeh directly principal reduce karta hai. Example:

  • ₹20L home loan, 8.5%, 20 years — Normal total interest: ₹21.65L
  • Agar har saal ₹50,000 extra prepay karo → Loan 14 years mein khatam, interest saved: ~₹7.5 lakh!
  • Agar har saal ₹1,00,000 extra prepay karo → Loan 11 years mein khatam, interest saved: ~₹11 lakh!

Key rules:

  • Home loans mein RBI rule hai — floating rate pe koi prepayment penalty nahi
  • Car/personal loans mein 2-4% prepayment charges lag sakte hain — pehle check karo
  • Jitni jaldi prepay karo utna zyada benefit — early years mein interest component zyada hota hai

Banks EMI Kaise Decide Karte Hain?

Loan approve karte waqt banks yeh dekhte hain:

  • Income — EMI aapki monthly income ka 40-50% se zyada nahi honi chahiye
  • CIBIL Score — 750+ score pe best rates milte hain
  • Existing loans — Agar pehle se EMI chal rahi hai toh new loan ki eligibility kam hogi
  • Job stability — Salaried ko easily milta hai, self-employed ko thoda mushkil
  • Age — Retirement age tak loan khatam hona chahiye

Kab Loan NAHI Lena Chahiye?

Loan ek tool hai, lekin har situation mein sahi nahi hai:

  • Lifestyle expenses ke liye — Vacation, phone, gadgets ke liye personal loan mat lo
  • Jab income unstable ho — Freelancers with irregular income should be extra careful
  • Jab EMI income ka 50%+ ho — Financial stress aayega, emergencies handle nahi ho payengi
  • Jab interest rate bahut high ho — 18% pe personal loan lena matlab double pay karna
  • Depreciating assets ke liye — ₹15L ki car 5 saal mein ₹5L ki ho jayegi, lekin loan abhi bhi chal raha hoga
