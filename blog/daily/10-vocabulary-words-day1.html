📖 10 Must-Know Vocabulary Words — Day 1

English⚡ Easy-Medium🎯 CAT · Banking · SSC · GRE📅 Day 1 · April 2026

Learn these 10 words today. Use them in sentences to remember better.

1. Ubiquitous (yoo-BIK-wi-tus)
Meaning: Present everywhere / सर्वव्यापी
Synonym: Omnipresent, pervasive
Example: "Smartphones have become ubiquitous in modern life."
2. Pragmatic (prag-MAT-ik)
Meaning: Practical, realistic / व्यावहारिक
Synonym: Sensible, down-to-earth
Example: "We need a pragmatic approach to solve this problem."
3. Ephemeral (eh-FEM-er-ul)
Meaning: Short-lived / क्षणभंगुर
Synonym: Transient, fleeting
Example: "Fame on social media is often ephemeral."
4. Resilient (reh-ZIL-ee-ent)
Meaning: Able to recover quickly / लचीला
Synonym: Tough, adaptable
Example: "The Indian economy has proven to be resilient."
5. Ambiguous (am-BIG-yoo-us)
Meaning: Unclear, open to interpretation / अस्पष्ट
Synonym: Vague, equivocal
Example: "The contract language was deliberately ambiguous."
6. Benevolent (beh-NEV-oh-lent)
Meaning: Kind, generous / परोपकारी
Synonym: Charitable, philanthropic
Example: "The benevolent donor funded 100 scholarships."
7. Meticulous (meh-TIK-yoo-lus)
Meaning: Very careful, precise / सावधान
Synonym: Thorough, painstaking
Example: "She is meticulous about checking every detail."
8. Candid (KAN-did)
Meaning: Honest, straightforward / स्पष्टवादी
Synonym: Frank, outspoken
Example: "I appreciate your candid feedback."
9. Exacerbate (ig-ZAS-er-bayt)
Meaning: Make worse / बिगाड़ना
Synonym: Aggravate, worsen
Example: "Pollution exacerbates respiratory problems."
10. Inevitable (in-EV-it-uh-bul)
Meaning: Certain to happen / अनिवार्य
Synonym: Unavoidable, inescapable
Example: "Change is inevitable in any growing organization."

❓ Quick Quiz — Test Yourself!

Q1: "The company took a ___ approach to cut costs." Which word fits?

✅ Pragmatic = practical, realistic approach

Q2: Antonym of "Benevolent"?

✅ Malevolent = wishing harm (opposite of benevolent)
