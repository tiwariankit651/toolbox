🧠 Profit & Loss — Shortcuts That Save 2 Minutes Per Question

Quantitative Aptitude⚡ Medium🎯 CAT · Banking · SSC📅 Day 1 · April 2026

📖 Key Concepts

  • Profit % = (Profit / CP) × 100
  • Loss % = (Loss / CP) × 100
  • SP = CP × (100 + Profit%) / 100
  • CP = SP × 100 / (100 + Profit%)
⚡ Shortcut Trick: If an item is sold at X% profit and another at Y% loss, and SP is same:
Overall Loss % = (X-Y)² / (2×100) × 100
This saves 1-2 minutes in exam!

🧩 Solved Examples

Example 1:

A shopkeeper buys an item for ₹400 and sells at ₹500. Find profit %.

Solution: Profit = 500-400 = ₹100. Profit% = (100/400)×100 = 25%

Example 2:

If CP is ₹800 and loss is 15%, find SP.

Solution: SP = 800 × (100-15)/100 = 800 × 85/100 = ₹680

❓ Practice Questions

Click an option to check your answer:

Q1: A man buys a watch for ₹1200 and sells at ₹1500. Profit % is?

✅ Profit = 300, CP = 1200. Profit% = 300/1200 × 100 = 25%

Q2: SP = ₹450, Loss = 10%. CP is?

✅ CP = SP × 100/(100-Loss%) = 450 × 100/90 = ₹500

Q3: By selling at ₹600, a man gains 20%. To gain 30%, SP should be?

✅ CP = 600×100/120 = 500. New SP = 500×130/100 = ₹650

⚡ Common Mistakes

  • ❌ Calculating profit% on SP instead of CP
  • ❌ Forgetting to convert fractions to percentages
  • ❌ Not reading "successive discounts" carefully

📝 1-Minute Revision

  • Profit/Loss always calculated on CP
  • SP > CP = Profit, SP < CP = Loss
  • Discount is always on Marked Price (MP)
