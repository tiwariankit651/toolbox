🎓 15 Best Free Online Tools Every Student Needs in 2026

April 2026 · 5 min read

As a student, you need tools that are free, fast, and don't require signups. Here are 15 browser-based tools that run on any device — your files never leave your computer.

📝 Academic Tools

1. Typing Speed Test

Measure your WPM (words per minute) and accuracy. Essential for competitive exams like SSC, banking, and data entry tests. Practice with 30-second, 1-minute, or 2-minute tests.

2. CGPA / SGPA Calculator

Calculate your CGPA and SGPA from grades and credits. Supports both 10-point and 4-point grading systems used in Indian universities.

3. Word Counter

Count words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs. Check reading time and readability score — perfect for essays with word limits.

4. Citation Generator

Generate properly formatted citations in APA, MLA, and Chicago styles. Enter source details and get ready-to-use references for your research papers.

5. Text Compare / Plagiarism Checker

Compare two texts side by side and check similarity percentage. Useful for checking your work before submission.

6. Pomodoro Timer

Study for 25 minutes, take a 5-minute break. This proven technique helps you stay focused during long study sessions.

📄 Document Tools

7. PDF Editor

Edit PDFs directly in your browser — add text, draw, highlight, and annotate. Perfect for filling forms and marking up study materials.

8. Image to Text (OCR)

Extract text from photos of handwritten notes, textbook pages, or screenshots. Convert images to editable text instantly.

9. Resume Builder

Create a professional PDF resume by filling a simple form. Clean design that works for internships and job applications.

🔢 Calculators

10. Scientific Calculator

Full scientific calculator with trigonometry, logarithms, powers, roots, and factorial. Works on phone too — no app needed.

11. Age Calculator

Calculate exact age in years, months, and days. Useful for filling application forms that ask for precise age.

12. Unit Converter

Convert between units of length, weight, temperature, and data size. Quick reference for physics and chemistry problems.

🛠️ Utility Tools

13. Image Compressor

Reduce image file size by up to 90% for uploading to portals, sending via email, or attaching to assignments.

14. Quick Notes

A simple notepad that auto-saves to your browser. Jot down ideas, to-do lists, or quick notes during lectures.

15. Text to Speech

Listen to your notes or study material read aloud. Supports multiple languages and adjustable speed — great for revision.

All 43+ tools are free — no signup needed!
