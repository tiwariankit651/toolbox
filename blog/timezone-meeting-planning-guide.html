🌍 Time Zone Meeting Planning — DST, IST Gap Aur Overlap Hours

October 2026 · 10 min read

Time zone ki galti isliye hoti nahi ki arithmetic mushkil hai. Hoti isliye hai ki jawab badal jaata hai aur kisi ko batata nahi.

Aap ek baar nikalte ho ki New York IST se 10 ghante 30 minute peeche hai, likh lete ho, aur mahino tak use karte ho. March mein wo chup-chaap galat ho jaata hai. Call par aap ek ghanta jaldi pahunchte ho, ya client nahi aata, aur kisi ko samajh nahi aata ki kya hua.

Ye guide batati hai ki kyun hota hai, kahan-kahan hota hai, aur meeting time nikalne ka wo tareeka jo saal bhar sahi rehta hai.

Sabse Badi Galti: Conversion Ek Baar Nikal Kar Likh Lena

Yahi sabse common galti hai, aur sabse mehengi.

Maan lo aapne 1 March 2026 ko dekha ki India New York se 10 ghante 30 minute aage hai. Sahi hai. Aap note kar lete ho.

Ab dekho 15 March ko kya hua:

Practical matlab ye: client ka "9 AM my time" aapke liye kya hai?

  • 1 March ko: 19:30 IST
  • 15 March ko: 18:30 IST

Wahi "9 AM", par aapke liye ek ghanta farak. Agar aapke calendar mein recurring 19:30 wali entry hai, toh 15 March se aap ek ghanta late pahunch rahe ho — har hafte, jab tak koi notice na kare.

India Mein Ghadi Nahi Badalti — Aur Yahi Dikkat Hai

India saal bhar IST par rehta hai, UTC+5:30. Kabhi nahi badalta. Isse lagta hai ki hamein DST se koi lena-dena nahi.

Ulta sach hai. Kyunki hamari ghadi nahi badalti, aur unki badalti hai, isliye gap badalta hai bina hamare taraf kuch hone ke. Aapko koi notification nahi aati, koi clock change nahi hota. Bas ek din client ka time matlab badal jaata hai.

London ke saath bhi wahi:

  • 15 January ko 09:00 London = 14:30 IST (gap 5h 30m)
  • 15 July ko 09:00 London = 13:30 IST (gap 4h 30m)

London apni British Summer Time par chala jaata hai, aur India se gap 5:30 se 4:30 ho jaata hai.

Teen Hafte Ka Wo Window Jab Sab Gadbad Hota Hai

Ye hissa sabse kam logon ko pata hai, aur March mein yahi confusion paida karta hai.

US aur Europe ek hi din ghadi nahi badalte.

  • US: March ka doosra Sunday
  • Europe: March ka aakhri Sunday

Beech mein teen hafte ka window aata hai jahan US ne switch kar liya hai aur Europe ne nahi. Us window mein New York aur London ka aapas ka gap bhi badal jaata hai:

Agar aapki ek call mein New York aur London dono hain, toh March ke un teen hafton mein jo slot dono ke liye kaam karta tha wo nahi karega. October mein ulta hota hai — Europe pehle (aakhri Sunday October) aur US baad mein (pehla Sunday November).

Australia Ulti Direction Mein Jaata Hai

Southern hemisphere ka summer northern ka winter hai, toh unka DST ulta chalta hai.

Sydney January mein UTC+11 hai aur July mein UTC+10. Northern hemisphere ke exactly ulta.

Isliye "Australia India se saadhe paanch ghante aage hai" jaisa rule saal ke ek hisse mein sahi hai aur baaki mein galat:

  • 1 March: Sydney India se 5h 30m aage
  • 5 April: Sydney India se 4h 30m aage

Aur Australia ke andar bhi sab same nahi hain. Brisbane DST follow nahi karta, Sydney karta hai — toh saal ke aadhe hisse mein wo dono ek hi time par hote hain aur aadhe mein ek ghanta alag. Ek hi desh, do answers.

Half-Hour Aur Quarter-Hour Zones

Har zone UTC se poore ghante door nahi hai. Agar aap poore ghante gin kar conversion karte ho, toh Asia ke bade hisse ke liye aap galat honge:

JagahUTC offset

Nepal wala case roz kaam mein aata hai aur dhyan se nikal jaata hai. India aur Nepal ka difference 15 minute hai. Agar aapne maan liya ki dono same hain, toh aap har call mein pandrah minute ka farak carry kar rahe ho.

Date Bhi Badalti Hai, Sirf Time Nahi

Ye deadline miss hone ki sabse badi wajah hai.

09:00 IST par New York mein pichhla din hota hai. 10 March ko subah 9 baje Delhi mein, New York mein 9 March ki raat 23:30 ho rahi hoti hai.

Matlab: agar koi application ki deadline "10 March, 11:59 PM EST" hai, toh aapke liye wo 11 March ki subah khatam hoti hai — par agar aap ulta samajh lein toh ek pura din ka farak ban jaata hai.

Isi tarah Tokyo ki taraf: 09:00 IST = 12:30 Tokyo, usi din. Tokyo aage hai par date wahi hai. Dono taraf date check karna zaroori hai, assumption nahi.

Overlap Hours — "Sabko Suit Kare" Ka Matlab

Meeting time nikalne ka asli sawaal ye nahi hai ki "unke liye kya time hai". Sawaal ye hai: kaunsa ghanta aisa hai jo sabke working hours ke andar ho.

Kuch combinations mein jawab aaraam se mil jaata hai:

  • India + Dubai — gap sirf 1h 30m, aur Dubai DST follow nahi karta. Saal bhar 8 ghante overlap milte hain, aur wo kabhi badalte nahi.
  • India + London — gap 4:30 ya 5:30. India ki shaam London ki subah-dopahar hoti hai, toh ek theek band mil jaata hai.

Aur kuch mein jawab "koi nahi hai" hota hai:

  • India + Los Angeles — gap 12:30 ya 13:30. Agar dono taraf 9:30 se 18:30 ka din maanein toh ek bhi ghanta aisa nahi jo dono ke working hours mein ho. Ye compromise ki baat nahi, ye arithmetic hai.

Jab aisa case ho, toh teen hi raaste hain: ek taraf apne hours ke bahar aaye, ya dono thoda stretch karein, ya call hi na ho aur async kaam chale. Jo cheez kaam nahi karti wo ye hai ki ek slot chun liya jaaye ye soch kar ki "theek hi hoga".

Edge band bhi dekho

Ek 09:30 ka start matlab 08:45 bilkul namumkin nahi hai. Working-hours ko ek sakht line maanne ke bajaye, "bilkul andar", "thoda bahar" aur "bahut bahar" ko alag dekhna zyada kaam ka hai. Aksar jo slot pehli nazar mein fail lagta hai wo kisi ek banda ke liye pandrah minute ki baat hoti hai.

Time Share Karne Ka Sahi Tareeka

Aapne sahi slot nikal liya. Ab usko bhejne mein galti ho sakti hai.

Jo kaam nahi karta

"Kal 3 baje?" — kiska 3 baje, aur kiska kal. Ye sabse buri form hai.

"3 PM IST" — behtar hai, par ab har banda khud convert karega. Paanch log, paanch mauke galti ke. Aur agar koi apna purana note dekh kar convert karega toh DST ki wajah se galat nikalega.

Jo kaam karta hai

Calendar invite. Ek .ics file moment ko UTC mein store karti hai. Har attendee ka calendar usko apne zone mein dikhata hai, apne DST rules ke saath. Koi conversion nahi, koi galti nahi.

Ye farak samajhne layak hai: calendar invite mein "3 PM" nahi jaata. Jo jaata hai wo ek absolute moment hai — jaise 20260310T130000Z. "3 PM" wo hai jo aapki screen us moment ko dikhati hai. Isi liye invite se galti nahi hoti aur text se hoti hai.

Agar text mein likhna hi pade, toh dono taraf ka time likho: "Tuesday 10 March, 18:30 IST / 09:00 New York". Isse galti pakdi jaati hai, kyunki agar koi ek figure galat hai toh doosra usse match nahi karega.

Recurring Meetings — Ek Alag Problem

Ek baar ki meeting mein aap ek baar sahi nikal lo, baat khatam. Recurring meeting DST ke saath khisakti hai, aur yahi sabse zyada pareshan karta hai.

Hota ye hai: aapne call ko apne IST calendar mein 19:30 pe fix kiya kyunki wo client ka 9 AM tha. US DST shuru hone par client ka 9 AM 18:30 IST ho jaata hai. Aapki entry 19:30 par rehti hai, toh aap har hafte ek ghanta late.

Isse bachne ke do tareeke:

  1. Series uss banda ke calendar se bane jiske zone mein time fix hai. Agar "client ka 9 AM" matlab hai, toh invite unke calendar se aaye — unka calendar DST ke saath khud adjust karega.
  2. DST switch dates par ek reminder rakho — March ka doosra aur aakhri Sunday, November ka pehla, October ka aakhri. Un hafton mein recurring calls ek baar check kar lo.

Practical Checklist

  1. Jis date ki meeting hai, usi date ka offset dekho — aaj ka nahi. Agar meeting April mein hai toh April ka offset chahiye.
  2. Date column check karo, sirf time nahi. Doosre zone mein din badal sakta hai.
  3. Overlap dekho, single conversion nahi. "Unke liye 9 AM hai" kaafi nahi — ye bhi dekho ki aapke liye wo kaam ka time hai ya nahi.
  4. Half-hour zones ko alag se verify karo — Nepal, Adelaide, India khud.
  5. .ics bhejo, ya dono taraf ka time likho.
  6. Recurring calls ko DST weeks mein dobara check karo.

Ye Kaam Haath Se Na Karein

Har ek cheez jo is guide mein hai — DST ki date, southern hemisphere ka ulta shift, half-hour offsets, date rollover — wo aisi hai jo yaad rakhne se nahi nikalti. Har baar lookup karni padti hai.

Hamara Time Zone Planner yahi karta hai. Aap jo date aur time chun rahe ho, usi moment ka offset browser ki time zone database se padhta hai — toh DST apne aap handle hota hai. 12 shehar tak ek saath dikhte hain, kiske working hours ke andar hai wo mark hota hai, aur poore 24 ghante scan karke wo slots batata hai jo sabke liye kaam karte hain.

Aur jab koi slot kaam nahi karta — India aur US west coast jaise — toh wo saaf bol deta hai, koi aisa ghanta suggest nahi karta jo asal mein theek nahi hai. Saath hi .ics download kar sakte ho taaki har attendee ka calendar khud conversion kar le.

Sab kuch browser mein chalta hai, koi account nahi, kuch upload nahi hota.

FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions

India aur New York ka time difference kitna hai?

Ye fixed nahi hai. India New York se 10 ghante 30 minute aage hota hai jab New York standard time par hai, aur 9 ghante 30 minute aage jab wahan daylight saving chal raha hai. US March ke doosre Sunday ko DST shuru karta hai aur November ke pehle Sunday ko khatam. Isliye saal mein do baar ye gap ek ghante badal jaata hai.

India mein daylight saving kyun nahi hai?

India saal bhar IST par rehta hai, UTC+5:30. Iska matlab hai ki India ki ghadi kabhi nahi badalti, lekin dusre deshon ki badalti hai. Isliye jab Europe ya US apni ghadi badalte hain, toh India ke saath unka difference badal jaata hai — bina India mein kuch badle.

Ek baar time difference likh lena kaafi nahi hai?

Nahi. Agar aapne January mein nikala ki London IST se 5 ghante 30 minute peeche hai, toh wo April mein galat ho jaata hai kyunki London ne British Summer Time par shift kar liya aur gap 4 ghante 30 minute ho gaya. Isliye recurring meetings saal mein do baar ek ghanta khisak jaati hain.

Nepal ka offset kya hai?

Nepal UTC+5:45 par hai, matlab India se 15 minute aage. Ye quarter-hour offset hai. Agar aap sirf poore ghante gin kar conversion karenge toh Nepal, India aur Australia ke kuch hisson ke liye aap 15 se 45 minute galat honge.

Deadline doosre time zone mein ho toh kya dhyan rakhna chahiye?

Date bhi check karein, sirf time nahi. 09:00 IST par New York mein pichhla din hota hai. Matlab aapke liye jo deadline kal lagti hai, wo unke liye aaj khatam ho sakti hai. Application aur submission deadlines mein yahi galti sabse mehengi padti hai.

Meeting time share karne ka sabse safe tareeka kya hai?

Calendar invite bhejna, kyunki wo moment ko UTC mein store karta hai aur har attendee ka calendar usko apne zone mein dikhata hai. Message mein likhne par har banda khud convert karta hai, aur ek galti kaafi hai. Agar text mein likhna hi ho toh zone ka naam saath likhein aur dono taraf ka time mention karein.
