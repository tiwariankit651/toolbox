💰 Income Tax Old vs New Regime 2026 — Konsa Regime Better Hai?

July 2026 · 8 min read

Har saal ITR file karte waqt yeh confusion hota hai — old regime choose karu ya new regime? FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) ke liye dono regimes mein kuch important changes aaye hain. Is article mein hum dono regimes ko detail mein compare karenge, example calculations ke saath, taaki aap easily decide kar sako.

New Regime vs Old Regime — Basic Difference

New Regime (Default): Lower tax rates, lekin almost koi deductions nahi milte (80C, 80D, HRA exemption sab gone). Standard deduction ₹75,000.

Old Regime: Higher tax rates, lekin aap 80C (₹1.5L), 80D (health insurance), HRA exemption, home loan interest (Section 24) sab claim kar sakte ho.

Old Regime Mein Available Deductions

  • Section 80C (₹1.5L) — PPF, ELSS, LIC, EPF, tuition fees, home loan principal
  • Section 80D (₹25K-₹1L) — Health insurance premium (self + parents)
  • HRA Exemption — Rent pay karte ho toh significant tax saving
  • Section 24(b) (₹2L) — Home loan interest deduction
  • Section 80CCD(1B) (₹50K) — NPS additional deduction
  • Section 80TTA (₹10K) — Savings account interest

Example Calculations — Konsa Regime Mein Kitna Tax?

Case 1: ₹5,00,000 Annual Income (Entry-level job)

New Regime: Taxable income after ₹75K SD = ₹4,25,000. Tax = 5% of ₹25,000 = ₹1,250. But 87A rebate applicable → Tax = ₹0

Old Regime: Taxable income after ₹50K SD = ₹4,50,000. Tax = 5% of ₹2,00,000 = ₹10,000. 87A rebate → Tax = ₹0

✅ Verdict: Dono mein zero tax. New regime default rakho.

Case 2: ₹8,00,000 Annual Income

New Regime: Taxable = ₹7,25,000. Tax = 5% of ₹3,25,000 = ₹16,250. 87A rebate → Tax = ₹0

Old Regime (with ₹1.5L 80C + ₹50K SD): Taxable = ₹6,00,000. Tax = ₹12,500 + 20% of ₹1,00,000 = ₹32,500. No 87A (above ₹5L) → Tax = ₹32,500

✅ Verdict: New regime clearly better — zero tax vs ₹32,500!

Case 3: ₹12,00,000 Annual Income

New Regime: Taxable = ₹11,25,000. Tax calculation under slabs = ₹20,000 + ₹32,500 = ₹52,500. 87A rebate applicable (under ₹12L taxable) → Tax = ₹0

Old Regime (₹1.5L 80C + ₹25K 80D + ₹50K SD + ₹1.8L HRA): Taxable = ₹7,95,000. Tax = ₹12,500 + 20% of ₹2,95,000 = ₹71,500 → Tax = ₹71,500

✅ Verdict: New regime wins massively — ₹0 vs ₹71,500!

Case 4: ₹20,00,000 Annual Income (Senior professional)

New Regime: Taxable = ₹19,25,000. Tax = ₹20,000 + ₹40,000 + ₹60,000 + ₹65,000 = ₹1,85,000 → Tax ≈ ₹1,85,000 + cess

Old Regime (₹1.5L 80C + ₹50K 80D + ₹50K SD + ₹2.4L HRA + ₹2L home loan): Taxable = ₹12,60,000. Tax = ₹12,500 + ₹1,00,000 + ₹78,000 = ₹1,90,500 → Tax ≈ ₹1,90,500 + cess

✅ Verdict: Almost same! Agar heavy deductions hain (HRA + home loan + 80D) toh old regime thoda better. Otherwise new regime simpler hai.

Kab Old Regime Better Hai?

  • ✅ High HRA claim karte ho (metro city mein rent ₹20K+ per month)
  • ✅ Home loan chal raha hai (₹2L interest deduction Section 24)
  • ✅ Parents ke liye bhi health insurance hai (80D mein ₹50K-₹1L claim)
  • ✅ NPS mein invest karte ho (extra ₹50K deduction)
  • ✅ Total deductions ₹3.75 lakh se zyada hain

Kab New Regime Better Hai?

  • ✅ Koi investments nahi karte (no PPF, no ELSS, no LIC)
  • ✅ Own house mein rehte ho (no HRA claim)
  • ✅ Income ₹12.75L se kam hai (zero tax with rebate!)
  • ✅ Simple filing chahiye — deductions ka paperwork nahi karna
  • ✅ Freshers aur young professionals jinka salary structure simple hai

Kaise Decide Kare? Quick Formula

Apne total eligible deductions jodo (80C + 80D + HRA + home loan + NPS). Agar yeh total ₹3.75 lakh se zyada hai, toh old regime consider karo. Nahi toh new regime default better hai.

Last Date to Choose Regime

  • Salaried employees: Financial year start mein employer ko inform karo (April). ITR file karte waqt bhi switch kar sakte ho
  • Business/Professional income: Ek baar new regime choose kiya toh switch back limited hai (only once in lifetime)
  • ITR filing deadline: 31st July 2026 (AY 2026-27)

Important: New regime ab default hai. Agar old regime chahiye toh actively opt-in karna padega Form 10-IEA file karke.
