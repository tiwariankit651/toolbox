✂️ How to Trim Audio Files Online

April 2026 · 3 min read

Need to cut a specific part from an audio file? Create a ringtone, trim a podcast clip, or cut music for a presentation — all free in your browser.

How to Trim Audio

  1. Open Audio Trimmer
  2. Upload your audio file (MP3, WAV, OGG)
  3. See the waveform visualization
  4. Set precise start and end times
  5. Preview your selection
  6. Click Trim and Download

Use Cases

  • Ringtones — cut the best part of a song
  • Podcasts — extract a specific segment
  • Presentations — trim background music to exact length
  • Voice notes — remove silence from beginning/end
