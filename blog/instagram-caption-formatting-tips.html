📱 Format Instagram Captions with Clean Line Breaks

April 2026 · 3 min read

Instagram removes extra line breaks from captions, making your carefully formatted text look messy. Our Caption Spacer fixes this.

The Problem

When you type a caption with blank lines in Instagram, it removes them and squishes everything together.

The Solution

Our tool adds invisible Unicode characters between lines that Instagram cannot remove. Your formatting stays clean.

Tips for Great Captions

  • Keep paragraphs short (2-3 lines)
  • Use emojis as bullet points
  • Put hashtags in a separate paragraph at the end
  • Instagram post limit: 2200 characters
  • Bio limit: 150 characters
