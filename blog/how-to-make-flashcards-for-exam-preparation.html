🃏 Flashcards Kaise Banaye for Exam Preparation — Spaced Repetition Method

July 2026 · 7 min read

Competitive exams ki preparation mein sabse badi problem kya hai? Yaad karna aur yaad rakhna. Aap chapter padh lete ho, notes bana lete ho, lekin 2 hafte baad sab bhool jaate ho. Yahi woh problem hai jo flashcards solve karti hain. Is guide mein hum dekhenge ki flashcards kya hain, yeh kyun kaam karti hain, aur aap humara free tool kaise use kar sakte ho apni exam preparation supercharge karne ke liye.

Flashcards Kya Hain?

Flashcard ek simple concept hai — ek card ke ek taraf question hota hai, doosri taraf answer. Jaise:

  • Front: "Bharat ka sabse lamba river bridge kaun sa hai?" → Back: "Dhola-Sadiya Bridge (9.15 km, Assam)"
  • Front: "Mitochondria ka function kya hai?" → Back: "Powerhouse of the cell — ATP produce karta hai"
  • Front: "Article 370 kab revoke hua?" → Back: "5 August 2019"

Simple lagta hai, lekin yeh simplicity hi iska power hai. Aap ek time pe ek fact pe focus karte ho — koi distraction nahi.

Flashcards Kyun Kaam Karti Hain? — The Science

Active Recall

Jab aap flashcard dekhte ho aur answer yaad karne ki koshish karte ho, toh aapka brain active recall perform karta hai. Yeh passive reading (sirf padh ke jaana) se 3x zyada effective hai. Research shows ki jab aap information ko actively retrieve karte ho, toh woh memory mein stronger connections banati hai.

Socho aise — agar aap sirf notes padhte ho, toh brain ko lagta hai "yeh toh already pata hai." Lekin jab aapko blank card dekhke answer yaad karna padta hai, toh brain ko mehnat karni padti hai. Yeh mehnat hi learning hai.

Spaced Repetition — Bhoolne Se Pehle Revise Karo

German psychologist Hermann Ebbinghaus ne "Forgetting Curve" discover ki thi. Iska matlab hai ki naya information learn karne ke baad:

  • 1 din baad — 70% bhool jaate ho
  • 1 week baad — 90% bhool jaate ho
  • 1 month baad — almost sab bhool jaate ho

Spaced repetition is curve ko beat karta hai. Idea simple hai — jo card aapko yaad hai, woh baad mein dikhao. Jo bhool gaye, woh jaldi dikhao. Har successful recall ke baad interval badhta jaata hai: 1 din → 3 din → 7 din → 15 din → 30 din.

Effective Flashcards Kaise Banaye — Golden Rules

  • One fact per card — ek card pe ek hi cheez likho. "5 rivers of Punjab" ko 5 alag cards mein todo.
  • Short aur clear — answer 1-2 lines se zyada nahi hona chahiye. Paragraph mat likho.
  • Apni language mein likho — textbook copy mat karo. Apne words mein likho toh yaad rehta hai.
  • Images use karo — diagrams, maps, formulas ka screenshot — visual memory strong hoti hai.
  • Question format use karo — "Capital of Meghalaya" ki jagah "Meghalaya ki capital kya hai?" likho.
  • Mnemonics add karo — "My Very Educated Mother Just Served Us Nachos" (planets) jaise tricks card pe likho.

SM-2 Algorithm Kya Hai?

"Hard" mark kiya toh kal hi aayega. Yeh algorithm Anki app mein bhi use hota hai — lekin humara tool free hai aur browser mein kaam karta hai, koi app install nahi karna.

Subject-Wise Flashcard Tips

General Knowledge (GK) / Current Affairs

  • Daily 10-15 new cards banao from newspaper/current affairs
  • Format: "Who/What/When/Where" questions
  • Categories banao: Polity, Geography, History, Economy, Science
  • Monthly revision se purane cards delete karo jo outdated ho gaye

Vocabulary (English/Hindi)

  • Front: Word → Back: Meaning + Example sentence
  • Synonyms aur antonyms bhi add karo
  • Root words ke cards banao — ek root se 5-10 words yaad ho jaate hain
  • Humara Quiz Maker use karo vocabulary test ke liye

Formulas (Math/Physics/Chemistry)

  • Front: Formula name/situation → Back: Formula + units
  • Derivation nahi, sirf final formula aur kab use karna hai
  • Related formulas ko group karo (e.g., all kinematics equations together)
  • Numerical example bhi add karo — "If v=20m/s, a=5m/s², find t" type

Common Mistakes Jo Students Karte Hain

  • Too much information per card — ek card pe paragraph likhna. Break it down!
  • Cards banake review nahi karna — flashcards tabhi kaam karti hain jab aap daily review karo
  • Sirf easy cards review karna — hard cards skip mat karo, wahi important hain
  • Copy-paste from textbook — apne words mein likho, understanding zaroori hai

Daily Study Routine with Flashcards

Ek effective routine kuch aisa ho sakta hai:

  • Morning (15 min) — previous day ke new cards review karo
  • Afternoon (10 min) — spaced repetition due cards complete karo
  • Evening (20 min) — aaj jo padha usse 10-15 new cards banao
  • Weekend (30 min) — week ke sabhi cards ka comprehensive review

Total sirf 45 minutes daily — lekin consistency se 3 months mein 1000+ facts permanently yaad ho jaate hain.
