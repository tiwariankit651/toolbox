🔒 Remove EXIF Metadata from Photos

April 2026 · 3 min read

Every photo you take with your phone contains hidden data called EXIF metadata — including your GPS location, camera model, date, and time. Sharing these photos online can expose your home address and daily routine.

What is EXIF Data?

  • GPS coordinates — exact location where photo was taken
  • Camera model — phone or camera brand
  • Date and time — when the photo was taken
  • Lens info — focal length, aperture
  • Software — editing app used

How to Remove It

  1. Open EXIF Metadata Remover
  2. Drop one or multiple images
  3. The tool re-draws each image on a clean canvas, stripping all metadata
  4. Download the clean images

Unlike other tools, your photos never leave your device. The cleaning happens entirely in your browser.

When to Remove EXIF Data

  • Before posting on social media
  • Before sending photos to strangers
  • Before uploading to public websites
  • When selling items online with photos
