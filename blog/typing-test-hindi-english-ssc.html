⌨️ Typing Test Practice for SSC CHSL — Hindi & English

June 2026 · 4 min read

SSC CHSL (Combined Higher Secondary Level) has a mandatory typing test that is qualifying in nature. If you clear Tier-1 and Tier-2 but fail the typing test, you lose everything. Many candidates underestimate this stage. Here is everything you need to know about SSC typing requirements and how to practice for free.

SSC CHSL Typing Test Requirements 2026

LanguageSpeed RequiredDuration
English35 WPM (words per minute)10 minutes
Hindi30 WPM10 minutes

Note: This is a qualifying test — no marks are awarded. You either pass or fail. 5% relaxation in speed is given to VH (Visually Handicapped) candidates.

Key Facts About SSC Typing Test

  • Conducted on computer (not typewriter)
  • English typing on standard QWERTY keyboard
  • Hindi typing on Kruti Dev or Mangal font (Remington keyboard layout)
  • Mistakes reduce your net speed — accuracy matters
  • You can choose either English or Hindi (based on post preference)

How to Practice Typing for SSC

  1. Open the FreeToolHubs Typing Test
  2. Select your language (English or Hindi)
  3. Set timer to 10 minutes (matching SSC exam duration)
  4. Start typing the displayed passage
  5. Check your WPM and accuracy after each session
  6. Practice daily for at least 30 minutes

Tips to Improve Typing Speed

  • Learn touch typing: Do not look at the keyboard — memorize key positions
  • Home row first: Master ASDF JKL; before moving to other rows
  • Accuracy over speed: Start slow and accurate, speed comes naturally
  • Daily practice: 30 minutes daily for 2 months is enough for most people
  • Use all fingers: Each finger has assigned keys — do not type with 2 fingers
  • Practice paragraphs: Not just random words — SSC gives full paragraphs

Hindi Typing Tips (Mangal/Kruti Dev)

  • Learn the Remington keyboard layout — it is different from Inscript
  • Practice matras (vowel signs) separately — they are the trickiest part
  • Use Hindi typing tutor software alongside our online tool
  • Start with simple sentences before attempting full paragraphs

30-Day Practice Plan

  • Week 1: Learn key positions, aim for 15 WPM with 95% accuracy
  • Week 2: Practice common words, aim for 25 WPM
  • Week 3: Full paragraphs, aim for 30 WPM
  • Week 4: Timed 10-minute tests, aim for 35+ WPM
