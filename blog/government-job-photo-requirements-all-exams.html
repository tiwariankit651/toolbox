📸 Government Job Photo Requirements — All Exams in One Place

May 2026 · 5 min read

Every government exam in India has specific photo and signature upload requirements. Getting these wrong means your application gets rejected — no second chances. We have compiled the photo specifications for all major competitive exams in one easy reference table so you never have to search multiple websites again.

Why Photo Requirements Matter

Recruitment bodies use strict image specifications to ensure uniformity in admit cards and verification documents. If your photo does not meet the exact size, format, or dimension requirements, the upload system will either reject it outright or your application may be cancelled during verification. Every year, lakhs of candidates lose their application fee because of photo-related errors.

How to Get Your Photo to the Right Size

  1. Take a clear passport-size photo with a white background
  2. Open the Image Resizer and set the exact pixel dimensions from the table above
  3. If the file is still too large, use the Image Compressor to reduce KB size
  4. Need to remove a coloured background? Use the Background Remover first

General Tips for All Exams

  • Recency — photo should be taken within the last 3 months
  • Face coverage — face should occupy 70–80% of the frame
  • No accessories — remove sunglasses, caps (religious headwear allowed)
  • Ears visible — both ears should be clearly visible
  • Signature consistency — sign the same way during exam and verification
  • Black ink only — use black or dark blue pen for signature
  • White paper — sign on plain white paper, scan or photograph it
