⌨️ How to Type Faster for Government Exams — Typing Speed Improve Kaise Kare

July 2026 · 8 min read

Agar aap SSC CHSL, CSIR, Railway, ya kisi bhi government job ki taiyari kar rahe ho jahan typing test hota hai, toh yeh article aapke liye hai. Bahut saare students written exam clear kar lete hain but typing test mein fail ho jaate hain. Sad reality hai — lekin fix karna possible hai agar aap sahi method follow karo.

Is guide mein main aapko bataunga ki typing speed kaise badhaye, daily practice routine kya honi chahiye, aur ek complete 30-day improvement plan dunga jo actually kaam karta hai.

Government Jobs Mein Typing Speed Kitni Chahiye?

Pehle samajh lo ki aapko exactly kitni speed chahiye:

  • SSC CHSL (LDC/DEO) — English: 35 WPM, Hindi: 30 WPM
  • SSC CGL (Tax Assistant) — English: 35 WPM (only for specific posts)
  • CSIR — English: 40 WPM
  • Railway (NTPC) — English: 30 WPM, Hindi: 25 WPM
  • High Court (Clerk) — English: 35-40 WPM
  • State Government Posts — Varies, usually 25-35 WPM

Note: WPM ka matlab hai Words Per Minute. Ek "word" = 5 characters (spaces included). Toh 35 WPM matlab 175 characters per minute accurately type karna.

Typing Speed Slow Kyun Hoti Hai? — Common Mistakes

Pehle apni galtiyan identify karo:

  • Hunt and peck method — Keyboard dekhke 2 ungliyon se type karna. Yeh sabse badi galti hai.
  • Wrong finger placement — Home row pe fingers nahi rakhna
  • Speed pe focus, accuracy ignore — Fast type karne ki koshish mein bahut errors aate hain
  • Irregular practice — Ek din 2 ghanta, phir 5 din kuch nahi. Consistency matters more than duration.
  • Wrong posture — Slouching ya wrist angle galat hone se fatigue jaldi aati hai

Correct Finger Placement — Home Row Method

Touch typing ka foundation hai home row. Yeh samajh lo toh speed automatically badhegi:

  • Left hand: A (pinky), S (ring), D (middle), F (index)
  • Right hand: J (index), K (middle), L (ring), ; (pinky)
  • Thumbs: Space bar pe rest karein
  • F aur J keys pe small bumps hote hain — bina dekhe fingers wapas home row pe laane ke liye

Rule: Har finger ki apni territory hai. Index fingers zyada keys cover karti hain. Practice karo ki kaunsi finger kaunsi key press karegi — keyboard dekhe bina.

Daily Practice Routine — Kaise Practice Karein

  1. Warm-up (5 min) — Home row keys practice: asdf jkl; repeatedly type karo
  2. Paragraph typing (15 min) — FreeToolHubs Typing Test pe paragraphs type karo. Focus on accuracy.
  3. Problem keys (10 min) — Jo keys miss hoti hain (usually Q, Z, X, P) unko specifically practice karo
  4. Speed test (5 min) — Timed test lo aur apna WPM note karo
  5. Cool down (5 min) — Slow, relaxed typing without pressure.

Total: 40 minutes daily. Consistency zyada important hai duration se.

30-Day Typing Improvement Plan

Week 1: Foundation (Day 1-7)

  • Home row master karo — bina keyboard dekhe ASDF JKL; type kar sako
  • Top row add karo (QWERTY UIOP)
  • Target: 15-20 WPM with 95%+ accuracy
  • Speed ki chinta mat karo — accuracy first!

Week 2: Building Speed (Day 8-14)

  • Bottom row add karo (ZXCVBNM)
  • Numbers aur common punctuation practice karo
  • Daily 2-3 timed tests lo
  • Target: 20-25 WPM with 93%+ accuracy

Week 3: Consistency (Day 15-21)

  • Full paragraphs type karo — news articles, book passages
  • Capital letters (Shift key) practice karo
  • Common words ki muscle memory develop karo
  • Target: 25-30 WPM with 95%+ accuracy

Week 4: Exam Ready (Day 22-30)

  • Actual exam-style passages practice karo
  • 10-minute continuous typing sessions (SSC format)
  • Hindi typing bhi start karo (agar required hai)
  • Target: 30-35 WPM with 94%+ accuracy

Hindi Typing vs English Typing

Bahut students confuse hote hain Hindi typing ko lekar:

  • Keyboard layout: SSC mein Mangal font with Inscript/Remington layout hota hai
  • Inscript layout — Government standard. Keys phonetically arranged hain.
  • Remington (GAIL) — Typewriter-based layout. Purane typists prefer karte hain.
  • Speed requirement: Hindi mein usually 25-30 WPM chahiye (English se 5 WPM kam)
  • Tip: Agar dono mein option hai, English choose karo — practice easier hai

Typing Speed Badhane Ke Pro Tips

  • Keyboard mat dekho — Pehle slow lagega, but 1 week mein habit ban jayegi
  • Errors ko ignore mat karo — Agar ek word baar baar galat hota hai, usko 10 baar separately type karo
  • Posture fix karo — Straight back, elbows 90 degrees, wrists floating
  • Mechanical keyboard try karo — Budget option: Cosmic Byte or Redgear (₹1000-1500)
  • Daily progress track karo — Notebook mein date, WPM, accuracy likho
  • Breaks lo — Har 20 min pe 2 min break. Wrist exercises karo.

Exam Day Tips

  • Exam se pehle 10 min warm-up karo (agar allowed hai)
  • Pehle poora passage ek baar padh lo — unfamiliar words identify karo
  • Steady pace maintain karo — rush mat karo starting mein
  • Agar galti ho jaye toh turant backspace karo
  • Last 2 minutes mein speed thodi badha do agar behind ho

Conclusion

Typing speed improve karna koi rocket science nahi hai — bas consistent practice chahiye. Agar aap daily 40 minutes de sakte ho with correct technique, toh 30 days mein aap easily 30-35 WPM cross kar loge. Remember: accuracy pehle, speed baad mein. Keyboard dekhna band karo, home row pe fingers rakho, aur daily practice karo.

SSC CHSL ya kisi bhi govt exam ka typing test clear karna aapke haath mein hai. Start today!
