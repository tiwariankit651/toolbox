🏠 Rent Receipt Aur HRA Claim — Poora Hisaab

October 2026 · 11 min read

Har saal January-February mein HR ka mail aata hai: "rent receipts submit karo". Aur phir wahi sawaal — kitni receipts? Landlord ka PAN chahiye ya nahi? Revenue stamp lagana padega? Aur asli mein kitna tax bachega?

Is guide mein har sawaal ka seedha jawab hai, formula ke saath, aur wo do-teen galtiyan bhi jo claim reject karwa deti hain.

Pehle Sabse Zaroori Baat: Old Regime Ya New?

Ye sabse pehle clear hona chahiye, warna baaki sab bekaar hai.

HRA exemption sirf old tax regime mein milti hai. New regime mein HRA ki koi exemption nahi hai — chahe aap saal bhar ki receipts jama kar do, ek rupaya tax kam nahi hoga.

Aaj new regime default hai. Toh agar aapne specifically old regime nahi chuna, toh aap new mein hain aur rent receipts se aapko kuch nahi milega. Receipts jama karne se pehle ye confirm karo ki aap kis regime mein hain — HR se, ya apne last year ke Form 16 se.

💡 Agar aapka HRA bada hai aur rent bhi zyada hai, toh old regime chunna faydemand ho sakta hai. Lekin ye dono regimes ka poora comparison hai, sirf HRA ka nahi — standard deduction, 80C, slab rates sab milkar decide karte hain. Ek baar dono calculate karke dekh lena behtar hai.

HRA Exemption Ka Formula — Teen Mein Se Sabse Kam

Section 10(13A) kehta hai ki exemption teen amounts mein se jo sabse kam ho, wahi milegi:

  1. Actual HRA jo aapko mila saal bhar mein
  2. Rent paid minus basic salary ka 10%
  3. Basic salary ka 50% (metro cities) ya 40% (baaki shehar)

Yahan "basic salary" ka matlab basic + DA hai, poora CTC nahi. Ye log aksar galat samajh lete hain.

Ek Example Se Samjho

Asha Pune mein rehti hai. Uski saalana figures:

Basic + DA    = ₹6,00,000
HRA received = ₹2,40,000
Rent paid    = ₹1,80,000 (₹15,000/month)

Teen amounts:

Kaunsa amount Hisaab Value
1. Actual HRA jo mila ₹2,40,000
2. Rent − 10% basic 1,80,000 − 60,000 ₹1,20,000 ← sabse kam
3. 40% of basic Pune non-metro tha FY 2025-26 tak ₹2,40,000

Toh exemption ₹1,20,000 hai. Baaki ₹1,20,000 HRA taxable hai.

Dhyan do: Asha ko ₹2,40,000 HRA mila par exemption sirf aadha hi mili. Ye sabse common surprise hai — log sochte hain poora HRA tax-free hai, par aksar wo amount #2 se limit ho jaata hai, kyunki rent utna zyada nahi hota.

Metro Cities Ki List Badal Gayi Hai — Ye Zaroori Hai

Ye wo update hai jo bahut logon ko nahi pata.

Pehle sirf chaar shehar metro the — Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai. Yahan 50% of basic milta tha, baaki sab jagah 40%.

Ab chaar naye shehar jud gaye hain: Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune aur Ahmedabad. Total aath shehar, aur in sab mein 50% of basic.

⚠️ Timing matter karti hai: ye chaar naye shehar FY 2026-27 se 50% pe aaye hain. FY 2025-26 ke filing mein wo abhi bhi 40% wale the. Toh agar aap purane saal ka return file kar rahe ho, purana rate lagega. Apne employer se confirm kar lo ki wo kaunsa category use kar rahe hain.

Asha ke example mein agar Pune 50% pe aa jaye toh amount #3 ban jaata hai ₹3,00,000 — par exemption phir bhi ₹1,20,000 rahegi, kyunki amount #2 sabse kam hai. Metro status se fayda tab hota hai jab aapka rent zyada ho aur amount #3 limiting factor ho.

Landlord Ka PAN — Ye Wo Jagah Hai Jahan Claim Atakta Hai

Rule simple hai: saalana rent ₹1 lakh se zyada ho toh landlord ka PAN dena padta hai.

₹1 lakh saal ka matlab ₹8,334 mahina. Ye threshold itna kam hai ki zyadatar shehron mein har kirayedar iske upar hai. Toh practically, PAN almost hamesha chahiye hota hai.

PAN na dene par employer HRA exemption Form 16 mein nahi dikhata, aur phir aapko ITR mein khud claim karna padta hai — jisme notice aane ka chance badh jaata hai.

Agar Landlord PAN Nahi Deta?

Ye aam problem hai. Kuch landlords PAN share karne se hichkichate hain kyunki unhe lagta hai rental income declare karni padegi — jo waise bhi karni chahiye.

Rule ke hisaab se agar landlord ke paas genuinely PAN nahi hai, toh uska signed declaration accept hota hai jisme likha ho ki unke paas PAN nahi hai, saath mein naam aur address. Lekin agar PAN hai aur wo dena nahi chahte, toh declaration technically galat hoga.

Aur ek level upar: agar saalana rent ₹6 lakh se zyada hai (₹50,000 mahina), toh aapko PAN ke saath TDS bhi deduct karna hota hai. Ye bahut log miss kar dete hain.

Receipt Mein Kya Hona Chahiye

Ek valid rent receipt mein ye cheezein honi chahiye:

  • Tenant ka naam — aapka
  • Landlord ka naam aur signature
  • Rent amount — figures mein
  • Period — kaunsa mahina ya kaunsi duration
  • Property ka address
  • Landlord ka PAN — agar ₹1 lakh se zyada
  • Payment mode — cash, UPI, bank transfer

Humara rent receipt generator yahi format banata hai, aur 12 mahine ki receipts ek saath — kyunki asli zaroorat tab padti hai jab HR poore saal ki maangta hai.

Revenue Stamp Ka Sach

Parampara hai ki ₹5,000 se zyada cash receipt par ₹1 ka revenue stamp lagta hai, aur landlord uske upar sign karta hai.

Lekin practically: agar aap bank transfer ya UPI se rent dete ho, toh transfer khud hi proof hai aur stamp aksar nahi maanga jaata. Cash mein dete ho toh stamp lagana behtar hai — ye ek purana convention hai jo kai employers abhi bhi follow karte hain.

Parents Ko Rent Dena — Allowed Hai, Par Dhyan Se

Ye bilkul legal hai aur bahut log karte hain. Rules:

  • Property parent ke naam honi chahiye — agar ghar aapke naam hai toh khud ko rent nahi de sakte
  • Parent ko wo rental income apni return mein dikhani padegi — ye hissa log skip kar dete hain, aur yahi problem banti hai
  • Bank transfer karo, cash nahi — paper trail chahiye
  • Receipts lo, bhale ghar ka mamla ho

Is arrangement ki scrutiny zyada hoti hai kyunki iska misuse bahut hua hai. Agar parent ki income tax slab aapse kam hai toh overall family ko fayda hota hai — wahi iska asli logic hai. Lekin paperwork pura rakho.

Aur ek baat: spouse ko rent dene par claim aksar challenge hota hai, kyunki marriage mein property arrangement ko genuine landlord-tenant relationship maanna mushkil hota hai. Isse bachna behtar hai.

Jab Rent Receipts Bilkul Nahi Chahiye

Agar saalana rent ₹36,000 se kam hai — matlab ₹3,000 mahina — toh employers aksar bina receipt claim accept kar lete hain. Ye chhoti rents ke liye ek practical relaxation hai.

Lekin ₹3,000 mahina aaj ke zamane mein bahut kam hai, toh zyadatar logon pe ye apply nahi hota.

Checklist — Submission Se Pehle

  • ☐ Confirm kiya ki main old regime mein hoon
  • ☐ Poore saal ki receipts hain (ya jitne mahine rent diya)
  • ☐ Landlord ka naam aur signature har receipt pe
  • ☐ PAN hai (agar saal ka rent ₹1 lakh se zyada)
  • ☐ Address sahi hai, aur flat badla hai toh dono addresses ki alag receipts
  • ☐ Bank transfer ke statements rakh liye (agar transfer se diya)
  • ☐ Rent agreement ki copy taiyar hai (kai employers maangte hain)
  • ☐ Calculator se check kiya ki asli exemption kitni milegi

FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions

Landlord ka PAN kab mandatory hota hai?

Jab saalana rent ₹1 lakh se zyada ho, matlab lagbhag ₹8,334 mahina. Iske bina employer HRA exemption aksar process nahi karta. Agar landlord ke paas PAN nahi hai toh uska signed declaration accept hota hai.

HRA exemption ka formula kya hai?

Exemption teen amounts mein se sabse kam hoti hai: actual HRA received, rent paid minus basic salary ka 10%, aur basic ka 50% metro cities mein ya 40% baaki shehron mein. Ye sirf old tax regime mein milti hai.

Kya new tax regime mein HRA exemption milti hai?

Nahi. HRA exemption sirf old regime mein available hai. New regime chunne par rent receipts se tax kam nahi hota, isliye regime chunne se pehle dono compare karna chahiye.

Parents ko rent de kar HRA claim kar sakte hain?

Haan, agar arrangement genuine ho. Property parent ke naam honi chahiye aur unhe wo rent apni income mein dikhana padta hai. Bank transfer aur receipts rakhna zaroori hai kyunki is arrangement ki scrutiny hoti hai.

Revenue stamp lagana zaroori hai?

Parampara ke hisaab se ₹5,000 se zyada cash receipt par ₹1 ka revenue stamp lagta hai aur landlord uske upar sign karta hai. Bank transfer ya UPI se payment ho toh transfer khud proof hai, isliye stamp aksar nahi maanga jaata.

Bina receipt kitna HRA claim kar sakte hain?

Agar saalana rent ₹36,000 se kam hai, matlab ₹3,000 mahina, toh employers aksar bina receipt claim accept kar lete hain. Usse zyada par receipts maangi jaati hain.

Summary

Teen cheezein yaad rakho. Pehla, old regime mein ho tabhi HRA ka matlab hai. Doosra, exemption teen amounts mein se sabse kam hoti hai aur aksar wo "rent minus 10% basic" wala hota hai — isliye poora HRA tax-free nahi milta. Teesra, ₹1 lakh saal se upar landlord ka PAN lagega, aur ye threshold itna kam hai ki practically sab pe apply hota hai.
