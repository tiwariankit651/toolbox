🎨 Color Palette Generator for Web Design — Website Ke Liye Colors Kaise Choose Kare

July 2026 · 8 min read

Agar aap website design kar rahe ho — chahe personal blog ho, freelance portfolio, ya client project — toh sabse pehla aur important decision hota hai: colors kaunse use kare? Galat colors choose karne se website unprofessional lagti hai, aur sahi colors se trust build hota hai instantly. Aaj hum detail mein samjhenge ki color palette kaise generate kare, color psychology kya hai, aur Indian websites ke liye best color schemes kaunse hain.

Color Psychology — Colors Ka Dimaag Pe Asar

Har color ek emotion trigger karta hai. Years of research se prove hua hai ki colors directly affect karte hain ki user kya feel karta hai aur kya action leta hai:

  • Blue (Neela) = Trust & Reliability — Isliye banks (SBI, HDFC), tech companies (Facebook, LinkedIn), aur government websites blue use karti hain. Agar aapka business trust pe depend karta hai, blue choose karo.
  • Red (Laal) = Urgency & Energy — Zomato, YouTube, Swiggy — sab red use karte hain. Yeh color attention grab karta hai aur action lene ko motivate karta hai. Sale banners aur CTA buttons ke liye perfect.
  • Green (Hara) = Growth & Health — PhonePe, WhatsApp, aur health-related websites green prefer karti hain. Freshness, nature, aur prosperity represent karta hai.
  • Orange = Friendly & Affordable — Amazon, Flipkart ke sale sections mein orange dominate karta hai. Approachable feel deta hai without being too aggressive.
  • Purple = Premium & Creative — Cadbury, Byju's — purple luxury aur creativity ka signal hai. Premium products ke liye ideal.
  • Yellow = Optimism & Warmth — Attention grab karta hai but carefully use karo — too much yellow eye strain deta hai.

Brand Colors Kaise Pick Kare — Step by Step

  1. Apna brand personality define karo — Kya aap trustworthy dikhna chahte ho? Fun? Premium? Yeh decide karo pehle.
  2. Primary color choose karo — Ek main color jo aapki brand identity represent kare. Logo, headers, aur buttons mein use hoga.
  3. Color scheme type select karo — Complementary, analogous, ya triadic (neeche detail mein).
  4. Palette generate karo — Hamare Color Picker Tool mein ek color dalo, baaki automatically generate ho jayenge.
  5. Contrast check karo — Contrast Checker se verify karo ki text readable hai ya nahi.

Color Schemes Explained Simply

1. Complementary Colors (Opposite Wale)

Color wheel pe ek dusre ke exactly opposite — jaise blue aur orange, red aur green. High contrast deta hai aur elements ko pop karta hai. CTA buttons ke liye perfect — agar background blue hai toh button orange rakho.

2. Analogous Colors (Padosi Wale)

Color wheel pe ek dusre ke bagal mein — jaise blue, blue-green, green. Harmonious aur soothing lagta hai. Nature-inspired websites, wellness brands ke liye ideal. Contrast kam hota hai, toh text ke liye dark shade use karo.

3. Triadic Colors (Triangle Wale)

Color wheel pe equally spaced — jaise red, yellow, blue. Vibrant aur balanced feel. Children's websites, creative agencies ke liye great. Ek dominant rakho aur baaki accent ke liye use karo.

5 Popular Color Schemes for Indian Websites

  1. Saffron + White + Navy Blue — Patriotic feel, government projects, educational platforms. Trust aur authority convey karta hai.
  2. Deep Green + Gold — Financial services, investment platforms. Prosperity aur growth ka signal.
  3. Bright Red + White + Dark Grey — Food delivery, e-commerce, festive sales. Diwali/Holi sales ke liye perfect.
  4. Purple + Light Lavender + White — EdTech, premium services, women-focused brands. Modern aur premium feel.
  5. Teal + Coral + Off-White — Startups, creative agencies, personal brands. Young Indian audience ke liye ideal.

Accessibility — Contrast Check Zaroori Hai

Sundar colors choose karna kaafi nahi — readable bhi hona chahiye. WCAG guidelines ke according:

  • Normal text ke liye minimum contrast ratio 4.5:1
  • Large text (18px+ bold) ke liye minimum 3:1
  • Light grey text on white background — common mistake jo avoid karni chahiye

Contrast Checker mein text color aur background dalo — instantly pata chalega ki WCAG AA/AAA pass ho raha hai ya nahi.

Dark Mode Ke Liye Colors

  • Pure black (#000000) avoid karo — dark grey (#1a1a2e ya #121212) use karo. Pure black harsh lagta hai.
  • Pure white text avoid karo — off-white (#e0e0e0) use karo. Kam eye strain.
  • Accent colors slightly desaturate karo — bright neon dark background pe glare karte hain.
  • Elevation ke liye lighter shades — cards ko background se thoda lighter rakho.

Common Mistakes Avoid Karo

  • Too many colors — Maximum 3-4 (1 primary, 1 secondary, 1-2 accent). Zyada colors chaotic lagte hain.
  • Random picking — Hamesha color wheel ya generator use karo.
  • Color blindness ignore karna — 8% Indian males color blind hain. Red-green alone pe rely mat karo.
  • 60-30-10 rule bhoolna — 60% dominant, 30% secondary, 10% accent.
