📱 QR Code Kaise Banaye Free With Logo — Complete Guide 2026

July 2026 · 7 min read

Aaj ke digital India mein QR codes har jagah dikh rahe hain — chai ki tapri se lekar corporate offices tak. Agar aap soch rahe hain ki QR code kaise banaye free mein, with your own logo and custom colors, toh yeh guide aapke liye hai. Koi app install karne ki zaroorat nahi, koi signup nahi — seedha browser mein banao aur download karo.

QR Code Kya Hota Hai?

QR code (Quick Response code) ek 2D barcode hai jo information store karta hai. Jab koi ise phone camera se scan karta hai, toh instantly information open ho jaati hai — chahe wo website link ho, phone number ho, ya WiFi password. 1994 mein Japan mein invent hua tha, lekin India mein demonetization ke baad (2016) iska use explosion hua, especially UPI payments ke liye.

QR Code Kahan-Kahan Use Hota Hai?

  • UPI Payments — Dukaan pe "Scan & Pay" wala QR code. Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm sab support karte hain
  • Business Cards — Physical card pe QR code lagao, scan karte hi contact save ho jaye
  • Restaurant Menus — COVID ke baad digital menu QR codes standard ban gaye hain
  • Event Tickets — Concert, conference, ya movie tickets mein entry verification ke liye
  • WiFi Sharing — Guests ko password batane ki jagah QR scan karwa do, auto-connect ho jayega
  • Product Packaging — Scan karo toh product details, warranty info, ya review page khule
  • Education — Teachers assignment links ya study material QR se share karte hain

QR Code With Logo — Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?

Aapko lagta hoga ki logo lagane se QR code kharab ho jayega — lekin nahi! QR codes mein built-in error correction hota hai. Iska matlab hai ki 30% tak area damage ya covered ho, tab bhi code scan hota hai. Isliye center mein logo rakhna safe hai.

Tips for logo QR codes:

  • Logo size QR code ka 20-25% se zyada na ho
  • Simple logos better work karte hain — detailed logos mein scanning issues aa sakte hain
  • High error correction level (H) select karo jab logo use kar rahe ho
  • Logo ke around thoda white space rakho for better contrast

Color Customization Tips

Default black-white QR codes boring lagte hain. Apni brand colors use karo, lekin yeh dhyan rakho:

  • Dark foreground + light background — Yeh rule follow karo, ulta mat karo
  • Sufficient contrast — Light blue on white nahi chalega, scanner read nahi kar payega
  • Background white ya light hi rakho — Dark backgrounds se scanning problems aati hain
  • Test karo — Customize karne ke baad apne phone se scan karke check karo

WiFi QR Code — Guests Ke Liye Perfect

Ghar pe ya office mein guests aate hain toh WiFi password batana padta hai. WiFi QR code se yeh problem solve:

  1. QR generator mein "WiFi" type select karo
  2. Network name (SSID) enter karo
  3. Password daalo
  4. Security type choose karo (WPA/WPA2 mostly)
  5. Generate karo aur print karke wall pe laga do

Ab guest scan karega aur automatically WiFi connect ho jayega — no typing, no spelling mistakes!

vCard QR Code — Digital Business Card

Physical business cards kho jaati hain. vCard QR code scan karte hi aapka poora contact — name, phone, email, company, website — directly phone mein save ho jaata hai.

Networking events, conferences, ya client meetings mein bahut useful hai. Card pe print karo ya email signature mein lagao.

Batch QR Code Generation

Agar aapko multiple QR codes banana hain (jaise 50 products ke liye alag-alag), toh:

  • Ek-ek karke generate karo with consistent styling
  • Same color scheme aur logo use karo brand consistency ke liye
  • File naming convention follow karo (product-001.png, product-002.png)
  • SVG format mein save karo agar resize karna ho baad mein

Print Tips — Size Matters!

QR code print karte waqt size bahut important hai. Chhota QR code scan nahi hoga:

  • Minimum size: 2 cm × 2 cm — Isse chhota mat banao
  • Business card pe: 1.5-2 cm enough hai (close se scan hota hai)
  • Poster/banner pe: Scanning distance ke hisaab se size badhao
  • Rule of thumb: Scanning distance ÷ 10 = minimum QR size

Example: Agar 1 meter door se scan hona chahiye, toh QR code minimum 10 cm ka hona chahiye.

Common Mistakes Jo Avoid Karni Hain

  • ❌ QR code ko stretch mat karo — always square rakho
  • ❌ Low resolution mein print mat karo — pixelated QR scan nahi hota
  • ❌ QR ke around quiet zone (white border) hatao mat — scanner ko yeh chahiye
  • ❌ Inverted colors (white on dark) avoid karo — older phones struggle karte hain
  • ❌ Too much data mat daalo — zyada data = zyada complex = harder to scan

Kya QR Code Expire Hota Hai?

Nahi! Static QR codes kabhi expire nahi hote. Jo data encode hua hai wo permanently accessible hai. Lekin agar aapne URL encode kiya hai aur wo website band ho gayi, toh obviously link kaam nahi karega — QR code toh wahi rahega.
