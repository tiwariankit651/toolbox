📸 SSC CGL Photo Size 200x230 & Signature 150x200 — Upload Guide 2026

June 2026 · 4 min read

Every year, lakhs of candidates face form rejection because their photo or signature does not meet SSC's strict specifications. Whether you are applying for SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, or GD Constable, the photo and signature requirements remain the same. This guide will help you resize and compress your images correctly — for free, without any software installation.

SSC Photo Requirements 2026

  • Dimensions: 200 x 230 pixels (width x height)
  • File size: 4 KB to 100 KB
  • Format: JPEG / JPG only
  • Background: White or light-coloured
  • Face coverage: 50–75% of the frame
  • Recency: Taken within last 3 months

SSC Signature Requirements 2026

  • Dimensions: 150 x 200 pixels (width x height)
  • File size: 1 KB to 50 KB
  • Format: JPEG / JPG only
  • Background: White paper
  • Ink: Black or dark blue pen

Step-by-Step: Resize Signature to 150x200 Pixels

  1. Sign on white paper with a black pen and take a clear photo
  2. Crop the image to show only the signature
  3. Open the Image Resizer Tool
  4. Set width to 150 and height to 200 pixels
  5. Download the resized signature

Compress to Meet File Size Limits

After resizing, your file might still exceed the size limit. Use our Image Compressor to reduce the file size below 100 KB (photo) or 50 KB (signature) without losing visible quality.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Wrong aspect ratio — do not stretch or distort the image
  • Blurry photo — use a well-lit, focused image
  • Signature on coloured paper — always use plain white paper
  • File too large — compress after resizing
  • PNG format — SSC only accepts JPG/JPEG
