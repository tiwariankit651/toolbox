💹 SIP Calculator Guide

April 2026 · 3 min read

SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) is the best way to invest in mutual funds. Our SIP Calculator shows how your money grows.

How SIP Works

  • Invest a fixed amount every month
  • Money grows with compound interest
  • Even Rs. 500/month can grow significantly over 10-20 years

Example

Rs. 5,000/month at 12% for 10 years = Rs. 11.6 lakhs (invested Rs. 6 lakhs, returns Rs. 5.6 lakhs)
