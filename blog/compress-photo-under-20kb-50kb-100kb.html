🗜️ How to Compress Photo Under 20KB, 50KB, or 100KB — Free Online

June 2026 · 4 min read

Government exam forms in India have strict file size limits for photo uploads. SSC requires under 100 KB, IBPS needs under 50 KB for photos and under 20 KB for signatures, and many state exams have their own limits. If your photo exceeds the limit, the form simply will not accept it. Here is how to compress any image to your exact target size — for free.

Tips for Better Compression

  • Resize first, compress second: Use the Image Resizer to reduce dimensions before compressing. A 200x230 pixel image compresses much better than a 4000x3000 pixel one.
  • Use JPEG format: JPEG compresses much smaller than PNG for photographs
  • Plain background helps: White backgrounds compress better than complex ones
  • Avoid over-compression: If the image looks blurry, try a slightly higher target size

Why File Size Matters

Exam portals have upload limits for two reasons: server storage and page load speed during high-traffic registration periods. When crores of candidates upload photos simultaneously, smaller files prevent server crashes. That is why the limits are strict and non-negotiable.

What If My Photo Is Too Small?

Some forms also have a minimum file size (e.g., 20 KB). If your compressed image is below the minimum:

  • Increase the image dimensions slightly
  • Use a higher quality setting during compression
  • Ensure the original photo has good resolution
