📋 UPSC Form Photo & Signature Requirements 2026

June 2026 · 4 min read

Filling the UPSC Civil Services (IAS/IPS) application form requires uploading a photograph and signature that meet exact specifications. Every year, thousands of forms are rejected due to incorrect image dimensions or file size. This guide covers the exact UPSC photo and signature requirements and shows you how to resize them for free.

UPSC Photo Specifications 2026

  • Dimensions: 2 x 2.5 inches (approximately 600 x 750 pixels at 300 DPI)
  • Minimum pixel size: 200 x 230 pixels
  • File size: 20 KB to 300 KB
  • Format: JPEG / JPG
  • Background: Plain white or light grey
  • Face visibility: Both ears visible, no sunglasses
  • Recency: Taken within last 6 months

UPSC Signature Specifications 2026

  • Dimensions: 2 x 0.8 inches (approximately 600 x 240 pixels)
  • File size: 10 KB to 200 KB
  • Format: JPEG / JPG
  • Ink colour: Black ink on white paper
  • Style: Must match your ID proof signature

How to Resize Your Photo for UPSC

  1. Take a passport-style photo against a white background
  2. Open the FreeToolHubs Image Resizer
  3. Upload your photo and set dimensions to 600 x 750 pixels
  4. Download the resized JPEG file
  5. If file size exceeds 300 KB, use the Image Compressor to reduce it

How to Prepare Your Signature

  1. Sign on a clean white A4 paper using black ink pen
  2. Take a photo in good lighting — avoid shadows
  3. Crop tightly around the signature
  4. Use the Image Resizer to set dimensions to 600 x 240 pixels
  5. Compress if needed using the Image Compressor

Common Rejection Reasons

  • Photo is blurry or pixelated after resizing
  • Signature background is not white (yellowish paper)
  • File size exceeds the maximum limit
  • Wrong format (PNG instead of JPG)
  • Photo has coloured background or filters
