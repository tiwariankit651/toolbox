Exact Age Kyon Chahiye?

Bahut saari jagah pe sirf "25 years" likhna kaafi nahi hota. Aapko exact years + months + days chahiye:

  • Government forms — Aadhaar, passport, PAN card mein exact date of birth aur age maangte hain
  • Insurance — life insurance premium aapki exact age pe depend karta hai
  • Retirement planning — kitne saal baaki hain 58/60 tak? Exact calculation chahiye
  • School admission — cutoff date hoti hai (e.g., 31 March tak 6 years complete)
  • Competitive exams — UPSC, SSC mein age limit hoti hai (years + months)
  • Legal purposes — voting age 18, driving license 18, drinking 21

Age Calculate Karna Itna Tricky Kyon Hai?

Simple lagta hai — birth year minus current year. Lekin actually itna simple nahi hai:

  • Leap years — February 29 ko born hue toh? Har 4 saal mein extra day
  • Different month lengths — January 31 days, February 28/29, April 30
  • Birthday aaya ya nahi — agar birthday abhi nahi aaya toh 1 year minus
  • Specific date pe age — "1 January 2027 ko meri age kya hogi?"

Isliye manually calculate karna error-prone hai. Calculator use karo — instant aur accurate!
