⌨️ How to Improve Your Typing Speed — 7 Proven Tips

April 2026 · 4 min read

Whether you're preparing for competitive exams (SSC, banking, railways), applying for data entry jobs, or just want to be more productive — typing speed matters. The average typing speed is 40 WPM, but with practice, you can reach 60-80 WPM or more.

First, check your current typing speed — then follow these tips to improve.

1. Learn the Home Row Position

Place your fingers on the home row keys: left hand on A-S-D-F and right hand on J-K-L-;. Your thumbs rest on the space bar. Every finger has assigned keys — learn which finger presses which key. This is the foundation of touch typing.

2. Stop Looking at the Keyboard

This is the hardest habit to break but the most important. Looking at the keyboard slows you down because your eyes constantly switch between screen and keys. Cover your keyboard with a cloth if needed — force yourself to type by feel.

3. Focus on Accuracy First, Speed Later

Many beginners try to type fast and make lots of errors. This actually slows you down because you spend time correcting mistakes. Type slowly but accurately — speed will naturally increase as your muscle memory develops.

4. Practice Daily — Even 15 Minutes

Consistency beats intensity. Practicing 15 minutes every day is better than 2 hours once a week. Use our Typing Speed Test daily to track your progress. Set a specific time — like right after morning tea.

5. Use All 10 Fingers

If you're a "hunt and peck" typist using 2-4 fingers, switching to all 10 fingers will dramatically increase your speed. It feels slow at first, but within 2-3 weeks, you'll be faster than before.

6. Maintain Good Posture

  • Sit straight with feet flat on the floor
  • Keep your wrists slightly elevated — don't rest them on the desk
  • Screen should be at eye level
  • Elbows at 90-degree angle

Good posture reduces fatigue and prevents wrist pain during long typing sessions.

7. Set Goals and Track Progress

Measure your WPM weekly and set realistic goals:

  • Beginner: 20-30 WPM → Target 40 WPM in 2 weeks
  • Intermediate: 40-50 WPM → Target 60 WPM in 1 month
  • Advanced: 60+ WPM → Target 80 WPM in 2 months

Typing Speed Requirements for Exams

  • SSC CHSL: 35 WPM (English) / 30 WPM (Hindi)
  • SSC CGL: 35 WPM
  • Court Stenographer: 80-100 WPM
  • Data Entry Operator: 35-40 WPM
