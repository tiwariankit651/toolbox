📝 How to Summarize Articles Quickly

April 2026 · 3 min read

Reading long articles takes time. Whether you are studying for exams, researching a topic, or catching up on news, summarizing helps you extract the most important information quickly.

When to Use a Summarizer

  • Exam preparation — summarize textbook chapters into key points
  • Current affairs — get the gist of long news articles
  • Research — quickly understand academic papers
  • Work — summarize long emails and reports

How It Works

Our Article Summarizer uses extractive summarization — it scores each sentence based on important keywords and selects the most relevant ones. This works with any language including English, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, and more.

How to Use

  1. Paste your article or text
  2. Choose summary length (3, 5, 8, or 10 key points)
  3. Click Summarize
  4. Copy or download the summary

The tool also shows word count reduction — see how much reading time you saved!
