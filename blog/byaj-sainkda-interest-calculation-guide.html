💰 Sainkda Byaj Kaise Nikale — "Do Rupaye" Ka Asli Matlab

October 2026 · 9 min read

"Bhai, sirf do rupaye sainkda lagega." Sunne mein kuch bhi nahi lagta — do rupaye. Lekin ye saal ka 24% hai. Aur "paanch rupaye sainkda" ka matlab hai saal ka 60%.

Ye guide sainkda system ko samjhata hai, uska asli annual cost nikalta hai, aur wo fark dikhata hai jo simple aur compound byaj mein hota hai — numbers ke saath, theory ke bina.

Sainkda Kya Hai

"Sainkda" shabd "sainkra" se aaya hai — sau, hundred. Matlab ₹100 par kitna rupaya.

Par asli baat ye hai: sainkda almost hamesha per month hota hai, per year nahi. Toh:

₹1 sainkda = 1% monthly = 12% saal
₹2 sainkda = 2% monthly = 24% saal
₹3 sainkda = 3% monthly = 36% saal
₹5 sainkda = 5% monthly = 60% saal
₹10 sainkda = 10% monthly = 120% saal

Kuch jagah "paisa" mein bhi bolte hain — "aath aane sainkda" matlab 50 paise per 100 per month, jo 0.5% monthly aur 6% annual hai.

💡 Hamesha confirm karo ki rate monthly hai ya yearly. Fark 12 guna ka hai. Agar koi "do rupaye sainkda saalana" bole toh wo sirf 2% annual hai — bahut sasta. Par aam bol-chaal mein sainkda monthly hi hota hai.

Hisaab — Ek Example

Ramesh ne ₹50,000 udhaar liya, ₹2 sainkda, ek saal ke liye.

Simple Byaj

Mahine ka byaj = 50,000 × 2% = ₹1,000
12 mahine ka   = 1,000 × 12 = ₹12,000
Total dena      = 50,000 + 12,000 = ₹62,000

Compound Byaj (monthly)

Agar byaj har mahine mool rakam mein jud jaye:

Total = 50,000 × (1.02)12 = ₹63,412
Byaj  = ₹13,412

Fark  = 13,412 − 12,000 = ₹1,412

Ek saal mein ₹1,412 ka fark. Chhota lagta hai — par do saal mein ye ₹4,000 se zyada ho jaata hai, aur teen saal mein ₹9,000 se. Compound time ke saath tez bhaagta hai.

Effective Annual Rate — Wo Number Jo Koi Nahi Batata

₹2 sainkda ka "nominal" annual rate 24% hai (2% × 12). Par agar byaj monthly compound hota hai, toh asli cost zyada hai:

Effective rate = (1.02)12 − 1 = 26.82%

Ye wo number hai jisse aap bank loan se compare kar sakte ho. Ek personal loan 14% par mehnga lagta hai — par ₹2 sainkda uska lagbhag do guna hai.

₹10 sainkda par compound hone se saal mein rakam teen guna ho jaati hai. Ye wo situation hai jisme log phas jaate hain.

Udhaar Lene Se Pehle Kya Puchhna Chahiye

Ye chaar sawaal, aur jawab likhwa lo:

  1. Rate monthly hai ya yearly? — 12 guna ka fark hai
  2. Simple ya compound? — aur compound kitne-kitne time mein
  3. Byaj kab dena hai? — har mahine alag, ya end mein sab ek saath (end mein dene par compound jaisa effect banta hai)
  4. Pehle chuka doon toh kya? — kuch lenders poore period ka byaj maangte hain chahe aap jaldi chuka do

Humara byaj calculator sainkda rate seedha leta hai aur dono — simple aur compound — ka poora month-by-month schedule dikha deta hai. Jo number baat karte waqt kaam aata hai wahi dikhta hai.

Jahan Sainkda Aaj Bhi Chalta Hai

  • Gaon ka udhaar — kheti ke season mein, fasal ke baad chukane ki shart par
  • Dukaan ka maal — wholesaler se udhaar maal, mahine ke hisaab se byaj
  • Gold loan local jeweller se — aksar sainkda mein quote hota hai
  • Committee aur chit fund — internal byaj ka hisaab
  • Hand loan dost-rishtedaron mein — chhoti rakam, mahine ka hisaab

Legal Side — Ek Zaroori Baat

Money lending India mein regulated hai. Zyadatar states mein professional money lending ke liye licence chahiye, aur maximum interest rate bhi decide hota hai. Bina licence bahut zyada rate par udhaar dena kai states mein illegal hai.

Agar aapse bahut zyada rate maanga ja raha hai, ya blank cheque / property papers security mein maange ja rahe hain, toh legal advice lein — ye tool sirf hisaab karta hai, rate legal hai ya nahi ye nahi batata.

Student Ke Liye — Formula

Exam mein ye formulas chahiye hote hain:

Simple Interest
SI = P × R × T / 100

Compound Interest
A = P × (1 + R/100)T
CI = A − P

Effective annual rate
EAR = (1 + r)n − 1

Sainkda wale sawaalon mein R monthly rate hota hai aur T mahine mein. Agar T saal mein diya hai toh mahine mein badlo, ya R ko 12 se multiply karo — dono ek hi jawab denge simple interest mein, par compound mein nahi.

FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions

2 rupaye sainkda ka matlab kya hai?

₹100 par ₹2 per month, matlab 2% monthly. Saal ka 24% simple interest. Agar compound ho toh effective annual rate 26.82% ban jaata hai.

Sainkda monthly hota hai ya yearly?

Aam bol-chaal mein sainkda monthly hota hai. Phir bhi lene-dene se pehle confirm karna chahiye kyunki fark 12 guna ka hai — ₹2 sainkda monthly 24% saal hai, par yearly maano toh sirf 2%.

Simple aur compound byaj mein kya fark hai?

Simple byaj sirf mool rakam par lagta hai. Compound mein pichhle byaj par bhi byaj lagta hai. ₹50,000 par ₹2 sainkda ek saal mein simple ₹12,000 banta hai aur compound ₹13,412 — ₹1,412 ka fark. Lambe time mein ye fark bahut badh jaata hai.

Kya sainkda rate legal hai?

Money lending ke rules state ke hisaab se alag hain. Zyadatar states mein professional money lending ke liye licence chahiye aur maximum rate decide hota hai. Bahut zyada rate par legal advice lena chahiye.

Effective annual rate kya hai?

Jab byaj compound hota hai toh saal ke end mein actual cost nominal rate se zyada hoti hai. 2% monthly ka nominal annual 24% hai par effective 26.82%, kyunki har mahine ka byaj aage byaj kamata hai.

Summary

Sainkda ka ek hi rule yaad rakho: sainkda × 12 = annual percent. ₹2 sainkda 24% hai, ₹5 sainkda 60% hai. Aur agar compound ho toh usse bhi zyada. Rate sunte waqt usko turant annual mein badlo — tabhi pata chalta hai ki wo sasta hai ya mehnga.
