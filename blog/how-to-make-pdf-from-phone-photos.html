📄 How to Make PDF from Phone Photos — Free Guide

May 2026 · 4 min read

Need to submit scanned documents, assignment pages, or ID proofs as a PDF? You do not need to install any app or pay for Adobe Acrobat. You can convert photos taken on your phone directly into a professional PDF file using a free browser-based tool. This guide shows you exactly how to do it step by step.

Why Convert Photos to PDF?

There are many situations where you need images in PDF format:

  • Government forms — many portals only accept PDF uploads for documents
  • College assignments — professors often require PDF submissions
  • Job applications — HR departments prefer PDF resumes and certificates
  • Legal documents — PDFs maintain formatting across all devices
  • Email attachments — one PDF is cleaner than 10 separate image files
  • Printing — PDFs ensure consistent print quality and page layout

Step-by-Step: Convert Phone Photos to PDF

  1. Open the FreeToolHubs Image to PDF tool on your phone browser (Chrome, Safari, or any browser)
  2. Tap the upload area or "Choose Files" button
  3. Select the photos you want to convert — you can select multiple images at once
  4. Rearrange the order by dragging images if needed (first image becomes first page)
  5. Choose page size — A4 is standard for documents, Letter for US format
  6. Select orientation — Portrait for documents, Landscape for wide photos
  7. Click "Convert to PDF" and wait a few seconds
  8. Download your PDF — it is saved directly to your phone

Tips for Better Results

Before Taking Photos

  • Good lighting — use natural light or a desk lamp, avoid shadows
  • Flat surface — place documents on a flat, dark background
  • Steady hands — hold your phone parallel to the document to avoid distortion
  • Full frame — capture the entire document with minimal background

Before Converting

  • Check order — make sure pages are in the correct sequence
  • Compress if needed — if your photos are very large (5MB+), use our Image Compressor first to reduce file size
  • Crop unnecessary parts — remove extra background before converting
