📄 How to Merge PDF Files Online for Free

April 2026 · 3 min read

Need to combine multiple PDF files into a single document? Whether you are merging certificates for a job application, combining assignment pages, or creating a single report from multiple files, here is how to do it for free.

Why Merge PDFs?

  • Job applications — combine resume, cover letter, and certificates into one file
  • University submissions — merge assignment pages, references, and appendix
  • Government forms — combine supporting documents into a single upload
  • Business reports — merge monthly reports into quarterly or annual documents

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Open FreeToolHubs PDF Merger
  2. Click or drag and drop to add your PDF files
  3. Rearrange the order by dragging files up or down
  4. Click "Merge PDFs"
  5. Download your combined PDF file

Privacy and Security

Unlike most online PDF tools, FreeToolHubs processes everything in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server. This is especially important when merging sensitive documents like Aadhaar cards, bank statements, or medical records.

Tips for Best Results

  • Make sure all PDFs are in the correct order before merging
  • If the merged file is too large, use our PDF Compressor to reduce the size
  • Need to extract specific pages first? Use our PDF Splitter
  • Want to add text or annotations? Use our PDF Add Text tool after merging

Common Use Cases

Students often need to merge scanned pages of handwritten assignments. Professionals merge invoices, contracts, and reports. Government job applicants combine all required documents into a single PDF for online submission portals that accept only one file.
