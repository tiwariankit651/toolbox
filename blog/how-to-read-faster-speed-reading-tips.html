📖 How to Read Faster — Speed Reading Tips

April 2026 · 3 min read

The average person reads at 200-250 words per minute. With practice, you can double or triple that speed without losing comprehension.

Speed Reading Tips

  • Stop subvocalizing — do not read words aloud in your head
  • Use a pointer — follow text with your finger or cursor
  • Read in chunks — group 3-4 words together instead of reading one by one
  • Do not re-read — keep moving forward, trust your brain
  • Practice daily — even 10 minutes of speed reading practice helps
