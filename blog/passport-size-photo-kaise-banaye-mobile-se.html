📸 Passport Size Photo Kaise Banaye Mobile Se — Free Guide

July 2026 · 7 min read

Studio jaake passport photo khichwana — ₹50-100 lagta hai har baar. Aur jab urgently chahiye (raat ko 11 baje form ki last date hai), tab studio band hota hai! Lekin agar tumhare paas smartphone hai, toh ghar pe hi professional quality passport photo bana sakte ho — bilkul free.

Is guide mein main tumhe bataunga — exact Indian passport photo specifications, ghar pe photo kaise lo, resize kaise karo, aur ek 4x6 inch sheet pe 8 photos kaise print karo.

Indian Passport Photo Specifications (2026)

India mein passport photo ke liye ye official requirements hain:

Parameter Requirement
Size3.5 cm × 4.5 cm (width × height)
BackgroundPlain white or light grey
Face CoverageFace 70-80% of frame (chin to crown)
ExpressionNeutral, mouth closed, eyes open
GlassesNo glasses (since 2021 rule)
Head PositionStraight, facing camera directly
EarsBoth ears visible (preferred)
File Size (online)10KB to 300KB (JPEG format)

Ghar Pe Photo Kaise Lo — Mobile Photography Tips

1. Background Setup

  • White wall ke saamne khade ho — ye sabse easy hai
  • White wall nahi hai? White bedsheet ya chart paper tape karo wall pe
  • Background pe koi shadow nahi honi chahiye — wall se 1-2 feet door khade ho

2. Lighting (Sabse Important!)

  • Natural light best hai — Window ke saamne khade ho (din mein 10am-3pm)
  • Direct sunlight avoid karo — harsh shadows aati hain
  • Face pe even lighting honi chahiye — ek side dark nahi honi chahiye
  • Agar natural light nahi hai, toh 2 table lamps use karo — ek left, ek right

3. Camera Settings

  • Front camera NAHI — back camera use karo (better quality)
  • Kisi se photo khichwa lo ya phone ko shelf/tripod pe rakho with timer
  • Portrait mode OFF rakho — background blur nahi chahiye
  • Flash OFF — natural lighting use karo
  • HDR ON kar sakte ho — better exposure milega

4. Pose & Expression

  • Seedha camera mein dekho — na left, na right, na upar, na neeche
  • Mouth band rakho, halka natural expression (na smile, na frown)
  • Shoulders straight aur relaxed
  • Baal face se hatao — forehead aur ears visible hone chahiye

Photo Resize Kaise Kare — Step by Step

Photo le liya, ab passport size mein crop aur resize karna hai:

  1. FreeToolHubs Image Resizer open karo
  2. Apni photo upload karo
  3. Width: 350 px, Height: 450 px set karo (ye 3.5×4.5 cm at 254 DPI hai)
  4. Crop tool se face center mein adjust karo — chin se thoda neeche se crown tak
  5. Download karo JPEG format mein

Online Form Ke Liye File Size Adjust Karna

Bahut se government portals file size limit rakhte hain (usually 10KB-300KB). Agar tumhari photo zyada heavy hai:

  1. Image Compressor tool open karo
  2. Photo upload karo
  3. Target size set karo (e.g., under 200KB)
  4. Compressed photo download karo

Background White Nahi Hai?

Agar tumhare peeche white wall nahi thi ya background mein kuch aa gaya, toh:

  1. Background Changer tool use karo
  2. Photo upload karo — tool automatically background remove karega
  3. White background select karo
  4. Download karo — clean passport photo ready!

4×6 Sheet Pe 8 Photos Print Kaise Kare

Studio wale ek 4×6 inch sheet pe 8 passport photos arrange karke dete hain. Tum bhi ye ghar pe kar sakte ho:

Method: Manual Arrangement

  1. MS Paint ya any image editor open karo
  2. Canvas size set karo: 1800 × 1200 pixels (4×6 inch at 300 DPI)
  3. Apni passport photo (350×450 px) ko 8 baar paste karo — 4 columns × 2 rows
  4. Thoda gap rakho photos ke beech (cutting ke liye)
  5. Save karo aur nearest photo print shop pe ₹5-10 mein print karwa lo

Pro tip: Photo print shop pe jaake bolo "ye file 4×6 glossy pe print kar do" — ₹5-10 mein 8 passport photos mil jayengi!

Common Rejection Reasons — Ye Galtiyan Mat Karo

Government offices aur online portals pe photo reject hone ke common reasons:

  • ❌ Background white nahi hai — Cream, blue, ya shadow wala background reject hota hai
  • ❌ Face too small/big — Face photo ka 70-80% hona chahiye
  • ❌ Glasses pehne hain — 2021 se glasses allowed nahi hain passport photo mein
  • ❌ Smile kar rahe ho — Neutral expression chahiye, teeth nahi dikhne chahiye
  • ❌ Photo blurry hai — Sharp, clear photo chahiye. Steady haath ya tripod use karo
  • ❌ Shadows on face — Even lighting chahiye, ek side dark nahi honi chahiye
  • ❌ Red eyes — Flash avoid karo, natural light use karo
  • ❌ Wrong dimensions — Exactly 3.5×4.5 cm hona chahiye, na zyada na kam
  • ❌ File size limit exceed — Compress karo agar portal limit se zyada hai

Quick Checklist Before Submitting

  • ☑️ Background plain white hai
  • ☑️ Face clearly visible hai, 70-80% frame cover kar raha hai
  • ☑️ No glasses, no smile, eyes open
  • ☑️ Photo sharp hai, blur nahi
  • ☑️ Size exactly 3.5×4.5 cm (ya portal requirement ke according)
  • ☑️ File size limit ke andar hai
  • ☑️ JPEG format mein hai
  • ☑️ Recent photo hai (6 months se purani nahi)
