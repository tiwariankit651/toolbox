📐 Bigha, Katha Aur Gaj — Kaunsa Kitna, State Ke Hisaab Se

October 2026 · 10 min read

"Das bigha zameen hai." Sunne mein clear lagta hai. Par asli sawaal ye hai: kahan ki das bigha?

Kyunki Uttar Pradesh mein das bigha matlab 2,72,250 square feet hai. Uttarakhand ke pahad mein wahi das bigha sirf 68,062 square feet. Chaar guna ka fark — ek hi shabd, ek hi desh.

Ye guide poori state-wise table deti hai, batati hai ki ye fark kyun hai, aur sabse zaroori — zameen kharidne se pehle kya verify karna chahiye.

Sabse Pehle: Bigha Standardised Nahi Hai

Acre poori duniya mein exactly 43,560 square feet hai. Hectare 10,000 square metre. Ye fixed hain, kisi bhi desh mein.

Bigha aisa nahi hai. Isko kisi central authority ne kabhi define nahi kiya. Ye purane revenue surveys se aaya hai, jinme har kshetra ne apna measuring rod use kiya — aur wo definitions aaj bhi land records mein chal rahi hain.

Isliye jab aap Google pe "1 bigha = ?" search karte ho, aur jawab milta hai 27,225 square feet — wo North Indian pucca bigha hai. Lucknow mein bilkul sahi. Kolkata, Shimla ya Bhopal mein bilkul galat.

Teen Cheezein Jo Log Miss Kar Dete Hain

1. Pucca Aur Kaccha Bigha

Kai states mein do live conventions hain. Pucca bada, official measure hai; kaccha chhota local measure — aksar aadha ya tihai.

Rajasthan mein ye fark hai: pucca 27,225 sq ft, kaccha 17,424 sq ft. Toh "das bigha" do bilkul alag plots ho sakte hain — lagbhag 1 lakh square feet ka fark.

Isliye seller se seedha puchhna chahiye: "pucca bigha ya kaccha?"

2. Ek Hi State Mein Pahad Aur Maidan Ka Fark

Uttarakhand sabse clear example hai. Pauri jaise pahadi zile mein bigha 6,806 sq ft hai. Haridwar aur maidani ilaakon mein 17,424 sq ft. Dhai guna fark, same state.

Uttar Pradesh mein bhi western aur eastern districts mein convention badalti hai. Toh state se zyada district matter karta hai.

3. Katha Ka Trap — Bengal Vs Assam

Ye sabse zyada confuse karne wala hai, aur dono states padosi hain.

West Bengal: 1 bigha = 20 katha → 1 katha = 720 sq ft
Assam:       1 bigha = 5 katha  → 1 katha = 2,880 sq ft

Dono ka bigha same hai — 14,400 sq ft. Par Assam ka katha Bengal ke katha se chaar guna bada hai. Agar deal katha mein quote hui hai toh pehle wahi pin karo, bigha baad mein.

Bihar mein bhi katha hai — 1,361.25 sq ft, 20 katha per bigha — aur wo district se district thoda vary karta hai.

Zameen Kharidne Se Pehle — Verification Checklist

  1. Seller se puchho: kaunsa bigha? Pucca, kaccha, pahadi ya maidani — naam se.
  2. Record mein kya likha hai? Land revenue record (khata-khatauni, 7/12, RTC) mein area aksar hectare ya square metre mein hota hai — wahi asli figure hai, bigha nahi.
  3. Sub-unit pin karo. Agar deal katha, biswa ya guntha mein hai toh us district ka sub-unit confirm karo.
  4. Square feet mein convert karke price per sq ft nikalo. Tabhi do plots compare ho sakte hain.
  5. Licensed surveyor se naap karao bade transactions mein. Paper ka area aur zameen ka area alag ho sakta hai.
  6. Mutation ke waqt record ka area use hoga, aapki calculation nahi.

Humara desi units converter pehle state puchhta hai, phir convert karta hai — aur jahan do conventions hain wahan dono dikhata hai. Saath mein square feet aur acre bhi, jo paperwork mein lagta hai.

Weight Units — Mandi Aur Jewellery

Land ke saath ye bhi roz kaam aate hain:

1 quintal = 100 kg      (fixed — mandi rates isme hote hain)
1 tonne   = 1,000 kg = 10 quintal
1 maund   = 37.324 kg = 40 seer
1 seer    = 0.933 kg   (lagbhag ek kilo)
1 tola    = 11.6638 g (gold ka standard)
1 pav     = 250 g

Quintal fixed hai, isliye mandi rate "₹2,200 per quintal" ka matlab ₹22 per kg hota hai — simple. Maund purane zamane mein regionally vary karta tha; aaj 37.324 kg standard maana jaata hai.

FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions

1 bigha kitne square feet hota hai?

Ye state par depend karta hai, koi ek national figure nahi hai. Uttar Pradesh aur Bihar mein 27,225 square feet, West Bengal mein 14,400, Gujarat mein 17,424, Punjab Haryana mein 21,780, Himachal mein 8,712 aur Uttarakhand ke pahadi zilon mein sirf 6,806 square feet.

1 acre mein kitne bigha hote hain?

State ke hisaab se alag. Uttar Pradesh, Bihar aur pucca Rajasthan mein lagbhag 1.6 bigha, Punjab Haryana mein theek 2, Gujarat mein 2.5, West Bengal aur Assam mein 3.025, Madhya Pradesh mein 3.63, Himachal mein 5 aur Uttarakhand ke pahad mein 6.4 bigha.

Bigha ka size kyun badalta hai?

Kyunki bigha metric system se bhi purana hai aur India ke current state boundaries se bhi. Har kshetra ke revenue survey ne apna measuring rod use kiya aur wahi definition records mein reh gayi. Acre ki tarah isko kabhi standardise nahi kiya gaya.

Pucca aur kaccha bigha mein kya fark hai?

Pucca bada official measure hota hai, kaccha chhota local measure — aksar aadha ya tihai. Rajasthan mein ye fark 27,225 aur 17,424 square feet ka hai, toh das bigha do bilkul alag plots ho sakte hain.

Kya ye figures registration ke liye use kar sakte hain?

Nahi. Ye commonly-cited reference values hain, legal standard nahi. Registration, mutation ya loan ke liye wahi area maanya hai jo district land revenue office ke record mein hai ya licensed surveyor ne naapa hai.

Kaunse land units fixed hain?

Square yard ya gaj hamesha 9 square feet, guntha 1,089, kanal 5,445, marla 272.25, cent aur shatak 435.6, aur killa matlab acre hi hota hai. Ye vary nahi karte, isliye paperwork mein inko prefer kiya jaata hai.

Summary

Ek line mein: bigha ek shabd hai, ek naap nahi. 1 acre = 1.6 bigha wala popular figure sirf North Indian pucca bigha ke liye sahi hai, aur Bengal, MP, Himachal ya Uttarakhand mein aadhe se zyada galat ho jaata hai.

Isliye teen cheezein karo: state aur district confirm karo, record ka hectare/square metre figure dekho, aur square feet mein convert karke price compare karo. Bigha baat karne ke liye theek hai, paisa dene ke liye square feet chahiye.
