📱 QR Code for Business Free — Business QR Code Kaise Banaye

July 2026 · 9 min read

Agar aap ek small business chalate ho — chahe dukaan ho, restaurant ho, freelance service ho, ya chai ki tapri — toh QR code aapke liye game changer hai. Sochiye, customer ko koi link yaad nahi karni padti, koi number type nahi karna padta. Bas scan kiya aur kaam ho gaya. Aaj ke time mein har choti-badi business ko QR code chahiye — UPI payment se lekar Google Maps location tak.

Is guide mein hum cover karenge ki QR code kyon zaroori hai, kitne types ke QR codes hote hain, apne logo ke saath QR kaise banaye, aur kahan-kahan place karna chahiye.

Har Small Business Ko QR Code Kyon Chahiye?

COVID ke baad se India mein contactless payments aur digital interaction explode ho gaya hai. Lekin QR code sirf payment ke liye nahi hai — yeh ek bridge hai aapke offline business aur online presence ke beech.

  • Contactless Payments — UPI QR code se customer seedha pay kar sakta hai. Cash handling kam, accounting easy
  • Digital Menu — Restaurant ya cafe mein printed menu ki jagah QR scan karke menu dikhao. Update karna bhi free
  • Google Maps Link — Customer ko directions dena hai? QR code scan karte hi Google Maps open ho jaaye
  • WhatsApp Chat Link — Bina number save kiye seedha WhatsApp pe message bhej sake customer
  • Digital Visiting Card — Paper card pe QR lagao, scan karne pe poori contact info save ho jaaye
  • WiFi Sharing — Cafe ya office mein WiFi password bolne ki jagah QR scan se connect kara do
  • Social Media Follow — Instagram, YouTube channel pe followers badhao bina link type karaye

QR Code Ke Types — Kaun Sa Kab Use Kare?

1. URL QR Code

Sabse common type. Koi bhi website link, Google Maps link, ya social media profile link QR mein convert karo. Customer scan kare aur seedha page open ho.

2. UPI Payment QR Code

India mein sabse zyada use hone wala QR type. Aapka UPI ID (like yourname@paytm) encode hota hai. Customer koi bhi UPI app se scan karke pay kar sakta hai — PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM sab work karte hain. Shops ke liye must-have hai.

3. vCard QR Code

Digital visiting card. Scan karne pe naam, phone, email, company, address sab ek click mein phone ke contacts mein save ho jaata hai. Networking events aur meetings mein bahut kaam aata hai.

4. WiFi QR Code

WiFi network name aur password encode hota hai. Customer ya guest scan kare aur automatically connect ho jaaye. Cafe, hotel, co-working space ke liye perfect.

5. WhatsApp QR Code

wa.me link generate hota hai with pre-filled message. Customer scan kare aur seedha aapke WhatsApp pe message aa jaaye. Enquiries, orders lene ke liye best.

Static vs Dynamic QR Code — Difference Samjho

Yeh decision important hai business ke liye:

  • Static QR — Content permanently fixed. Ek baar generate kiya, change nahi hoga. Free mein banta hai. Simple links ke liye perfect — UPI ID, social media profile, WiFi password
  • Dynamic QR — Content baad mein change kar sakte ho bina QR image change kiye. Lekin usually paid service chahiye. Restaurant menus ke liye useful (daily specials change hote hain)

Most small businesses ke liye static QR kaafi hai. Agar link change karna ho toh naya QR bana lo — free hai.

UPI Payment QR for Shops — Step by Step

Dukaan pe UPI QR lagana chahte ho? Simple steps:

  1. Apna UPI ID confirm karo (bank app ya PhonePe/GPay se check karo)
  2. QR generator mein UPI type select karo
  3. UPI ID enter karo (format: name@bankname)
  4. Merchant name add karo (customer ko dikhega payment ke time)
  5. Generate aur download karo
  6. Laminate karke counter pe lagao

Pro tip: Fixed amount wala QR bhi bana sakte ho — jaise ₹99 ka monthly subscription QR. Customer scan kare, amount pre-filled aaye.

QR Code Kahan Place Kare?

QR code banana half battle hai — sahi jagah lagana equally important:

  • Shop entrance / door — Google Maps review QR ya WhatsApp enquiry
  • Billing counter — UPI payment QR (laminated, clearly visible)
  • Bills / receipts — Feedback form ya loyalty program QR
  • Product packaging — Website link ya usage instructions video
  • Business cards — vCard QR (backside pe)
  • Restaurant tables — Menu QR (each table pe)
  • Vehicle / delivery bags — Order link ya website
  • Social media posts — WhatsApp order QR

Print Size Recommendations

QR code print karte time size matter karta hai — too small aur scan nahi hoga:

  • Business card — minimum 2 x 2 cm (close se scan hoga)
  • Table tent / counter — 5 x 5 cm (comfortable scanning distance)
  • Poster / banner — 10 x 10 cm ya zyada (door se bhi scan ho)
  • Billboard — 30 x 30 cm+ (lekin QR on billboards rarely work — logo scan kaise karega?)

Rule of thumb: Scanning distance ÷ 10 = minimum QR size. Matlab 30cm door se scan karna hai toh 3cm ka QR chahiye.

QR Code Design Tips for Business

  • Dark foreground + light background rakhna (inverted colors avoid karo)
  • Logo center mein rakho lekin 30% se zyada area cover na kare
  • Quiet zone (white border) hamesha maintain karo — crop mat karo
  • Generate karne ke baad ALWAYS test karo — 2-3 different phones se scan karke dekho
  • High error correction level use karo jab logo add karo (L → M → Q → H)

Common Mistakes Jo Business Owners Karte Hain

  • ❌ QR bahut chhota print karna — scan hi nahi hota
  • ❌ Low contrast colors — light grey on white background
  • ❌ QR pe koi instruction nahi — "Scan for Menu" likho, blank QR mat lagao
  • ❌ Broken link — QR bana diya lekin link expire ho gaya ya galat hai
  • ❌ Logo bahut bada — QR readable nahi rehta
