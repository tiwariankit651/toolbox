Reading Your WPM, KDPH and Pass/Fail Verdict Correctly

Why Net WPM is the number that decides your result

The results screen shows two speeds, and they are not interchangeable. Gross WPM is simply the characters you typed divided by five, then divided by the minutes elapsed — it ignores mistakes entirely. Net WPM takes that same figure and subtracts your error count before dividing, so every wrong character pulls the number down. Because the tool judges every exam preset against Net WPM (except the DEO preset), this is the figure that matters. It is clamped at zero, so a very sloppy run will not show a negative speed, but it can collapse to a value far below your Gross WPM.

A practical consequence: you can "feel fast" and still fail, because speed without accuracy is discounted. Watch the live Net WPM bar while typing, not the Gross one.

KDPH, and why the DEO preset is scored differently

Keystrokes per hour counts every key you press, including Backspace, and extrapolates it to an hourly rate. The SSC CGL DEO preset is the one case here judged on KDPH (the requirement is 8000, over a 15-minute run), mirroring how that exam is actually scored. Two things follow from the code: corrections inflate KDPH because backspaces count as keystrokes, and KDPH is computed from elapsed time, so a short burst can read very high early in the test before settling.

Settings that change the test, and what they do not do

Choosing an exam preset silently rewrites your duration, language and difficulty to match that exam, then applies a pass/fail rule — so set the preset first, before touching the other menus. Difficulty controls the passage pool: easy is lowercase words, medium adds punctuation and capitals, hard mixes numbers and symbols. Longer durations are built by shuffling and concatenating passages up to roughly 2000 characters, so you rarely run out of text.

  • Hindi mode relies entirely on your operating system's keyboard layout (Inscript or Remington); the tool does not transliterate Roman input into Devanagari.
  • Custom Text replaces the passage with whatever you paste, which is the cleanest way to rehearse a specific exam excerpt.

Checking the result and keeping a record

As you type, correct characters turn green and incorrect ones turn red with an underline, so you can see exactly where accuracy dropped rather than trusting the summary alone. Accuracy on the results card is correct characters divided by total typed, which is why pausing to fix an error helps your Net WPM but a spree of wrong keys hurts both numbers.

Your best runs are stored locally in your browser, ranked by WPM, with the top entries shown and a longer list retained. Nothing leaves your device. The downloadable PNG certificate restates Net WPM, Gross WPM, accuracy, KDPH, language, mode and date, and prints the pass/fail line only when a preset was active — a blank name defaults to a generic title, so fill it in before downloading if you want your own name on it.
