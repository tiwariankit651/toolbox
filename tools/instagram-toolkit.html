About This Tool

The Instagram Toolkit is a collection of creator utilities that run entirely in your browser. It does not download Instagram media — that isn't possible from a static web page because Instagram requires authentication, blocks cross-origin access, and prohibits scraping in its Terms of Service. Instead of pretending otherwise, this toolkit focuses on the parts of your Instagram workflow that genuinely work client-side.

Use the caption formatter to keep line breaks and blank lines that Instagram would normally collapse, the hashtag manager to organise curated and custom tag sets within the 30-tag limit, the image resizer to export photos at exact Instagram dimensions with a story safe-area guide, the bio builder to stay within the 150-character limit, and the grid planner to preview how your next posts will sit together in a 3-column feed.

To get a copy of your own Instagram content, Instagram provides an official "Download Your Information" feature in Accounts Center — that is the correct, permitted way to export your posts, reels, and stories.

Why does the caption formatter add invisible characters?

Instagram collapses consecutive blank lines, so your paragraph spacing disappears when you paste. The formatter inserts a zero-width space (U+200B) on each empty line so the spacing survives. It also counts characters against the 2,200 caption limit and copies the formatted version for you.

What Instagram sizes does the image resizer export, and what's the safe-area guide?

It exports Square 1080×1080, Portrait 1080×1350, Landscape 1080×566 and Story/Reel 1080×1920. You choose 'Crop to fill' or 'Letterbox (fit)', and JPEG or PNG with a quality slider. For the 1920-tall story format, a 'safe-area guide' shades the top (~250px) and bottom (~310px) UI zones so you don't place text where Instagram's interface covers it.

A 3-column grid planner lets you drag images to preview your feed.

The caption spacing trick and why it depends on a hidden character

The caption formatter solves a specific, maddening Instagram behaviour: when you paste a caption with blank lines between paragraphs, Instagram collapses them and your spacing vanishes. This tool works around it by walking through your text line by line and, on every line that is empty, inserting a single zero-width space (U+200B) — an invisible character that Instagram treats as content rather than whitespace, so the blank line survives. Knowing that is the key to using it well: the "blank" lines in your pasted caption are not truly empty, and if you later edit the caption inside Instagram and delete those lines, the spacing is gone again.

Character limits are enforced, not decorative

Each tool counts against Instagram's real limits as you type. The caption counter tracks the 2,200-character cap and turns red when you exceed it; the bio builder enforces 150 characters the same way. These are live counts, so trim before you copy rather than discovering the truncation after posting. The bio builder also preserves line breaks using the same zero-width-space technique, since Instagram bios strip multi-line formatting in the same way captions do.

What the image resizer outputs, and the crop decision

The resizer exports four exact Instagram dimensions — Square 1080×1080, Portrait 1080×1350, Landscape 1080×566, and Story/Reel 1080×1920 — as JPEG or PNG with a quality slider. The choice that matters most is Crop to fill versus Letterbox (fit):

  • Crop to fill covers the whole frame and centre-crops the overflow, so edges of a wide photo are lost in a portrait export.
  • Letterbox fits the entire image inside the frame on white, keeping everything visible but adding bars.
  • The story safe-area guide only appears on the 1080×1920 format: it shades the top ~250px and bottom ~310px where Instagram's interface sits, so you keep text out of covered zones.

Everything is drawn on a local canvas and never leaves the device, which also means the resizer re-encodes the file and drops the original's metadata.

Hashtag sets, the bio checklist, and the grid planner

The hashtag manager ships curated niche sets (study, motivation, travel, fitness and more), counts against the 30-tag maximum, de-duplicates on request, and lets you save your own sets to the browser's localStorage for reuse. The bio builder's checklist is a quick sanity pass — it verifies the text fits 150 characters, uses line breaks, contains an emoji, and references a link — nudging you toward a bio that reads well and converts. The grid planner lets you upload several images and drag to reorder them in a 3-column mock feed, so you can judge whether your next posts sit together coherently before publishing.

To export your own content, the correct route is Instagram's official "Download Your Information" in Accounts Center.
