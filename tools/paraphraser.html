About This Tool

This paraphrasing tool rewrites your text using a rule-based engine with a built-in synonym and phrase dictionary of several hundred entries. It offers five modes with genuinely different behaviour — Standard (synonym substitution), Simple (replaces difficult words with easier ones), Formal (expands contractions and swaps informal words for formal equivalents), Shorten (removes filler words and redundant phrases), and Expand (adds connective phrasing). A light/medium/heavy strength control decides how aggressively words are changed, and a side-by-side diff shows exactly what was altered — you can click any changed word to choose a different synonym. It is a dictionary tool, not an AI model, so output always needs human review. All processing happens in your browser — no data is sent anywhere.
