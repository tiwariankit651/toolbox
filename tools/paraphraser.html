About This Tool

This paraphrasing tool rewrites your text using rule-based synonym replacement and sentence restructuring. It offers five modes — Standard (balanced rewording), Fluent (natural flow), Creative (maximum variation), Formal (professional tone), and Simple (easy-to-read). The engine uses 100+ word mappings and clause reordering to produce unique output. All processing happens in your browser — no data is sent anywhere.
