About This Tool

When you take a photo, your phone embeds hidden data (EXIF metadata) including GPS location, camera model, date/time, and more. Sharing photos online with this data can expose your home address. This tool strips all metadata by re-drawing the image on a clean canvas. Supports multiple files at once. Your photos never leave your device.

What Gets Removed

  • GPS location coordinates
  • Camera make and model
  • Date and time taken
  • Lens information
  • All other EXIF, IPTC, and XMP data
