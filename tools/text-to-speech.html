🗣️ Text to Speech

Enter text below and click Speak. Uses your browser's built-in speech engine.

📌 Pronunciation Bookmarks

How to Use This Tool

  1. Step 1: Eyes tired from reading? Paste or type the text you want to hear
  2. Step 2: Pick a voice — there are different accents and male/female options
  3. Step 3: Adjust the speed — slow it down for learning, speed it up for quick listening
  4. Step 4: Hit Play and sit back while the computer reads it to you
  5. Step 5: Download the audio if you want to listen later — like on your commute

Common Use Cases

  • Listening to articles or documents while multitasking
  • Proofreading written content by hearing it spoken aloud
  • Creating audio versions of written content for accessibility
  • Practicing pronunciation of text in different languages
  • Generating voiceovers for presentations or videos

About This Tool

Convert any text to speech using your browser's built-in speech synthesis engine. Supports multiple voices and languages (depends on your operating system). Adjust speaking speed (0.5x to 2x) and pitch. Features play, pause, resume, and stop controls. Perfect for proofreading (hear your text read aloud to catch errors), language learning (hear correct pronunciation), accessibility (for visually impaired users), and listening to articles or study notes while doing other tasks. No external API — works offline.
