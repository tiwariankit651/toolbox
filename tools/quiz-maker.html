How to Use This Tool

You know how boring it is to just "read and revise"? Taking a quiz is 10x more effective — your brain actually has to recall the answer. Plus it's kinda fun when there's a timer ticking 😅 Here's how:

  1. Pick a subject — Indian GK, Science, SSC PYQ, Current Affairs, English... 19 subjects with 200+ questions ready.
  2. Set your timer — 30 seconds per question is the sweet spot. Or go no-limit if you're just learning.
  3. Answer questions — click your answer. Right = green, wrong = red + you see the explanation.
  4. Get your score — percentage, grade, and it saves to your leaderboard automatically.
  5. Create your own quiz — add questions, export as JSON/PDF, or share via URL with friends.
