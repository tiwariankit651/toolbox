    How to Use This Tool

    First job interview coming up and you don't have a resume? Or your current one looks like it was made in MS Paint? Don't worry — you can build a clean, professional PDF resume in under 5 minutes here. No Canva, no templates that ask you to pay at the end.

    1. Pick a template — Classic, Modern, Minimal, Creative, or Executive. Each looks different, all look professional.
    2. Fill in your details — name, email, phone, LinkedIn. Add a photo if you want.
    3. Add experience & education — click "+ Add" for each entry. Include your key responsibilities.
    4. List your skills — rate them 1-5 stars. They show up as nice dots on the PDF.
    5. Generate & Download — one click. Clean PDF ready to send to recruiters.

    The PDF is ATS-friendly — meaning those automated systems companies use to filter resumes won't reject yours. Standard fonts, clean formatting, proper sections. You're good to go.
