About This Tool

Build a professional PDF resume in minutes with 3 template styles: Classic (clean with blue accents), Modern (colored header bar), and Minimal (simple elegant). Customize accent color. Fill in personal info, LinkedIn/portfolio URLs, professional summary, skills, work experience (multiple entries with responsibilities), education (multiple entries with scores), certifications, and languages. The generated PDF is well-formatted with proper sections, fonts, and spacing. Perfect for freshers, experienced professionals, and anyone applying for jobs.
