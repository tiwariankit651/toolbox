💰 Budget / Expense Tracker

Track your monthly income and expenses. Auto-saves to browser.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Income add karo: Month end pe pata nahi chalta paisa kahan gaya? Pehle income sources add karo — salary, freelance, etc.
  2. Expenses enter karo: Rent, food, transport, subscriptions — category wise sab daalo
  3. Budget limits set karo: Har category ke liye limit set karo — "food pe ₹8000 se zyada nahi"
  4. Visual breakdown dekho: Charts mein clearly dikhega — paisa kahan ja raha hai
  5. Savings track karo: Goal set karo aur progress dekho — vacation fund, emergency fund, kuch bhi

Common Use Cases

  • Monthly expenses track karna hai? Sab ek jagah record karo
  • Unnecessary subscriptions identify karne hai? Category wise spending dekho
  • Roommates ke saath expenses split karna hai? Track and divide
  • Vacation ke liye save kar rahe ho? Goal set karo, progress dekho
  • Salary aate hi khatam ho jati hai? Spending patterns samjho aur control karo

About This Tool

Track your monthly income and expenses with this simple budget manager. Add entries with descriptions, amounts, and categories (Food, Rent, Transport, Shopping, Bills, Health, Education, Entertainment). See your total income, total expenses, and remaining balance at a glance. All data saves automatically in your browser's local storage — no account needed. Perfect for students managing hostel expenses, professionals tracking monthly budgets, and anyone who wants to understand where their money goes.
