About This Tool

Add the date of photograph on your images — required by many government exam forms in India. Supports 4 date formats (DD/MM/YYYY, DD-MM-YYYY, YYYY-MM-DD, DD Mon YYYY) and custom text. Choose position: bottom strip, top strip, or corner overlay. Select text color to match your photo. Essential for SSC, UPSC, Banking, Railway, and state PSC exam applications that mandate the date on photographs. Your photos are processed locally — never uploaded to any server.
