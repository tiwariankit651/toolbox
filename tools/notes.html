Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool really free?

Yes, completely free with no hidden charges, no signup required, and no usage limits. Use it as many times as you want.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. All processing happens in your browser using JavaScript. Your files and data are never uploaded to any server. We physically cannot access your data.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, this tool works on all devices — desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile phone. No app installation needed.

Does it work offline?

Yes! After your first visit, this tool works without internet connection. It is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that caches locally.

Can I use this for commercial purposes?

Yes, all output from this tool is yours to use for any purpose — personal, educational, or commercial.

About This Tool

A simple notepad that auto-saves to your browser. Write quick notes, save ideas, and to-do lists. Your notes persist even after closing the browser tab. No account or signup needed.

How to Use

Quick note likhna hai? Yahan likho — auto-save hota hai. Browser band karo, wapas aao — sab wahi milega. No login, no app download.

  1. + New Note dabao — naya note ban jayega
  2. Likhna shuru karo — auto-save hota hai har second
  3. Multiple notes banao — sidebar se switch karo
  4. Download ya copy karo — jab chahiye tab

Features

Create multiple notes with titles, organized in a sidebar. Each note auto-saves to your browser's local storage. Notes persist even after closing the browser. Download notes as text files or copy to clipboard. Perfect for quick ideas, to-do lists, meeting notes, and study summaries. No account needed — completely private.
