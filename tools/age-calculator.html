🎂 Age Calculator

Enter your date of birth to calculate exact age.

About This Tool

Calculate your exact age in years, months, days, hours, and minutes. Find out how many days until your next birthday. Enter your date of birth and get detailed age breakdown instantly.

How to Use

  1. Select your date of birth from the date picker
  2. Click Calculate to see your exact age
  3. View age in years, months, days, hours
  4. See days until your next birthday

Why Use an Age Calculator?

Many government forms, job applications, and exam registrations require your exact age as of a specific date. Manual calculation can lead to errors. This tool gives you precise age breakdown instantly. Useful for calculating age for insurance, retirement planning, and legal purposes.
