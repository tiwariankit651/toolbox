About This Tool

Convert one or multiple images to PDF with custom page sizes. Supports A4, Letter, Legal, and custom dimensions. Set margins, choose fit mode (fit within page maintaining ratio, fill page, or stretch). Perfect for converting scanned documents to PDF, creating photo albums, combining certificate images into one PDF for job applications, and preparing documents for government portal uploads that require specific page sizes. Batch processing supported — add multiple images and get one combined PDF.
