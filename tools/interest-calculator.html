How to Use This Tool

  1. Rate kaise diya hai, wahi chuno. Agar kisi ne "do rupaye sainkda" bola hai, toh Sainkda chuno aur 2 likho. Tool khud batayega ki ye 24% annual hai.
  2. Simple ya compound. Gaon-shehar ke udhaar mein aksar simple byaj hota hai. Bank aur chit fund compound karte hain — fark time ke saath bahut bada ho jaata hai.
  3. Time ka unit dhyan se. Months, years ya days — ek hi rakam pe mahine aur saal ka jawab bahut alag hoga.
  4. Schedule dekho. Har mahine kitna byaj bana, total kitna hua — poora table, CSV mein download bhi kar sakte ho.
  5. Dono compare karo tab mein dekho simple aur compound ka asli fark — ye wo number hai jo baat karte waqt kaam aata hai.

About This Tool

India mein byaj aksar "sainkda" mein bola jaata hai — ₹100 pe kitna rupaya mahine ka. Ye sunne mein chhota lagta hai: "sirf do rupaye sainkda". Lekin ₹2 sainkda ka matlab hai 2% monthly, jo 24% annual hai, aur ₹5 sainkda 60% annual. Zyadatar interest calculators sirf annual percent lete hain, isliye wo is conversion mein hi kaam nahi aate. Ye tool teenon tarah se rate leta hai — sainkda, monthly percent, yearly percent — aur hamesha dono equivalent dikhata hai, taaki "chhota" rate ka asli size saaf ho jaye. Simple aur compound dono, period-by-period schedule ke saath jo CSV mein download ho jaata hai. Sab kuch browser mein chalta hai, koi rakam server pe nahi jaati.

Frequently Asked Questions

₹2 sainkda ka matlab kya hai?

₹100 pe ₹2 per month — matlab 2% monthly. Saal ka 24% simple interest. Agar compound ho toh effective annual rate 26.8% ban jaata hai.

Sainkda monthly hota hai ya yearly?

Aam bol-chaal mein sainkda monthly hota hai. Phir bhi lene-dene se pehle confirm kar lena chahiye, kyunki fark 12 guna ka hai. Ye tool monthly maanta hai aur yearly equivalent saaf likhta hai.

Simple aur compound mein kya fark hai?

Simple byaj sirf mool rakam pe lagta hai. Compound mein pichhle byaj pe bhi byaj lagta hai. Chhote time mein fark kam hota hai, lambe time mein bahut bada — schedule table mein ye saaf dikh jaata hai.

Kya ye legal rate limit batata hai?

Nahi. Ye sirf hisaab karta hai. Money lending ke rules state ke hisaab se alag hain aur bahut jagah licence aur maximum rate decide hote hain. Agar rate bahut zyada lage toh legal advice lein.

Days wale option mein saal kitne din ka maana jaata hai?

365 din. Kuch banks 360 use karte hain, jisse answer thoda badal jaata hai — agar aapka lender 360 use karta hai toh months mein hisaab karna behtar hai.

Meri rakam kahin save hoti hai?

Settings yaad rehti hain par rakam nahi. Hisaab browser mein hota hai, kuch bhi server pe nahi jaata.
