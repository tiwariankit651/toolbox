About This Tool

Calculate Indian income tax for FY 2025-26 under both Old and New regimes and compare which saves more. Enter annual income, age group, and deductions (80C up to 1.5 lakh, 80D health insurance, HRA exemption, other deductions). Includes built-in HRA calculator — enter basic salary, HRA received, rent paid, and metro/non-metro to auto-calculate HRA exemption. Shows tax amount, effective rate, and recommendation for both regimes. Standard deduction (₹50K Old / ₹75K New) automatically applied. Disclaimer: For estimation only.
