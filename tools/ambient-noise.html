About This Tool

Ambient sounds help you focus, study, sleep, and relax. Mix multiple sounds together and adjust individual volumes. Uses browser-generated audio — no external files to load. Features include white noise, brown noise, pink noise, rain, thunder, wind, birds, fire, and ocean waves. Set a sleep timer to auto-stop. Perfect for Pomodoro study sessions, deep work, and meditation.
