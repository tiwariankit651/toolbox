🎧 Ambient Noise Generator

Mix sounds for focus, study, sleep, or relaxation. Click to toggle, adjust volume.

How to Use This Tool

Padhai mein focus nahi ho raha? Background sounds lagao — rain, cafe, birds. Brain automatically concentrate karne lagta hai.

  1. Koi bhi sound icon click karo — rain, birds, cafe, ocean, jo pasand ho
  2. Volume slider se adjust karo — soft rakhna hai ya loud, tumhari marzi
  3. Multiple sounds mix karo — rain + cafe = perfect study vibe
  4. Sleep timer set karo agar sote waqt use kar rahe ho — automatically band ho jayega

Common Use Cases

  • Creating a focused study environment with white noise
  • Falling asleep faster with rain or ocean sounds
  • Blocking office distractions during deep work sessions
  • Setting ambiance for meditation or yoga practice
  • Simulating a coffee shop atmosphere while working from home

About This Tool

Create the perfect study environment with ambient background sounds. Research shows that moderate background noise (around 70 dB) boosts creativity and focus. Mix multiple sounds together — white noise for blocking distractions, rain for relaxation, birds for a natural feel, or cafe sounds for a coffee shop atmosphere. Adjust individual volumes and set a sleep timer. All sounds are generated in your browser using the Web Audio API — no external files to download. Perfect for Pomodoro study sessions, deep work, meditation, and falling asleep. Works offline after first visit.
