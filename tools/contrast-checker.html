About This Tool

Check if your text and background color combination meets WCAG accessibility standards. Enter any two colors and instantly see the contrast ratio with pass/fail results for AA and AAA levels (both normal and large text). Includes a live preview showing how your text looks on the background, color swap button, and HEX input. Essential for web designers and developers building accessible websites that everyone can read, including people with visual impairments.
