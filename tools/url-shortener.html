Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool really free?

Yes, completely free with no hidden charges, no signup required, and no usage limits.

Do shortened URLs expire?

No, links created via is.gd are permanent and will continue to work indefinitely.

Is my data safe?

Your URL history is stored only in your browser's localStorage. We never track or store your URLs on any server.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, this tool works on all devices — desktop, tablet, and mobile phone.

Can I use shortened URLs for commercial purposes?

Yes, you can use the generated short links for any purpose — personal, educational, or commercial.

About This Tool

URL Shortener lets you convert long, unwieldy URLs into short, clean links that are easy to share on social media, messaging apps, emails, and documents. Powered by the is.gd API, your shortened links are permanent and reliable. The tool keeps a history of your last 5 shortened URLs in your browser for quick access. No signup, no tracking, no server-side storage — just fast, free URL shortening.
