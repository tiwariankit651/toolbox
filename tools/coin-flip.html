About This Tool

Flip a virtual coin or roll dice with cryptographically fair random results. The coin flip uses your browser's secure random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues) for truly unbiased results. Track heads vs tails statistics over multiple flips. The dice roller supports multiple dice types (D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, D20) and multiple dice at once. Keeps a history of rolls. Perfect for board games, making decisions, classroom activities, probability experiments, and tabletop RPGs.
