About This Tool

A complete random decision toolkit with cryptographically fair results. Flip one or many coins with a 3D animation and track heads-vs-tails statistics that converge toward 50/50 over many flips. Roll dice (D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, D20, D100), multiple at once, with modifiers and dice-notation support like 3d6+2. Generate random numbers with min, max, count and an allow-duplicates toggle. Use the Yes/No/Maybe decider or spin a custom-options wheel. Every random value comes from your browser's secure generator (crypto.getRandomValues), which is fairer and less predictable than Math.random. A statistics view charts the observed distribution against the expected (fair) frequency — handy for teaching probability and the law of large numbers. Includes optional Web Audio sound effects with a mute toggle, plus a session history you can copy or clear.
