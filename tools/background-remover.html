About This Tool

Remove backgrounds from images to get transparent PNG files. Works by detecting the dominant background color and making it transparent. Best results with photos that have solid or simple backgrounds — like portraits against a wall, product photos on a table, or objects on a plain surface. Adjust the threshold slider for more or less aggressive removal. Perfect for creating product images for e-commerce, profile pictures, design projects, and social media content. Everything runs locally in your browser.
