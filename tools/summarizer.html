📝 Article Summarizer

Paste any article or text. Get key points and summary. Works with English, Hindi, and all languages.

  1. Long text paste karo — article, notes, ya koi bhi content

  3. Summarize dabao — key points extract ho jaayenge
  4. Important lines highlighted milenge — main ideas clear
  5. Copy karo summary — apne notes mein paste karo

About This Tool

Summarize long articles and text into concise key points using extractive summarization. The tool scores each sentence based on important keyword frequency and selects the most relevant ones. Choose summary length (3, 5, 8, or 10 key points) and output format (numbered list, bullet points, or paragraph). Works with any language including English, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, and more. Shows word count reduction percentage. Perfect for students summarizing textbook chapters, professionals reading long reports, and anyone saving time.
