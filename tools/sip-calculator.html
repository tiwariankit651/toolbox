About This Tool

Calculate mutual fund SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) returns with three modes: SIP (monthly investment growth), Lumpsum (one-time investment growth), and Goal Planner (how much to invest monthly to reach a target amount). Enter investment amount, expected annual return rate, and time period. See total invested amount, estimated returns, and maturity value with a visual bar showing invested vs returns proportion. Essential for financial planning, retirement planning, and understanding the power of compound interest.
