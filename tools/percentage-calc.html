Quick mental-maths tips

  • x% of y = y% of x — e.g. 8% of 50 = 50% of 8 = 4.
  • To find 10%, just move the decimal one place left. 5% is half of that.
  • 15% = 10% + 5% (10% then add its half).
  • A rise of a% then a fall of a% is never the original — it drops by a²/100 %.
  • Successive % changes: net = a + b + (a·b)/100.
