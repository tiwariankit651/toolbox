🧠 Mind Map Maker

Create visual mind maps with draggable nodes, color coding, templates, and PNG export. All in your browser.

How to Use This Tool

Ideas organize nahi ho rahe? Visual mind map banao. Center mein main topic, branches mein sub-topics. Dimag mein jo chal raha hai — yahan draw karo.

  1. Center mein main idea likho — "Indian Freedom Movement" ya "App Idea"
  2. Branches add karo — sub-topics ya related points ke liye
  3. Har branch ko aur expand karo — details add karte jao
  4. Colors use karo — different categories ko visually alag karo
  5. Save karo ya image download karo — revision ke time kaam aayega

  • History chapter ka mind map — dates, events, connections sab ek jagah

  • Essay planning — introduction se conclusion tak flow decide karo

  • Startup idea map — problem, solution, market sab connect karo

About Mind Map Maker

The Mind Map Maker is a powerful, completely free online tool that helps you visually organize your thoughts, ideas, and information in a hierarchical, branching structure. Mind mapping is a proven technique used by students, professionals, writers, and creative thinkers worldwide to improve brainstorming sessions, plan projects, take structured notes, and solve complex problems. Our tool brings this powerful methodology directly to your browser with zero installation, no account creation, and complete privacy since all data stays on your device.

Whether you're planning a complex project with multiple deliverables, studying for exams by organizing course material into digestible chunks, brainstorming creative ideas for a new venture, or simply trying to make sense of interconnected concepts, this mind map tool provides an intuitive drag-and-drop interface that makes visual thinking effortless. Each node can be customized with different colors for visual categorization, and the entire map can be exported as a high-quality PNG image for sharing or embedding in presentations and documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new node?

Click on any existing node to select it, then click the "Add Child" button. A new child node will appear connected to the selected node.

Can I change the color of nodes?

Yes! Select a node by clicking it, then use the color picker in the editor panel to change its color for visual categorization.

How do I move nodes around?

Simply click and drag any node to reposition it on the canvas. The connecting lines will update automatically.

Can I save my mind map?

Yes, click the "Save" button to store your mind map in your browser's localStorage. Click "Load" to restore it later.

How do I export my mind map?

Click "Export PNG" to download your entire mind map as a high-quality PNG image that you can share or embed in documents.

What are the templates for?

Templates provide pre-built mind map structures for common use cases: Project Planning (with phases and tasks), Study Notes (with subjects and topics), and Brainstorm (with idea categories).

Is there a limit on the number of nodes?

No hard limit. You can add as many nodes as you need, though very large maps may benefit from the zoom controls.
