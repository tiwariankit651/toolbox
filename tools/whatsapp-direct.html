About This Tool

Send WhatsApp messages to any phone number without saving it to your contacts first. Select the country code, enter the number, optionally type a message, and click send. WhatsApp will open directly with the chat ready. Works on mobile and desktop. Supports 20+ countries. No data is stored or sent to any server.
