About This Tool

Compress PDF files to significantly reduce file size. The tool renders each page as a JPEG image at your chosen quality and DPI, then rebuilds the PDF — resulting in genuine compression of 30-90%. Choose compression level: Maximum (70-90% smaller), Medium (50-70%), Light (30-50%), or Minimal (10-30%). Select DPI: 72 (smallest), 100, 150 (recommended), or 200 (print quality). Shows progress per page, original vs compressed size with percentage saved. Essential for government portal uploads with strict size limits.
