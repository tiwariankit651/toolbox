About This Tool

Check your English text for 50 common grammar, spelling, and style errors. Detects issues like double spaces, missing capitalization, common misspellings (necessary, government, environment, achieve, believe, etc.), passive voice, wordy phrases (utilize→use, in order to→to, due to the fact that→because), filler words (basically, actually, literally), vague words (thing, stuff), and commonly confused words (their/there, your/you're, its/it's, affect/effect). Each issue is color-coded by type with a suggested fix. Note: This is rule-based, not AI — it catches common mistakes but cannot replace a human editor. Your text never leaves your browser.
