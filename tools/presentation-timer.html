About This Tool

The Presentation Timer is a comprehensive timing tool designed for speakers, students, and professionals who need to manage their time during presentations, viva voce examinations, speeches, and lectures. It automatically divides your total allotted time into equal sections based on the number of slides or topics you plan to cover, giving you audio and visual alerts when each section's time expires. The large countdown display is visible from a distance, making it ideal for practice sessions where your laptop sits across the room. Color-coded feedback instantly tells you whether you're on track: green means plenty of time remains, yellow signals you're past the halfway mark, and red warns that less than a quarter of your time is left. The practice mode records exactly when you transition between slides, then provides detailed pace analysis showing which sections you rushed through and which ones you spent too long on. This feedback loop helps you refine your delivery over multiple rehearsals until your pacing is consistent and natural. Whether you're preparing for a 5-minute lightning talk, a 20-minute conference presentation, or a 45-minute thesis defense, this timer adapts to your needs with no signup, no installation, and complete privacy since everything runs in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool really free?

Yes, completely free with no hidden charges, no signup required, and no usage limits. Use it as many times as you want.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. All processing happens in your browser using JavaScript. No data is sent to any server.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, this tool works on all devices — desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile phone. No app installation needed.

How does the section alert work?

The timer divides your total time equally among sections. When each section's time expires, you hear an audio beep and see a notification. A final-minute warning beep also plays.

What is Practice Mode?

In Practice Mode, you click "Next Slide" each time you move to a new slide. After the presentation ends, you get a pace analysis showing how long you spent on each section vs. the ideal time.

Can I use this for exams and viva?

Absolutely. Set your total viva time and number of questions/topics. The timer will alert you when to move on to stay within your allotted time.

Does the audio work on all browsers?

Yes, the beep uses the Web Audio API which is supported by all modern browsers. Make sure your device volume is on.

Can I pause and resume?

Yes, use the Pause/Resume button to temporarily stop the countdown without losing your progress.
