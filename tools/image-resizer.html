How to Use This Tool

You know that moment when a form says "photo must be exactly 200x230 pixels" and you're like... how do I even do that? Been there. Here's the easy way:

  1. Upload your photo — drag and drop or click. Any image format works.
  2. Pick a preset — Passport photo? SSC form? Instagram post? One click sets the exact size.
  3. Or type your own dimensions — pixels, cm, mm, inches — whatever the form asks for.
  4. Lock aspect ratio if you don't want your face to look stretched (trust me, keep it on 😄).
  5. Click Resize & Download — that's it. Your perfectly sized image is ready.

No need to install any app. Works on your phone too. I use this every time I fill government forms — saves so much headache.
