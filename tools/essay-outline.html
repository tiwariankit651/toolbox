📝 Essay / Answer Outline Generator

Enter a topic and get a structured outline for UPSC mains, essays, and answers.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Step 1: Enter your essay topic or thesis statement
  2. Step 2: Select the essay type (argumentative, expository, narrative, etc.)
  3. Step 3: Specify the number of body paragraphs needed
  4. Step 4: Click Generate to create a structured outline
  5. Step 5: Expand each section with your own research and ideas

Common Use Cases

  • Planning academic essays with proper structure and flow
  • Organizing thoughts before writing research papers
  • Creating outlines for college application essays
  • Structuring argumentative essays with clear thesis support
  • Breaking down complex topics into manageable sections

About This Tool

Generate structured outlines for essays and answers. Enter any topic and get a complete framework with introduction (quote/statistic), historical background, current scenario, arguments for and against, way forward, and conclusion. Supports three formats: full essay (1000+ words for UPSC mains), long answer (250 words), and short answer (150 words). Helps organize your thoughts before writing and ensures you cover all important points. Essential for UPSC mains, university essays, and competitive exam answers.
