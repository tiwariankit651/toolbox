About This Tool

The Weekly Timetable Maker is a powerful, free online scheduling tool designed to help students, professionals, and anyone who needs to organize their week efficiently. Whether you are managing school classes, university lectures, work shifts, or study sessions, this tool provides an intuitive visual interface to plan your entire week at a glance.

Built entirely in your browser, this timetable creator requires no downloads, no signups, and no server uploads. Your schedule data stays private on your device using localStorage. The tool features a full 7-day grid covering 6 AM to 10 PM with one-hour time blocks, giving you complete flexibility to plan early mornings through late evenings.

Color-coding makes it easy to distinguish between different subjects, activities, or priorities. Simply click any cell to add an activity and choose from a palette of vibrant colors, or let the tool auto-assign colors for you. Need to fill multiple time slots with the same activity? Just drag across cells to select a range and fill them all at once — perfect for double periods, long meetings, or extended study blocks.

Save multiple timetables for different weeks or purposes, switch between them instantly, and export your finished schedule as a high-quality PNG image to share with classmates, colleagues, or print for your wall. The print-friendly view ensures your timetable looks great on paper too. Pre-made templates for School, College, and Competitive Exam give you a head start so you can customize rather than build from scratch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this timetable maker really free?

Yes, completely free with no hidden charges, no signup required, and no usage limits. Create as many timetables as you want without paying anything.

Is my schedule data safe and private?

Absolutely. All data is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is ever sent to a server. We physically cannot access your timetable data.

Can I save multiple timetables?

Yes! Give each timetable a name and save it. You can create separate schedules for different weeks, semesters, or purposes and switch between them anytime.

How do I add a subject to multiple time slots at once?

Click and drag across multiple cells to select them. When you release, a dialog will appear where you can enter the subject name and pick a color. All selected cells will be filled at once.

Can I export or share my timetable?

Yes, click "Export PNG" to download your timetable as a high-quality image. You can share it via messaging apps, email, or social media. You can also use the Print button for a paper copy.

Does it work on mobile devices?

Yes, the timetable works on all devices. On smaller screens, you can scroll horizontally to view the full week. All features including drag-select work on touch devices.

What are the pre-made templates?

Templates give you a starting point: School (typical class schedule), College (lecture blocks with gaps), and Competitive Exam (study blocks with breaks for SSC/UPSC prep). You can customize any template after loading it.

Can I change colors after adding a subject?

Yes, simply click on any filled cell to open the editor. You can change the subject name, pick a new color, or clear the cell entirely.
