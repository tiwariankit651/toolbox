About This Tool

Study smarter with flashcards using active recall — scientifically proven as the most effective learning technique. This tool includes 250+ ready-made flashcards across 9 subjects: Ancient History, Medieval History, Modern History, Indian Polity, Indian Geography, Indian Economy, Science and Technology, English Vocabulary, and Computer Knowledge. Each card has a question on the front and answer on the back — click to flip. Rate difficulty (Easy/Hard/Don't Know) to track your progress. Create your own custom flashcard sets, export them as JSON to share with friends, and import sets from others. All data saves in your browser. Perfect for UPSC, SSC, Banking, Railway, and board exam preparation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool really free?

Yes, completely free with no hidden charges, no signup required, and no usage limits. Use it as many times as you want.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. All processing happens in your browser using JavaScript. Your files and data are never uploaded to any server. We physically cannot access your data.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, this tool works on all devices — desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile phone. No app installation needed.

Does it work offline?

Yes! After your first visit, this tool works without internet connection. It is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that caches locally.

Can I use this for commercial purposes?

Yes, all output from this tool is yours to use for any purpose — personal, educational, or commercial.
