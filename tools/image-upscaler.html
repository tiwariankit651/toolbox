About This Tool

Enlarge and enhance low-resolution images using advanced browser-based interpolation and sharpening. Upscale images 2x, 3x, or 4x with adjustable smoothing and sharpening. Best results with images under 500px. Note: This uses mathematical upscaling, not AI — results are good for basic enhancement but not comparable to AI tools like Topaz.
