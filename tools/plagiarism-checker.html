🔍 Plagiarism / Text Similarity Checker

Compare two texts or check one document for internal repetition — using real similarity algorithms. All processing happens in your browser.

⚠️ What this tool does — and does not do.

This tool compares the text you provide and finds internal repetition within a single document. It uses genuine algorithms — Jaccard similarity, cosine similarity, longest common substring, and shared n-grams. It does NOT search the internet or any academic database. It cannot tell you whether your text matches a source you have not pasted in. It is not a substitute for institutional tools like Turnitin. Use it to compare drafts, spot accidental copying, and reduce repetition in your own writing.

✍️ How to fix similarity: paraphrase & cite properly

  • Rewrite in your own words — read the source, close it, then write the idea from memory. Don't just swap a few synonyms; that is still plagiarism.
  • Change structure, not just words — combine or split sentences and reorder ideas so the phrasing is genuinely yours.
  • Always cite the source — even a good paraphrase needs a citation. Only common knowledge is exempt.
  • Quote directly when the exact wording matters — use quotation marks and a citation.
  • Need help? Try our Paraphraser to rephrase your own drafts, and the Citation Generator to format references correctly.

About This Tool

The Plagiarism and Text Similarity Checker is a powerful browser-based tool that compares two pieces of text to determine how similar they are. Unlike online plagiarism checkers that upload your content to external servers, this tool processes everything locally in your browser, ensuring complete privacy for sensitive documents like unpublished research papers, assignments, or proprietary content. It uses three complementary similarity methods to give you a comprehensive picture: word-level Jaccard similarity measures vocabulary overlap between the two texts, sentence matching identifies identical or near-identical sentences that may have been copied, and n-gram analysis (using 3-grams and 5-grams) detects phrase-level similarities that simple word comparison might miss. The tool highlights matching sentences in green so you can visually identify which parts are similar. It provides an overall similarity score with a clear verdict ranging from Original to High Similarity, along with detailed statistics including matching sentence count and unique word overlap percentage. This makes it ideal for teachers checking student work, writers ensuring originality, researchers comparing drafts, or anyone who needs to verify that two texts are sufficiently different. The tool handles texts of any length and provides instant results without waiting for server processing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool really free?

Yes, completely free with no hidden charges, no signup required, and no usage limits.

Is my text uploaded to any server?

No. All comparison happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your text never leaves your device.

How accurate is the similarity detection?

It uses three methods (Jaccard, sentence matching, n-gram overlap) combined for a balanced score. It detects exact and near-exact matches but cannot detect heavily paraphrased content.

What do the similarity levels mean?

Original (0-15%): texts are substantially different. Low (15-30%): minor overlap. Moderate (30-60%): significant shared content. High (60%+): likely copied or very similar source.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, this tool works on all devices — desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile phone.

Can I check long documents?

Yes, there is no character limit. However, very long texts (50,000+ words) may take a moment to process depending on your device.

Does it detect paraphrasing?

It detects word-level and phrase-level overlap. Heavily paraphrased content with completely different vocabulary may not be detected, as that requires AI-level semantic analysis.

Can I use this for academic purposes?

Yes, teachers and students can use this to check assignments. Note that this is a local comparison tool — it does not search the internet for matches.
