💡 CTR Tips — What Makes Good Thumbnails

  • Use faces with emotion — Thumbnails with expressive faces get 30%+ more clicks
  • Bold, readable text — Max 3-4 words, large enough to read on mobile
  • High contrast colors — Stand out in the feed; avoid YouTube's red/white/black
  • Rule of thirds — Place key elements at intersection points
  • Curiosity gap — Show enough to intrigue but not enough to satisfy
  • Consistent branding — Recognizable style builds channel identity

About This Tool

Preview how your thumbnail will look before publishing — everywhere it matters. Upload your image (recommended 1280×720) and see it rendered at real pixel sizes across YouTube contexts (desktop and mobile search, home feed grid, suggested/sidebar, watch-page end screen and channel page) as well as Instagram feed and 1:1 grid crop, Facebook link preview, Twitter/X card, LinkedIn post and a WhatsApp forward. Toggle YouTube's dark and light modes, simulate the duration badge, progress-watched bar and title text so you can see what actually covers your thumbnail, and switch on safe-area guides that show which regions get covered or cropped. Compare two thumbnails side by side (A/B) in every context, run the built-in legibility checker (dimensions, aspect ratio, sub-1280×720 warning, an estimate of whether in-image text is too small, plus brightness and contrast analysis with suggestions), and export a single contact-sheet PNG of all contexts to share with your team. Everything runs in your browser — images never leave your device.
