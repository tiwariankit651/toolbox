About This Tool

Preview how your YouTube thumbnail will look before publishing. Upload your thumbnail image (recommended 1280x720), enter video title and channel name, and see realistic previews for YouTube Desktop (search results view), YouTube Mobile (feed view), and Twitter/X (shared link view). Helps you check if text is readable at small sizes, if the image is eye-catching, and if it looks good across platforms. Essential for YouTubers, content creators, and social media managers.
