What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed amount you pay every month to repay a loan. It includes both principal and interest components. The EMI amount depends on three factors: loan amount, interest rate, and loan tenure. Higher loan amount or interest rate means higher EMI, while longer tenure reduces EMI but increases total interest paid.

EMI Formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ - 1), where P = Principal, r = monthly interest rate, n = number of months. This tool calculates instantly so you do not need to remember the formula.
