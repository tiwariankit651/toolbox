About This Tool

The Instagram Toolkit is a collection of creator utilities that run entirely in your browser. It does not download Instagram media — that isn't possible from a static web page because Instagram requires authentication, blocks cross-origin access, and prohibits scraping in its Terms of Service. Instead of pretending otherwise, this toolkit focuses on the parts of your Instagram workflow that genuinely work client-side.

Use the caption formatter to keep line breaks and blank lines that Instagram would normally collapse, the hashtag manager to organise curated and custom tag sets within the 30-tag limit, the image resizer to export photos at exact Instagram dimensions with a story safe-area guide, the bio builder to stay within the 150-character limit, and the grid planner to preview how your next posts will sit together in a 3-column feed.

To get a copy of your own Instagram content, Instagram provides an official "Download Your Information" feature in Accounts Center — that is the correct, permitted way to export your posts, reels, and stories.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool really free?

Yes, completely free with no hidden charges, no signup required, and no usage limits. Use it as many times as you want.

Can this tool download Instagram photos or videos?

No. A static web page cannot download Instagram media — Instagram requires you to be logged in, blocks cross-origin requests, and prohibits scraping in its Terms of Service. To export your own content, use Instagram's official "Download Your Information" feature in Accounts Center.

Why does Instagram strip my caption line breaks?

Instagram collapses consecutive blank lines. The caption formatter inserts an invisible zero-width character on empty lines so your spacing is preserved when you paste it in.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. The image resizer and grid planner process everything locally in your browser using the canvas API. Your images never leave your device.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, all tools work on phones, tablets, and desktops. The image resizer and grid planner work best where you can select photos from your device.

Where are my saved hashtag sets stored?

In your browser's localStorage. They stay on your device and are never sent to any server. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Can I use the output commercially?

Yes. Captions, hashtags, bios, and resized images you create are yours to use for any purpose, including commercial.
