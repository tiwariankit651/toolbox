FAQ

How many images can I use?

Upload as many images as you like. Each layout has a fixed number of cells — assign any image to any cell by clicking.

What layouts are available?

2×2, 3×3, 1+2 (one large + two small), 2×1 side-by-side, 1×2 stacked, 3-strip horizontal, 3-strip vertical, and freeform.

Can I adjust the crop per image?

Yes! After creating the collage, click any cell to assign/swap an image, drag inside a cell to pan, or scroll to zoom in/out for precise cropping.

What output sizes are supported?

Presets for Instagram (square, portrait, story), Facebook post, YouTube thumbnail, plus custom width/height.
