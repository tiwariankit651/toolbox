🖼️ Photo Collage Maker

Upload multiple photos and arrange them in a grid collage. Choose layout, spacing, and background color.

Drop images here — supports multiple files

FAQ

How many images can I use?

You can upload as many images as your layout supports. Extra images beyond the grid slots will be ignored.

What layouts are available?

2×1, 1×2, 2×2, 2×3, and 3×3 grid layouts. Choose the one that fits your needs.

Can I adjust the spacing between photos?

Yes, use the gap slider to set spacing from 0 to 20 pixels, and pick any background color for the gaps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool really free?

Yes, completely free with no hidden charges, no signup required, and no usage limits. Use it as many times as you want.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. All processing happens in your browser using JavaScript. Your files and data are never uploaded to any server. We physically cannot access your data.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, this tool works on all devices — desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile phone. No app installation needed.

Does it work offline?

Yes! After your first visit, this tool works without internet connection. It is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that caches locally.

Can I use this for commercial purposes?

Yes, all output from this tool is yours to use for any purpose — personal, educational, or commercial.

About This Tool

Create beautiful photo collages instantly in your browser. Upload multiple images, pick a grid layout (2×1, 1×2, 2×2, 2×3, or 3×3), adjust gap spacing, and choose a background color. Images are automatically cropped to fit each cell. Download your collage as a high-quality PNG. Perfect for social media posts, mood boards, photo grids, and presentations. Everything runs locally — your photos never leave your device.
