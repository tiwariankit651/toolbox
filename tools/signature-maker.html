About This Tool

Create digital signatures in two modes: Draw (use mouse or finger to draw your signature) and Type (select from 5 cursive fonts — Dancing Script, Great Vibes, Pacifico, Caveat, Satisfy). Customize pen color, thickness, and background (transparent or white). Download as PNG. The transparent background option lets you place the signature on any document. Perfect for signing PDF documents, government forms, job applications, and official paperwork without printing and scanning.
