✅ How ATS Parsers Actually Work (and What to Avoid)

An ATS reads your resume as plain text. Anything that confuses the text extraction can hurt you. Keep this checklist in mind:

  • Use a single-column layout. Multi-column and table-based layouts often get read out of order or dropped entirely.
  • Avoid text inside images. ATS cannot read text baked into a graphic, logo, or icon.
  • Keep info out of headers/footers. Some parsers ignore the header/footer region — put your contact details in the body.
  • Use standard section headings like Summary, Experience, Education, and Skills so sections are recognised.
  • Use simple bullet characters (• or -). Fancy symbols (◆, ➤, ●●) can turn into gibberish.
  • Stick to common fonts and standard date formats (e.g. Jan 2022 – Mar 2024).
  • Save as .docx or a text-based PDF — never a scanned image PDF.
  • Mirror the job's keywords naturally; don't keyword-stuff white text.

About This Tool

The Resume ATS Checker helps job seekers optimize their resumes for Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) — the software that most companies use to filter resumes before a human ever sees them. Studies show that up to 75% of resumes are rejected by ATS before reaching a recruiter, often due to formatting issues, missing keywords, or lack of quantifiable achievements. This tool analyzes your resume against a job description and provides a comprehensive score based on five key criteria: keyword match percentage, proper section formatting, optimal length, use of action verbs, and quantification of achievements. Each criterion is scored individually and combined into an overall ATS compatibility score from 0 to 100. The tool provides specific, actionable suggestions to improve your score, such as which keywords to add, which sections are missing, and how to strengthen your bullet points with numbers and action verbs. All processing happens entirely in your browser — your resume text is never uploaded to any server, ensuring complete privacy for your sensitive career information. Use this tool before every job application to maximize your chances of getting past the ATS filter and into the hands of a real recruiter.

What is an ATS?

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software used by employers to manage job applications. It scans resumes for keywords, formatting, and relevant content to rank candidates before a human reviews them.

What score should I aim for?

A score above 70 is considered good. Above 80 is excellent. If your score is below 50, your resume likely needs significant optimization for the target job.
