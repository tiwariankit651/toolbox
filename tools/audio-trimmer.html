✂️ Audio Trimmer

Upload audio, set start and end time, trim and download.

Drop audio file here (MP3, WAV, OGG)

How to Use This Tool

  1. Step 1: Want to make a ringtone from your favorite song? Upload the audio file here
  2. Step 2: See the waveform — drag the handles to select the part you want to keep
  3. Step 3: Fine-tune the start and end points until you get the perfect clip
  4. Step 4: Hit play to preview your trimmed section before saving
  5. Step 5: Download your trimmed audio — perfect 30-second ringtone ready!

Common Use Cases

  • Cutting ringtones from your favorite songs
  • Removing silence from the beginning or end of recordings
  • Extracting specific segments from podcast episodes
  • Trimming voice memos to keep only important parts
  • Creating short audio clips for presentations or videos

About This Tool

Cut and trim audio files directly in your browser. Upload MP3, WAV, or OGG files, see the waveform visualization, set precise start and end times, preview your selection, and download the trimmed audio as WAV. Perfect for creating ringtones from songs, extracting podcast clips, trimming voice recordings, and cutting background music to exact length for presentations. Uses the Web Audio API for processing — your audio files never leave your device.
