About This Tool

Convert video clips to animated GIF images directly in your browser. Upload MP4, WebM, or MOV files, set start and end time to trim, choose FPS (5-30), output width (100-800px), and playback speed (0.5x to 2x). The tool captures frames from the video and encodes them into a GIF using the gif.js library. Shows progress during conversion. Perfect for creating memes, social media content, tutorial snippets, and reaction GIFs. Keep clips under 5 seconds and width under 320px for smaller file sizes.
