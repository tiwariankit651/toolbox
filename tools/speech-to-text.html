Click the microphone button and start speaking. Your speech will be converted to text in real-time. Works best in Chrome and Edge.

🗣️ Voice Commands for Punctuation

While dictating, just say these words and the right character is inserted:

“comma” → ,
“full stop” / “period” → .
“question mark” → ?
“exclamation mark” → !
“new line” → ↵
“new paragraph” → ↵↵

⚠️ Speech recognition is not supported in this browser. It mainly works in Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge on desktop and Android. Please switch browsers to use this tool.

🔒 Privacy note: Browser speech recognition is not fully offline. When you dictate, your browser sends the recorded audio to its vendor's servers (Google for Chrome, Microsoft for Edge) for processing, then returns the text. We never see or store your audio — but the browser vendor does process it. If that matters for sensitive content, avoid dictating confidential information.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Step 1: Typing slow hai? Just click the microphone button to start
  2. Step 2: Speak clearly — don't mumble, the mic isn't your best friend yet
  3. Step 3: Watch your words appear on screen in real-time. Pretty cool, right?
  4. Step 4: Fix any mistakes — it's good but not perfect, especially with desi accents
  5. Step 5: Copy or download your text. You just "typed" 500 words in 2 minutes!

Common Use Cases

  • Transcribing meeting notes and discussions hands-free
  • Converting voice memos into written text quickly
  • Dictating emails, messages, or documents by speaking
  • Creating written records of interviews or lectures

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool really free?

Yes, completely free with no hidden charges, no signup required, and no usage limits. Use it as many times as you want.

Is my data safe?

The text and history stay in your browser and are never uploaded to our servers. However, be aware that browser speech recognition sends your recorded audio to your browser vendor's servers (Google or Microsoft) for processing. We cannot access it, but the vendor does process the audio. Avoid dictating confidential information.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, this tool works on all devices — desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile phone. No app installation needed.

Does it work offline?

No — unlike most of our tools, speech recognition needs an internet connection. Your browser (Chrome or Edge) sends the recorded audio to its vendor's servers for processing and returns the text. The page itself loads instantly, but dictation will not work without internet.

Can I use this for commercial purposes?

Yes, all output from this tool is yours to use for any purpose — personal, educational, or commercial.
