🎙️ Speech to Text

Click the microphone button and start speaking. Your speech will be converted to text in real-time.

⚠️ Speech recognition is not supported in this browser. Please use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Step 1: Typing slow hai? Just click the microphone button to start
  2. Step 2: Speak clearly — don't mumble, the mic isn't your best friend yet
  3. Step 3: Watch your words appear on screen in real-time. Pretty cool, right?
  4. Step 4: Fix any mistakes — it's good but not perfect, especially with desi accents
  5. Step 5: Copy or download your text. You just "typed" 500 words in 2 minutes!

Common Use Cases

  • Transcribing meeting notes and discussions hands-free
  • Converting voice memos into written text quickly
  • Dictating emails, messages, or documents by speaking
  • Creating written records of interviews or lectures
  • Accessibility aid for users who prefer speaking over typing

About This Tool

Convert your voice to text in real-time using browser-based speech recognition. Supports 19 languages including English (US/UK), Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic, Portuguese, Russian, Italian, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, and Urdu. Features continuous recording mode, real-time transcription display, copy to clipboard, clear, and download as text file. Works best in Chrome and Edge browsers. Perfect for voice typing, taking notes, transcribing meetings, and accessibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool really free?

Yes, completely free with no hidden charges, no signup required, and no usage limits. Use it as many times as you want.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. All processing happens in your browser using JavaScript. Your files and data are never uploaded to any server. We physically cannot access your data.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, this tool works on all devices — desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile phone. No app installation needed.

Does it work offline?

Yes! After your first visit, this tool works without internet connection. It is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that caches locally.

Can I use this for commercial purposes?

Yes, all output from this tool is yours to use for any purpose — personal, educational, or commercial.
