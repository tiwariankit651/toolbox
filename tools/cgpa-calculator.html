About This Tool

Calculate CGPA and SGPA from your grades and credits. Supports both 10-point and 4-point grading systems. Add multiple subjects, enter grade points and credits to get accurate CGPA instantly.

How to Use

  1. Add your subjects with grade points and credits
  2. The tool calculates SGPA/CGPA automatically
  3. Supports both 10-point and 4-point grading systems

CGPA to Percentage Conversion

For most Indian universities: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5 (for 10-point scale). For example, CGPA 8.5 = 80.75%. Different universities may use different conversion formulas. Always check your university guidelines. This tool is useful for filling job applications and higher education forms that require percentage.
