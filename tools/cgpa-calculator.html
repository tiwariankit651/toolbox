CGPA to Percentage Conversion

For most Indian universities: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5 (for 10-point scale). For example, CGPA 8.5 = 80.75%. Different universities may use different conversion formulas. Always check your university guidelines. This tool is useful for filling job applications and higher education forms that require percentage.
