About This Tool

Calculate and maintain correct aspect ratios for images and videos. Enter any width or height and get the matching dimension automatically. Essential for content creators making YouTube thumbnails (16:9), Instagram posts (1:1), Reels (9:16), presentations (4:3), and more. Includes 8 quick presets for popular platforms. Also calculates the simplified ratio from any dimensions. Useful for web designers, video editors, photographers, and social media managers who need pixel-perfect dimensions.
