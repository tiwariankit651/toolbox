About This Tool

Calculate and maintain correct aspect ratios for images and videos. Enter any two of width, height, or ratio and get the missing value, with a lock toggle. Includes clickable presets for every common ratio (16:9, 9:16, 4:3, 1:1, 21:9, golden ratio, A-series, cinema 1.85:1 and 2.39:1, and more) plus exact-pixel platform presets for YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Pinterest, WhatsApp, and print sizes. A live preview renders the chosen ratio to scale and can overlay a second ratio for comparison. The scale calculator resizes by width, height, or percentage while preserving the ratio, and the crop planner shows exactly what gets cropped or letterboxed for a target ratio. It also simplifies any ratio to lowest terms and reports the decimal ratio and megapixels. Useful for web designers, video editors, photographers, and social media managers who need pixel-perfect dimensions.
