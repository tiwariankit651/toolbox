📝 Lorem Ipsum Generator

About This Tool

Generate lorem ipsum placeholder text for your designs and development projects. Choose between paragraphs, sentences, or words. Copy generated text with one click for use in mockups and templates.

How to Use

  1. Select the type: paragraphs, sentences, or words
  2. Choose the quantity
  3. Click Generate
  4. Copy the generated text

What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is placeholder text used in design and development since the 1500s. It helps designers visualize how text will look in a layout without being distracted by readable content. Web developers, graphic designers, and print professionals use it daily for mockups, wireframes, and templates.
