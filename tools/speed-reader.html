About This Tool

Improve your reading speed with word-by-word or chunk display at adjustable speed. Set reading speed from 100 to 800 WPM (words per minute). Choose chunk size: 1, 2, 3, or 5 words at a time. Adjustable font size for comfortable reading. Paste any text — articles, current affairs, study notes, textbook chapters — and practice reading faster. The average person reads at 200-250 WPM; with practice, you can reach 400-600 WPM. Great for UPSC current affairs reading, newspaper speed reading, and exam preparation.
