How to Use This Tool

Need to add your signature to a PDF? Or highlight important lines in your study notes? Or white-out a mistake before submitting? You don't need Adobe Acrobat for this. Here's how:

  1. Upload your PDF — just drop it in. Any PDF works, even multi-page ones.
  2. Pick your tool — Text, Draw, Highlight, White-out, Arrow, Sticky Note, or insert an Image.
  3. Edit away — click anywhere on the page to add text, drag to highlight, draw freehand — whatever you need.
  4. Navigate pages — use Prev/Next buttons for multi-page PDFs. Zoom in if you need precision.
  5. Save PDF — click Save and download your edited PDF. All changes are baked in.

This is basically a free alternative to Adobe Acrobat. Works right in your browser, no installation, no signup. Your PDF never gets uploaded anywhere — it stays on your device the whole time.
