About This Tool

A full-featured PDF editor that runs entirely in your browser. Tools include: Text (4 fonts, custom size and color), Draw (freehand pen), Highlight (yellow marker), White-out (cover errors), Rectangle, Arrow, Sticky Notes, and Image Insert. Supports multi-page PDFs with page navigation, zoom in/out, and undo. All edits are saved into the final PDF when you download. Perfect for annotating documents, filling forms, marking up reports, adding signatures, and editing any PDF without expensive software like Adobe Acrobat.
