About This Tool

Change image backgrounds to any color — white, blue, red, or custom colors. Essential for passport photos (white/blue background required), ID cards, visa applications, and official documents. The tool detects the background from corner pixels and replaces it with your chosen color. Adjust the detection threshold for better results. Also supports gradient backgrounds. Works best with photos taken against solid or simple backgrounds. Your photos are processed entirely in your browser for complete privacy.
