How to Use This Tool

  1. Fill tenant, landlord and rent. These appear on every receipt, so you only enter them once.
  2. Add the landlord's PAN if your annual rent crosses ₹1 lakh — ₹8,334 a month. Above that figure most employers will not process the HRA exemption without it. The tool warns you when your rent crosses the line.
  3. Single or full year. One month for a missed receipt, or all twelve for the financial year at submission time. Twelve can come as one PDF or as a ZIP of separate files, depending on what your employer's portal accepts.
  4. Check the HRA tab to see what you can actually claim before you submit. The exemption is the least of three amounts, and it is often lower than people expect.
  5. Print and get it signed. A receipt above ₹5,000 should carry a ₹1 revenue stamp with the landlord's signature across it. The PDF leaves space for this.

About This Tool

A rent receipt is the document your employer or the Income Tax Department accepts as proof that you paid rent, which is what makes the HRA exemption under Section 10(13A) claimable. This generator produces receipts in the standard format — tenant, landlord, address, period, amount, payment mode and a signature line — and lets you make all twelve months of a financial year in one go, which is when most people actually need them. The landlord's PAN field is included because annual rent above ₹1 lakh requires it, and a declaration if the landlord has no PAN. The built-in calculator works out the exemption as the least of the three statutory amounts so you can see the real benefit before submitting anything. Names and addresses in Hindi, Marathi, Tamil and other Indian scripts print correctly, because the PDF embeds a Unicode font rather than dropping the characters. Nothing is uploaded — your landlord's PAN and your salary figures stay in this tab.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a rent receipt enough, or do I need a rent agreement too?

Receipts are the basic requirement. Many employers also ask for the rent agreement, and some ask for bank transfer proof, particularly for higher rents. Keeping all three is the safest approach.

When is the landlord's PAN mandatory?

When annual rent exceeds ₹1 lakh, which is about ₹8,334 a month. Without it the HRA exemption is usually disallowed. If the landlord genuinely has no PAN, a signed declaration to that effect is the accepted substitute.

Do I need a revenue stamp on the receipt?

Conventionally a ₹1 revenue stamp is affixed on cash receipts above ₹5,000, with the landlord signing across it. Payments made by bank transfer or UPI are usually accepted without a stamp since the transfer itself is the evidence. The PDF leaves space either way.

Can I claim HRA for rent paid to my parents?

Yes, if the arrangement is genuine. The parent must actually own the property and must declare the rent as income in their own return. Keep receipts and transfer records, because this arrangement attracts scrutiny.

Is HRA available under the new tax regime?

No. The HRA exemption applies under the old regime only. If you have opted for the new regime, rent receipts do not reduce your tax, and the comparison between regimes is worth running before you choose.

Can I claim without any receipts at all?

If annual rent is under ₹36,000 — ₹3,000 a month — employers commonly accept the claim without receipts. Above that, receipts are expected.

Are my details stored anywhere?

No. The PDF is built in your browser. Your landlord's PAN, your address and your salary figures are not transmitted anywhere, which is also why the tool works offline.
