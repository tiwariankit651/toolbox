🔐 Why This Redaction Is Actually Safe

When you export from this tool, the visible canvas — with your blur, pixelation or fill baked in — is re-encoded into a brand new image file. The original pixels under a redacted region no longer exist in the output, and because the file is regenerated from scratch, EXIF/GPS/camera metadata is dropped too.

Contrast that with unsafe "redaction" you may have seen:

  • Black box over text in a PDF: the rectangle is just another layer drawn on top. The real text is still in the document and can be selected, copied, or extracted with a script.
  • Semi-transparent overlay: if any of the original shows through, image-processing can amplify contrast and reconstruct what was underneath.
  • Light blur / small mosaic on text: weak blur can sometimes be partially reversed. Use a strong blur radius or a large pixel block — or better, a solid fill — for anything critical like account or card numbers.

Bottom line: because this tool destroys the pixels and re-encodes the file entirely in your browser, there is no hidden layer to recover. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
