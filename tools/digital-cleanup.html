About This Tool

Protect your privacy before sharing images online. This tool lets you blur, black out, or pixelate sensitive areas in screenshots and photos — like bank account numbers, phone numbers, addresses, or faces. It also strips all EXIF metadata (GPS location, camera info, date) from the image. Everything runs in your browser — your images never leave your device. Perfect for sharing screenshots on social media, sending documents, or posting product photos.

What You Can Do

  • Blur — Gaussian blur on selected area (adjustable intensity)
  • Black Out — Completely cover area with black
  • Pixelate — Mosaic effect on selected area
  • Remove Metadata — Strip GPS, camera, date, software info

Common Use Cases

  • Hide bank account/card numbers in screenshots
  • Blur faces in photos before posting online
  • Remove phone numbers and addresses
  • Strip location data from photos
  • Clean screenshots for bug reports
