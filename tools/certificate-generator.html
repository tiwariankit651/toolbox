About Certificate Generator

The Certificate Generator is a free, professional-grade online tool that enables anyone to create beautiful, print-ready certificates in seconds. Whether you're a teacher recognizing student achievements, a company acknowledging employee milestones, an event organizer issuing participation certificates, or a training institute providing completion credentials, this tool delivers polished results without the need for expensive design software or graphic design skills.

Frequently Asked Questions

What templates are available?

Four templates: Certificate of Achievement (for accomplishments), Certificate of Completion (for courses/programs), Certificate of Appreciation (for recognition), and Certificate of Participation (for events/workshops).

Can I add my own signature?

Yes! Use the signature upload field to add a PNG or JPG image of your signature. It will appear on the certificate above the issuer name.

What color themes can I choose?

Three elegant themes: Gold (classic and prestigious), Blue (professional and corporate), and Green (fresh and academic). Each theme affects borders, accents, and decorative elements.

What resolution is the downloaded certificate?

The certificate is generated at 1120×800 pixels, suitable for printing at high quality or sharing digitally.

Can I print the certificate directly?

Yes, click the "Print" button to open your browser's print dialog with the certificate ready to print on standard paper sizes.

Is there a limit on how many certificates I can create?

No limits at all. Generate as many certificates as you need, completely free.

Do I need to install any fonts?

No, the elegant serif fonts (Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, Great Vibes) are loaded automatically from Google Fonts.
