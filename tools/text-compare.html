🔍 Text Compare & Similarity Checker

Paste two texts to compare. Shows similarity percentage and highlights matching sentences.

About This Tool

Compare two texts side by side and check similarity percentage. Highlights matching sentences between documents in green and unique content in red. Also includes a line-by-line diff mode that shows exactly which lines were added, removed, or unchanged — similar to GitHub diff view. Useful as a basic plagiarism checker for students, content writers checking for duplicate content, and developers comparing code versions. Everything runs in your browser — your text is never uploaded.
