FAQ

How much can images be compressed?

Typically 50-90% size reduction depending on the quality setting. A quality of 70% gives great results for most images.

Does it reduce image quality?

Slightly, but at 70-80% quality the difference is invisible to the human eye. Enable "Enhance Quality" to sharpen before compressing.

What formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF — any image your browser can display.

How to compress for SSC/UPSC/IBPS?

Click the preset button for your exam. It auto-sets the correct dimensions and target file size as per official requirements.

What does Enhance Quality do?

It applies sharpening and slight contrast boost to make blurry/low-quality photos look clearer before compressing. Great for old scanned photos.

How to Use This Tool

Ever had your SSC form reject your photo because it was too big? Or that UPSC application saying "file size must be under 20KB"? Frustrating, right? Here's how to fix it in literally 5 seconds:
