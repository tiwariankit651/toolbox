How to Use This Tool

Professional email likhna nahi aata? Koi baat nahi — template select karo, details bharo, email ready. HR follow-up ho ya resignation, sab milega yahan.

  1. Template choose karo — Leave Application, Resignation, Job Inquiry, Thank You, jo bhi chahiye
  2. Apna naam, recipient ka naam, company aur position bharo
  3. Tone select karo — Formal, Friendly, ya Urgent
  4. Preview mein email dikh jayegi — check karo, copy karo, Gmail mein paste karo. Done!

Common Use Cases

  • Drafting professional emails to clients or managers quickly
  • Writing polite follow-up emails after meetings or interviews
  • Composing formal request emails for information or approvals
  • Creating thank-you emails after job interviews or collaborations
  • Writing difficult emails like complaints or resignation letters

About This Tool

Professional email templates for 11 common workplace situations. Select a template (HR follow-up, leave application, resignation, job inquiry, thank you, self introduction, apology, meeting request, recommendation request, complaint, internship application), fill in your details, and get a formatted email ready to send. Choose tone: formal, friendly, or urgent. Copy and paste into your email client. Perfect for students, job seekers, and professionals who need to write professional emails quickly.
