✅ Email Best-Practices Checklist

  • Clear, specific subject line — the reader should know the topic at a glance.
  • One main ask per email — don't bury multiple requests.
  • Polite greeting and a courteous close.
  • Proofread for spelling, grammar and the recipient's name.
  • Mention attachments — and actually attach them before sending.
  • Keep it concise; use short paragraphs.
