📱 Instagram Caption Spacer

Type your caption with line breaks. Copy the formatted text and paste in Instagram.

About This Tool

Instagram removes extra line breaks from captions. This tool adds invisible Unicode characters between lines so your formatting stays clean. Works for Instagram posts, bios, and comments. Also shows character count (Instagram limit: 2200 for posts, 150 for bio).
