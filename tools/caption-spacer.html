📱 Instagram Caption Spacer

Type your caption with line breaks. Copy the formatted text and paste in Instagram.

About This Tool

Format Instagram captions with clean line breaks that actually work. Instagram normally removes extra blank lines from captions, making your carefully formatted text look messy. This tool adds invisible Unicode characters between lines that Instagram cannot remove, preserving your formatting. Also shows character count (Instagram limit: 2200 for posts, 150 for bio). Perfect for influencers, businesses, and anyone who wants professional-looking Instagram captions with proper spacing.
