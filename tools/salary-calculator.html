Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool really free?

Yes, completely free with no hidden charges, no signup required, and no usage limits.

How accurate is this calculator?

This provides an estimate based on standard salary structures. Actual in-hand may vary based on company policies, additional deductions, and specific tax-saving investments.

What is the new tax regime 2026?

The new regime offers lower tax rates with fewer deductions. Slabs: 0-4L (0%), 4-8L (5%), 8-12L (10%), 12-16L (15%), 16-20L (20%), 20-24L (25%), 24L+ (30%). Standard deduction of ₹75,000 applies.

Is my data safe?

How to Use This Tool

Got a job offer saying "12 LPA CTC" and wondering how much actually lands in your bank account every month? Yeah, CTC and in-hand salary are very different things. PF, tax, professional tax — sab kat ke aata hai. Let me help you figure out the real number:

  1. Enter your annual CTC — the number from your offer letter. Example: 1200000 for 12 LPA.
  2. Adjust percentages — Basic is usually 40% of CTC, HRA is 50% of Basic, PF is 12%. Change if your offer letter says different.
  3. Click Calculate — instantly see your monthly in-hand salary under both Old and New tax regime.
  4. Compare regimes — the tool shows which one saves you more tax. Most people below 12 LPA benefit from New Regime.
  5. See full breakdown — Basic, HRA, Special Allowance, PF, Professional Tax, Income Tax — everything itemized.

This is the first thing I check when I get a new offer. The difference between CTC and in-hand can be 20-30% — better to know upfront than be surprised on salary day!

About This Tool

The Salary Calculator converts your annual CTC (Cost to Company) into actual in-hand (take-home) salary. It breaks down your salary into Basic, HRA, Special Allowance, PF (employee and employer contributions), Professional Tax, and Income Tax. Tax is calculated under both the Old Regime and New Regime (2026 slabs) so you can compare and choose the better option. Customize Basic %, HRA %, and PF % to match your offer letter. All calculations are instant and happen entirely in your browser.
