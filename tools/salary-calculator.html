Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool really free?

Yes, completely free with no hidden charges, no signup required, and no usage limits.

How accurate is this calculator?

This provides an estimate based on standard salary structures. Actual in-hand may vary based on company policies, additional deductions, and specific tax-saving investments.

What is the new tax regime 2026?

The new regime offers lower tax rates with fewer deductions. Slabs: 0-4L (0%), 4-8L (5%), 8-12L (10%), 12-16L (15%), 16-20L (20%), 20-24L (25%), 24L+ (30%). Standard deduction of ₹75,000 applies.

Is my data safe?

About This Tool

The Salary Calculator converts your annual CTC (Cost to Company) into actual in-hand (take-home) salary. It breaks down your salary into Basic, HRA, Special Allowance, PF (employee and employer contributions), Professional Tax, and Income Tax. Tax is calculated under both the Old Regime and New Regime (2026 slabs) so you can compare and choose the better option. Customize Basic %, HRA %, and PF % to match your offer letter. All calculations are instant and happen entirely in your browser.
