    FAQ

    How to merge PDF files?

    Click the upload area, select multiple PDF files, then click Merge. Your combined PDF will download automatically.

    Is it safe?

    Yes. Files are processed entirely in your browser and never uploaded to any server.

    Is there a limit?

    No limit on number of files. Works best with files under 100MB total.

    How to Use This Tool

    Picture this — you have your resume in one PDF, cover letter in another, and certificates in three more. The company wants ONE file. Sound familiar? Here's how you combine them in 10 seconds:

    1. Add your PDFs — drag and drop multiple files, or click and select them all at once.
    2. Arrange the order — use the up/down arrows to put them in the right sequence.
    3. Remove any you don't need — accidentally added the wrong file? Just click ✕.
    4. Hit "Merge All PDFs" — boom, one combined file downloads instantly.

    No file limit, no watermarks, no "sign up to download" nonsense. Your files never leave your browser — they're merged right here on your device. I use this literally every time I apply for jobs or submit college documents.
