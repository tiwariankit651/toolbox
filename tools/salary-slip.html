How to Use This Tool

  1. Company and month first. These appear on every slip, so with bulk generation you enter them once for the whole team.
  2. Earnings, then deductions. Leave PF and ESI on automatic and they are worked out from the figures you enter — PF at 12% of basic with the ₹15,000 wage ceiling, ESI at 0.75% only while gross stays within ₹21,000.
  3. Watch the live totals. Gross, deductions and net update as you type, so a wrong figure is obvious before you generate anything.
  4. Bulk from CSV when you run payroll for a team. Paste the rows or drop a file; you get one PDF with a page per employee, or a ZIP of separate files to email individually.
  5. Check the preview — it is the same layout as the PDF, including the amount written in words.

About This Tool

A salary slip is the document an employee needs for a loan, a visa, a rental agreement or simply as a record of what was paid and what was deducted. Many small businesses still build them by hand in Word, which is slow and easy to get wrong. This generator produces a standard payslip layout — company header, employee details, earnings and deductions in two columns, net pay, and the amount written out in words, which is what makes a slip acceptable as a document rather than a printout. PF is calculated at 12% of basic with the statutory ₹15,000 wage ceiling applied, and ESI at 0.75% of gross while the employee remains within the ₹21,000 eligibility limit, with both shown as editable so you can override them for your own arrangement. Bulk mode takes the employee list you already keep in a spreadsheet and produces every slip in one pass. Names in Hindi, Marathi, Tamil and other Indian scripts print correctly because the PDF embeds a Unicode font. Nothing is uploaded, which matters a great deal when the data is everyone's salary.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is PF calculated here?

12% of basic salary, with basic capped at ₹15,000 for the calculation — so the employee contribution stops at ₹1,800 a month. That is the statutory ceiling. If your organisation contributes on full basic without the cap, untick the automatic option and enter the figure yourself.

When does ESI apply?

ESI applies while gross monthly wages are ₹21,000 or less, at 0.75% from the employee. Above that threshold the employee stops being covered, and the tool stops adding it. The employer's 3.25% share is not an employee deduction, so it does not appear on the slip.

What should professional tax be?

It varies by state and by salary slab, and some states do not levy it at all. ₹200 a month is common in Maharashtra and Karnataka for mid salaries. Check your state's slab rather than assuming.

Is a slip from this tool legally valid?

A payslip's standing comes from being issued by the employer with correct figures, not from the software that formatted it. Fill it accurately, and have it signed or stamped if your organisation does that. This tool does the layout and the arithmetic; the accuracy of the figures is yours.

Can I generate slips for past months?

Yes — set the month field to whichever month you need. Nothing is tied to the current date.

Is the salary data stored anywhere?

Company name and your preferences are remembered for convenience. Employee names and salary figures are not persisted, and nothing is transmitted — the PDF is built in your browser, which is why it also works offline.
