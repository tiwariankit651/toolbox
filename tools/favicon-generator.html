About This Tool
Create favicons for your website from text, initials, or emoji. Customize background color, text color, font size, and shape (square, rounded corners, or circle). Preview in multiple sizes and download in 32x32, 64x64, 128x128, or 256x256 pixels. Perfect for website owners, bloggers, and developers who need a quick favicon without design software.
