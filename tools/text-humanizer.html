About This Tool

The Text Humanizer is a rule-based writing refinement tool. It takes stiff, formal, or robotic-sounding text and applies transparent transformations to help it read more naturally: it replaces overused filler phrases (like "delve into", "it is important to note", "in conclusion", "furthermore", "moreover") with simpler alternatives, varies repeated sentence openings, breaks up very long sentences, converts clearly-detectable passive voice to active, reduces heavy nominalisations (utilisation → use), trims redundant intensifiers, and can add contractions for a less formal tone. Every change is shown to you individually so you can accept or reject it. It also reports readability and average sentence-length variation before and after. Honesty note: this is a mechanical rule engine, not AI, and it makes no claim about AI-detection tools — it will not and cannot help you disguise AI-generated content, and doing so in graded academic work may breach your institution's rules.
