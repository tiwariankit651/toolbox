Paste AI-generated text and make it sound more natural and human-written.

Humanized Text

About This Tool

Make AI-generated text sound more natural and human-written. The tool applies 56 rule-based transformations: adds contractions (do not→don't), replaces formal/corporate phrases with casual alternatives (utilize→use, leverage→use, commence→start, methodology→method, robust→strong, cutting-edge→modern), varies sentence length, and adds transition words. Choose tone (Casual, Professional, Academic) and level (Light, Medium, Heavy). Shows Flesch-Kincaid readability score and percentage changed. Note: Rule-based, not AI.
