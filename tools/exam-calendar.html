How to Use This Tool

Next exam kab hai? 2026 ke saare dates ek jagah. JEE, NEET, UPSC, Board exams — sab ka schedule yahan. Daily check karo — panic nahi hoga last moment pe.

  1. Apne exams add karo — naam, date, aur subject daalo
