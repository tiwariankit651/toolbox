How to Use This Tool

Roz exercise karna hai but bhool jaate ho? Streak banao yahan. Jab streak dikhega — todne ka mann nahi karega. Consistency is key, bhai.

  1. Habits add karo — "Morning Walk", "DSA Practice", "No Phone After 11 PM"
  2. Roz check karo jo complete kiya — green tick lagega
  3. Streak count dekho — 7 days, 30 days, 100 days! 🔥
  4. Weekly view mein dekho kahan miss kiya — pattern samjho
  5. New habits add karo jab purane wale routine ban jaayein

Common Use Cases

  • Daily coding practice — LeetCode streak maintain karo
  • Roz 8 glass paani peena — health track karo
  • Exam ke time daily revision habit banana
  • Social media limit — "No Instagram after 9 PM" track karo
  • Morning routine build karna — early wake up streak

About This Tool

Build better daily habits with streak tracking and weekly reports. Add your habits (study 2 hours, exercise, read newspaper, drink water, etc.), check them off each day, and watch your streaks grow. The 7-day visual history shows your consistency at a glance. Weekly bar chart report shows completion percentage for each day. See total habits, completed today, and overall completion rate. All data saves automatically in your browser — come back daily to maintain your streaks. Perfect for students building study routines, fitness goals, and self-improvement.
