✅ Habit Tracker

Add habits, check them off daily, build streaks. Auto-saves to your browser.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Step 1: Add habits you want to build or track daily
  2. Step 2: Check off each habit as you complete it throughout the day
  3. Step 3: View your streak count and consistency percentage
  4. Step 4: Review weekly and monthly progress charts
  5. Step 5: Adjust or add new habits as your routine evolves

Common Use Cases

  • Building consistent daily routines like exercise or reading
  • Tracking water intake, sleep, or meditation habits
  • Monitoring study habits during exam preparation periods
  • Breaking bad habits by tracking days without them
  • Maintaining accountability for personal development goals

About This Tool

Build better daily habits with streak tracking and weekly reports. Add your habits (study 2 hours, exercise, read newspaper, drink water, etc.), check them off each day, and watch your streaks grow. The 7-day visual history shows your consistency at a glance. Weekly bar chart report shows completion percentage for each day. See total habits, completed today, and overall completion rate. All data saves automatically in your browser — come back daily to maintain your streaks. Perfect for students building study routines, fitness goals, and self-improvement.

Yes, completely free with no hidden charges, no signup required, and no usage limits. Use it as many times as you want.
