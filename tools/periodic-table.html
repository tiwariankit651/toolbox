About This Tool

An interactive periodic table with 54 elements. Click any element to see its atomic number, symbol, name, atomic mass, category, period, and group. Color-coded by element category: Alkali Metals, Alkaline Earth, Transition Metals, Post-Transition, Metalloids, Nonmetals, Halogens, Noble Gases, Lanthanides, and Actinides. Search elements by name, symbol, or atomic number. Includes a color legend. Perfect for chemistry students studying for board exams, JEE, NEET, and anyone who needs quick element reference.
