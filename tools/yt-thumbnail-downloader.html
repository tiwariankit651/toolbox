FAQ

What YouTube URL formats are supported?

Standard watch URLs (youtube.com/watch?v=), short URLs (youtu.be/), and Shorts URLs (youtube.com/shorts/) are all supported.

Are all thumbnail sizes always available?

Most videos have all sizes. However, maxresdefault (1280×720) may not be available for older or lower-quality videos.

Can I use downloaded thumbnails commercially?

Thumbnails are owned by the video creator. Use them only for personal reference, commentary, or with proper permission.
