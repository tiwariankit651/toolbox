📊 Word Counter & Readability

Paste or type text to get word count, character count, reading time and readability score.

About This Tool

Count words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs in your text. Check reading time and readability score. Essential for students writing essays, bloggers, and content creators.

How to Use

  1. Type or paste your text in the box
  2. Word count, character count, sentences, and lines update in real-time
  3. Check reading time and readability

Why Count Words?

Word counting is essential for students writing essays with word limits, bloggers optimizing content length, social media managers checking character limits (Twitter: 280, Instagram: 2200), and professionals writing reports. This tool also shows reading time estimate and readability score to help you write better content.
