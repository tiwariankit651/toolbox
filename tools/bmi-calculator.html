About This Tool

Calculate your Body Mass Index (BMI) to check if your weight is healthy for your height. BMI is widely used by doctors and health professionals as a quick screening tool. Supports both metric (kg/cm) and imperial (lbs/inches) units. Get instant results with category classification and healthy weight range.

BMI Categories

  • Below 18.5 — Underweight
  • 18.5 – 24.9 — Normal weight
  • 25.0 – 29.9 — Overweight
  • 30.0 and above — Obese
