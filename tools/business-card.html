The Business Card Generator is a powerful, completely free online tool that lets you design professional business cards right in your browser. Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool really free?

Yes, completely free with no hidden charges, no signup required, and no usage limits. Design and download as many business cards as you want.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. All processing happens in your browser using JavaScript. Your contact information and logo are never uploaded to any server. We physically cannot access your data.

What size are the business cards?

The cards follow the standard 3.5 × 2 inch (89 × 51 mm) ratio, which is the most widely used business card size globally and accepted by all professional printers.

Can I upload my company logo?

Yes! Click the Upload Logo option and select any image file (PNG, JPG, SVG). The logo will appear on your card and is included in the downloaded PNG.

What format is the download?

Cards are downloaded as high-resolution PNG images. You can download the front and back separately, making them ready for professional printing services.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, this tool works on all devices — desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile phone. The interface adapts to your screen size for comfortable editing.
