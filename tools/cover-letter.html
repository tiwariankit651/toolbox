✅ Cover Letter Checklist — Avoid These Mistakes

  • Address a real person where possible — avoid generic "To Whom It May Concern".
  • Don't repeat your resume word-for-word; tell the story behind your achievements.
  • Keep it to one page — a cover letter should rarely exceed 400 words.
  • Open with a hook, not "I am writing to apply for…" alone.
  • Show you researched the company — reference their work, product, or mission.
  • Quantify achievements (numbers, %, impact) using the STAR pattern.
  • Match your skills to the job description's key requirements.
  • Proofread for typos and the correct company/role name.
  • End with a clear call to action and a professional sign-off.
  • Always tailor the letter for each application — never send the same letter to every employer.
